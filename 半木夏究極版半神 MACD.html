1. Executive Summary

1. 核心總結 (Executive Summary)

The speaker argues that traders rarely lose because their strategy is bad; they lose because they enter at the wrong time, when no liquidity is available to fuel a move. Liquidity means resting orders (pending orders and stop losses), which institutions need in order to fill very large positions. Price therefore tends to sweep liquidity around support and resistance, swing highs and lows, equal highs and lows, and consolidations before moving in its real direction. Knowing “where” is not enough; you also need “when”: the London Open and the London–New York overlap attract the most institutional participation, while the Asian session is mostly low-volatility consolidation. The speaker merges both ideas into a five-step “Golden Bullet Strategy”. His conclusion: liquidity is the fuel, timing is the ignition, and the real difficulty is discipline and patience.

講者認為，交易者虧損並非策略太差，而是在沒有流動性、時機錯誤時進場。流動性即市場中的掛單（待成交單與止損單），是機構建立大額部位所需的「燃料」，因此價格常先掃蕩支撐壓力、波段高低點等位置的流動性，再朝真正方向移動。但知道「哪裡」還不夠，還要掌握「何時」：倫敦開盤與倫敦／紐約重疊時段機構參與度最高，亞洲時段則多為低波動盤整。講者將兩者整合為五步驟的「黃金子彈策略」：判斷結構、找出流動性、等待活躍時段、等掃蕩後的確認訊號、以約 1% 風險執行。結論是：流動性是燃料、時機是點火，兩者結合才會產生價格擴張，而真正的難關是紀律與等待。

2. Key Concepts & Definitions

2. 知識地圖與核心概念 (Key Concepts & Definitions)

[00:01:57] Liquidity: Resting orders in the market, made of pending orders and stop losses. It is the fuel institutions need to execute large positions and push price in their desired direction.

流動性 (Liquidity)：市場中的掛單，由待成交單與止損單組成；是機構執行大額部位、推動價格朝預期方向移動所需的「燃料」。

[00:01:16] Smart Money / Institutions: Large players such as banks and funds. Their orders are so big that no single counterparty can match them at once, so they draw on liquidity from retail and other traders.

主力機構 (Smart Money)：銀行、基金等大型參與者；單量龐大，無法在同一時間找到單一對手盤，因此必須汲取散戶與其他交易者的流動性。

[00:02:57] Resting Orders (Stop Loss, Pending Orders): Stop losses close losing trades; pending orders such as buy stops and sell stops enter the market when price reaches a level (for example to trade a breakout).

掛單（止損單、待成交單）：止損單用於平掉虧損部位；待成交單（如 buy stop、sell stop）在價格到達特定位置時進場，常用於交易突破。

[00:02:10] Liquidity Sweep: Price runs through an area full of resting orders to collect them, then moves (often reverses) in the institutions’ intended direction.

流動性掃蕩 (Liquidity Sweep)：價格衝過掛單密集區以取得流動性，隨後朝機構預期方向發展（常見為反轉）。

[00:02:28] Support & Resistance Liquidity: Retail traders sell at resistance with stops above it and buy at support with stops below it, so liquidity piles up just beyond these levels.

支撐壓力位流動性：散戶習慣在壓力位做空並把止損放在上方、在支撐位做多並把止損放在下方，因此流動性堆積在這些位置之外。

[00:03:43] Swing High / Swing Low: Buy stops sit above a swing high; sell stops and the stop losses of continuation buyers sit below a swing low.

波段高點／波段低點：波段高點上方有 buy stop；波段低點下方有 sell stop 以及順勢買入者的止損單。

[00:04:29] Equal Highs / Equal Lows: Two or more highs (or lows) at nearly the same level, creating a visible pool of liquidity beyond them.

等高點／等低點：兩個以上高點（或低點）位於相近價位，其外側形成明顯的流動性池。

[00:04:38] Consolidation: A sideways range with liquidity above and below it. Price can use the liquidity on one side to fuel a pullback or the next leg.

盤整 (Consolidation)：價格橫向整理的區間，上下兩側都有流動性；價格可利用其中一側的流動性推動回檔或下一段走勢。

[00:20:26] Market Structure / Break of Structure (BOS): The sequence of highs and lows. A bearish BOS creates a lower high and a fresh lower low; a bullish BOS is the opposite and shows the trend direction.

市場結構／結構突破 (BOS)：由高低點序列構成的價格結構。空頭 BOS 會形成更低的高點與新的更低低點；多頭則相反，用來判斷趨勢方向。

[00:05:23] Bias: Your trade idea: where to buy, where to sell, and roughly when to enter. It comes from understanding market structure and liquidity.

偏向／交易觀點 (Bias)：你的交易想法：在哪裡買、在哪裡賣，以及大約何時進場；來自對市場結構與流動性的理解。

[00:07:26] Timing: Not a convenient time of day for you, but the windows when institutions are actually participating. Outside them, price does not move much.

時機 (Timing)：不是挑你方便的時間，而是找出機構實際參與的時窗；在這些時窗之外價格通常波動不大。

[00:09:02] Trading Sessions: Asian session (before London), London session 3–11 AM EST, New York session opening 8 AM EST. The London Open is 3–6 AM EST.

交易時段：亞洲時段（早於倫敦）、倫敦時段美東 3–11 點、紐約時段美東 8 點開盤；其中倫敦開盤為美東 3–6 點。

[00:09:59] London–New York Overlap: Roughly 8–11 AM EST, when UK and US traders are active together; the highest volume and the most volatile period of the day.

倫敦／紐約重疊時段：約美東 8–11 點，英美交易者同時在場，是一天中成交量最高、波動最劇烈的時段。

[00:12:30] Kill Zones: A popular nickname for the session windows. There is no magic: they exist because liquidity and institutional participation overlap.

殺戮區間 (Kill Zones)：交易者對上述時窗的稱呼；並無神奇之處，只是因為流動性與機構參與在此重疊。

[00:22:19] Supply / Demand Zone: Areas where sell (supply) or buy (demand) orders are likely clustered. Price gravitates toward them; the zone that caused a structural break is especially important.

供給／需求區：賣單（供給）或買單（需求）可能聚集的區域，價格會自然被吸引過去；造成結構突破的那個區域尤其重要。

[00:23:53] POI (Point of Interest): Pre-marked levels (supply or demand zones, liquidity levels) where you expect a reaction. You wait to see which one is respected.

興趣點 (POI)：事先標記、預期會出現反應的位置（供需區、流動性位置）；你只需等待觀察哪一個被市場尊重。

[00:14:16] Accumulation / Distribution: In the Asian session price accumulates and positions rather than expands. After a sweep, a sharp reversal signals smart money distributing.

累積／派發：亞洲時段價格多在累積與佈局而非擴張；掃蕩後的急速反轉則被視為主力在派發。

[00:25:18] V-Shaped Reaction: A sharp reversal right after liquidity is swept, confirming the sweep and that smart money manipulated price.

V 型反應：流動性被掃蕩後立即出現的急速反轉，用來確認掃蕩成立。

[00:25:41] Internal Structure Break: During a pullback, the micro structure (higher highs/lows) breaks back in the trend direction, confirming that the pullback is over.

內部結構突破：回檔期間的小級別結構（更高高點／低點）反向突破，確認回檔結束、原趨勢恢復。

[00:20:00] Golden Bullet Strategy: The speaker’s five-step plan that combines liquidity and timing into an execution plan.

黃金子彈策略：講者提出的五步驟執行計畫，將流動性與時機整合為具體的交易流程。

[00:35:37] Risk per Trade & Reward-to-Risk (R): Risk about 1% of account equity per trade, put the stop beyond the sweep candle or demand zone, and aim for a target such as 3R.

單筆風險與風險報酬比 (R)：單筆風險約為帳戶淨值的 1%，止損放在掃蕩 K 線或需求區之外，並以 3R 等目標設定報酬。

[00:29:53] Drawdown: The decline from a peak in account equity. Maximum-loss and profit-target rules help limit it and avoid giving profits back.

資金回撤 (Drawdown)：帳戶淨值自高點回落的幅度；最大虧損與獲利目標規則可限制回撤並避免把獲利還給市場。

[00:37:54] Trade Idea – Location – Timing: The speaker’s three foundations of profitability: what to trade (idea), where to trade (POI), and when to trade (session).

交易想法—位置—時機：講者提出的三大獲利基礎：交易什麼（想法）、在哪裡交易（POI 位置）、何時交易（時段）。

[00:27:52] Discipline: Described as the single most important trait for consistent profitability; in practice, the ability to wait and follow your rules.

紀律 (Discipline)：被視為穩定獲利最重要的特質；實務上就是耐心等待並遵守規則的能力。

4. Mental Models & Logic

4. 底層邏輯與思維模型 (Mental Models & Logic)

[00:01:16] Counterparty Constraint Model

對手盤限制模型

•         Why: a huge order needs someone on the other side, and such a counterparty rarely exists at the exact moment. How: ask where other traders’ stops and pending orders sit, because institutions must travel there to fill.

為什麼：龐大訂單需要對手盤，而這種對手盤很少在剛好的時間出現。怎麼做：問自己「別人的止損與掛單在哪裡」，因為機構必須到那裡才能成交。

[00:05:03] Perspective Inversion

視角翻轉

•         Why: retail logic (enter at levels, stop beyond them) creates predictable pools. How: look at the chart from the institution’s seat. If you cannot spot liquidity, you are the liquidity.

為什麼：散戶邏輯（在關鍵位進場、止損放其外）會製造可預測的流動性池。怎麼做：站在機構的角度看圖。看不出流動性在哪，你就是別人的流動性。

[00:19:33] Fuel and Ignition (AND Logic)

燃料與點火（且邏輯）

•         Why: each factor is necessary but not sufficient. How: require both liquidity nearby and an active session. Timing without liquidity chops; liquidity without participation can sit untouched for hours.

為什麼：兩個因素都是必要但不充分條件。怎麼做：同時要求「附近有流動性」與「處於活躍時段」。有時機沒流動性會盤整；有流動性沒人參與可能閒置數小時。

[00:06:08] Direction ≠ Profit

方向正確 ≠ 獲利

•         Why: success = strategy + timing; being early is as bad as being wrong. How: after confirming direction, check whether the move is already extended and whether institutions are active.

為什麼：成功 = 策略 + 時機；太早和錯誤一樣糟。怎麼做：確認方向後，再檢查走勢是否已延伸過度、機構是否正在參與。

[00:08:32] Data-Driven Window Selection

以數據選擇時窗

•         Why: trading hours should come from evidence, not lifestyle. How: use the sessions with the most opportunities (London Open, overlap) and verify with your own trade records.

為什麼：交易時間應基於證據而非作息。怎麼做：選擇機會最多的時段（倫敦開盤、重疊時段），並用自己的交易紀錄驗證。

[00:24:45] Conditional Scenario Planning

條件式情境規劃

•         Why: there is no crystal ball. How: pre-mark several POIs, define an invalidation level (the last lower high), act only after confirmation, and flip bias if structure breaks.

為什麼：沒有人有水晶球。怎麼做：事先標記多個 POI、設定失效位置（最近的更低高點）、確認後才行動，結構被破壞就翻轉偏向。

[00:17:08] Selectivity & Patience as Edge

選擇性與耐心即優勢

•         Why: professionals trade only when the market can pay out. How: wait for the A+ setup that checks every box and say no to sub-par trades.

為什麼：專業者只在市場有能力給付報酬時交易。怎麼做：等待符合所有條件的 A+ 型態，對次級機會說不。

Module 2: What Liquidity Is and Why Institutions Need It  [00:01:16–00:02:28]

模組 2：流動性的定義與機構的需求  [00:01:16–00:02:28]

Concept Explanation

【概念解釋】

•         [00:01:16] Example: an institution wants to sell about one billion dollars of short orders and needs buyers on the other side, but no single counterparty is likely to match the order at that exact moment.

舉例：機構想建立約十億美元的空單，需要有人在另一端買進，但不太可能剛好有單一對手盤在同一時間接下。

•         [00:01:42] So they take available liquidity from the market, from retail and other traders.

因此他們必須從市場、散戶與其他交易者手中取得可用的流動性。

•         [00:02:10] To move price significantly up or down, they must first sweep some liquidity.

要讓價格大幅上漲或下跌，必須先掃蕩一部分流動性。

Key Steps / Workflow

【關鍵步驟／操作流程】

1.        [00:01:16] Institution wants a large position.

機構想建立大額部位。

2.        [00:01:35] No counterparty is available at the desired moment.

在想要的時間點沒有足夠對手盤。

3.        [00:02:10] Price travels to areas full of resting orders and sweeps them.

價格移動到掛單密集處並將其掃蕩。

4.        [00:03:31] The position is filled and price moves in the intended direction.

部位得以成交，價格朝預期方向移動。

Key Formulas / Terms

【重要公式／專有名詞】

[00:02:57] Liquidity = Resting Orders = Pending Orders + Stop Losses

流動性 = 掛單 = 待成交單 + 止損單

Common Pitfalls

【常見誤區／注意事項】

•         [00:18:18] Liquidity existing nearby does not mean price will move right away; it can sit untouched for hours until institutions step in.

附近有流動性不代表價格馬上會動；在機構出手之前，它可能閒置數小時。

Module 3: Where Liquidity Hides  [00:02:17–00:05:23]

模組 3：流動性藏在哪裡  [00:02:17–00:05:23]

Concept Explanation

【概念解釋】

•         [00:02:23] Liquidity is not mysterious; it sits in four recurring places on the chart.

流動性並不神祕，它固定出現在圖表上的四種位置。

Key Steps / Workflow

【關鍵步驟／操作流程】

1.        [00:02:28] Support and resistance: stops of traders who sell at resistance or buy at support, plus sell stops placed under support for breakout trades.

支撐與壓力：在壓力位做空、支撐位做多者的止損單，以及為交易突破而掛在支撐下方的 sell stop。

2.        [00:03:43] Swing highs and lows: buy stops above a swing high; sell stops and continuation buyers’ stop losses below a swing low.

波段高低點：波段高點上方有 buy stop；波段低點下方有 sell stop 與順勢買入者的止損單。

3.        [00:04:29] Equal highs and equal lows: a visible pool beyond both highs or both lows.

等高點與等低點：兩個高點（或低點）外側形成明顯的流動性池。

4.        [00:04:38] Consolidation: liquidity above and below the range.

Module 6: Liquidity + Timing and the Golden Bullet Strategy (Bearish Example)  [00:18:11–00:27:00]

模組 6：流動性 + 時機與黃金子彈策略（空頭範例）  [00:18:11–00:27:00]

Concept Explanation

【概念解釋】

•         [00:18:11] Professionals do not trade all the time; they trade only when the market is capable of paying out.

專業者不會隨時交易，只在市場有能力給付報酬時才交易。

•         [00:18:58] Perfect timing with no resting orders nearby leaves price chopping around or barely moving. You need both.

時機再好，若附近沒有掛單，價格只會盤整或幾乎不動；兩者缺一不可。

[00:19:33] Liquidity + Timing = Expansion  (Liquidity = fuel, Timing = ignition)

流動性 + 時機 = 擴張（流動性＝燃料，時機＝點火）

Key Steps / Workflow

【關鍵步驟／操作流程】

1.        [00:20:26] Identify trend and structure. A bearish break of structure creates a lower high and a new lower low; you trade within that swing high and swing low.

判斷趨勢與結構。空頭結構突破會形成更低的高點與新的更低低點，你在該波段高低點範圍內交易。

2.        [00:20:49] Identify where liquidity sits. To keep falling, price needs a lower high, so it must first sweep liquidity above swing highs. If price floats in the middle with no sweep, do not enter.

找出流動性位置。要繼續下跌，價格需形成更低的高點，因此會先掃蕩波段高點上方的流動性；若價格在中間且尚未掃蕩，就不進場。

3.        [00:21:56] Wait for the active session (London Open, New York Open or the overlap). Bonus tip: mark the supply zones that caused the structural break, especially the one next to the liquidity.

等待活躍時段（倫敦開盤、紐約開盤或重疊時段）。加碼技巧：標記造成結構突破的供給區，尤其是緊鄰流動性的那一個。

4.        [00:24:08] Let price sweep and wait for confirmation: a V-shaped reaction, then an internal structure break (the micro bullish structure breaks its recent higher low to the downside).

讓價格掃蕩並等待確認：出現 V 型反應，接著內部結構突破（小級別多頭結構跌破最近的更高低點）。

5.        [00:26:20] Execute with defined risk, typically 1% of account equity per trade.

以明確風險執行，通常單筆風險為帳戶淨值的 1%。

Key Formulas / Terms

【重要公式／專有名詞】

•         [00:23:02] The last lower high is the invalidation point: if price breaks above it, structure turns bullish and you look for longs instead.

最近的更低高點是失效位置：若價格突破，結構轉為多頭，改找做多機會。

•         [00:23:45] Until London opens, do nothing; wait for price to reach one of the marked POIs and see which is respected.

倫敦開盤前不動作；等價格到達標記的 POI，觀察哪一個被尊重。

Common Pitfalls

【常見誤區／注意事項】

•         [00:24:33] Price may sweep a higher liquidity level first and pull back into the next supply zone, so reversals can occur at any POI.

價格可能先掃更高的流動性，再回到下一個供給區，因此反轉可能發生在任何 POI。

•         [00:24:45] There is no crystal ball: wait for confluences to validate the idea before executing.

沒有水晶球：要等多重條件驗證想法後才執行。
