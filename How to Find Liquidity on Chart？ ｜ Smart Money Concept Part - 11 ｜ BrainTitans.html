Executive Summary

一、核心總結

The lesson explains liquidity through order matching and inferred clusters of stop orders. It maps potential buy-side liquidity above highs and sell-side liquidity below lows, then applies this framework to ranges, declining trends, and a Nifty 50 five-minute example. Its practical message is to locate likely order clusters before trading and distinguish sustained breakouts from brief sweeps. External liquidity sits beyond a selected range; internal liquidity lies within it. These are the speaker’s chart-reading heuristics, not verified order-book measurements or guaranteed forecasts. Learners should practice annotation, record competing scenarios, and test outcomes rather than chase an apparent breakout. [file:1]

本片以買賣撮合及停損委託的推估分布，說明如何辨識圖表上的流動性。講者從等高、等低與下降趨勢切入，解釋高點上方的買方流動性、低點下方的賣方流動性，以及空頭回補可能造成的推升；再以 Nifty 50 五分鐘圖區分區間內外的流動性。核心做法是先標記潛在訂單聚集區，再觀察價格突破後能否維持，避免將短暫掃單誤認為趨勢延續。這些內容屬圖表推論，並非委託簿實測或獲利保證；應搭配情境紀錄與回放練習驗證。[file:1]

Mental Models and Logic

四、底層邏輯與思維模型

1. Counterparty constraint: ask what executable opposite-side quantity is available, rather than treating a submitted order as a completed trade. [file:1]

1. 對手方約束：先問有多少可成交的反向數量，不把送出委託等同完成交易。 [file:1]

2. Position reversal: infer the exit direction from the original position; short exits buy, long exits sell. [file:1]

2. 部位反向：由原持倉推導出場方向；空單買回，多單賣出。 [file:1]

3. Incentive-based inference: imagine where a trader entering at a visible level might place protection; treat the result as a hypothesis. [file:1]

3. 誘因推估：設想在明顯價位進場者可能把保護單放在哪裡；結論只是待驗證假說。 [file:1]

4. State dependence: declining prices may clear earlier lower stops while leaving higher short stops; the speaker infers an asymmetry, not a measured inventory. [file:1]

4. 狀態依賴：下跌可能觸發下方先前停損、留下上方空單停損；這是講者推估的不對稱，不是實測存量。 [file:1]

5. Competing scenarios: a crossed boundary can lead either to continuation or rejection; location alone does not settle direction. [file:1]

5. 競爭情境：越過邊界後可能延續，也可能遭拒反轉；僅知道位置不足以決定方向。 [file:1]

6. Reference-frame model: internal and external require a specified range and timeframe before classification. [file:1]

6. 參照框架：先固定區間與時間週期，才有內部、外部的分類意義。 [file:1]
