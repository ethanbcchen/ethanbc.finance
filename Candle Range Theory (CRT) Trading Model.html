1. Executive Summary
1. 核心總結 (Executive Summary)

This tutorial demystifies the Candle Range Theory (CRT) trading model and integrates it with Smart Money Concepts (SMC) to construct a high-probability, trend-following trading strategy. The core premise of CRT is that every single candlestick represents an independent trading range whose high (Candle Range High, CRH) and low (Candle Range Low, CRL) act as critical liquidity turning points on lower timeframes. Standard CRT operates via a three-candle sequence: the first candle defines the range, the second sweeps liquidity beyond one extreme and closes back inside, and the third provides the entry toward the opposite liquidity pool. While traditional CRT often targets counter-trend reversals around the New York session open, the video introduces an optimized trend-continuation framework. By waiting for pullbacks into Discount or Premium zones within a clear trend, applying CRT solely to counter-trend candles, zooming into lower timeframes (such as 5-minute or 15-minute charts) to enter on Fair Value Gaps (FVG), and taking partial profits at a 1:1 risk-to-reward ratio, traders can execute objective, risk-controlled trades aligned with institutional order flow.

本教學影片深入簡出地拆解了 K線區間理論（Candle Range Theory, CRT）交易模型，並將其與聰明錢概念（Smart Money Concepts, SMC）結合，構建出一套高勝率的順勢交易策略。CRT 的核心前提在於：每一根獨立 K線都代表一個完整的交易區間，其最高點（K線區間高點 CRH）與最低點（K線區間低點 CRL）在較小時間框架上皆為關鍵的流動性轉折點。標準 CRT 遵循三根 K線的演變邏輯：第一根定義區間、第二根掃蕩單邊流動性後收回區間內部、第三根提供進場並瞄準另一端流動性。相較於傳統在紐約開盤前後進行逆勢操作的做法，講者提出了一套優化的「順勢延續框架」：在明確趨勢中等待價格回調至折價區（Discount）或溢價區（Premium），僅針對逆勢回調 K線標記區間；待確認流動性掃蕩並收回後，下切至小時間框架（如 5 分鐘或 15 分鐘圖）尋找公允價值缺口（FVG）精準掛單進場，並於 1:1 風險報酬比處平倉一半部位鎖定零風險，從而實現順應主力資金流向的穩健交易。

[00:02:10] K線區間高點與低點 (CRH & CRL)：參考基準 K線的上方流動性位準（最高價，CRH）與下方流動性位準（最低價，CRL）。

·        [00:02:33] Liquidity Sweep (Manipulation): When price penetrates beyond the CRH or CRL with a wick to trigger stop-loss orders, then immediately reverses and closes back inside the range.

[00:02:33] 流動性掃蕩 (Liquidity Sweep)：價格以影線刺穿 CRH 或 CRL 以觸發停損單（獲取流動性），隨後立即反轉並收盤回到原 K線區間內部的行為。

·        [00:03:42] New York Session Open Reversal: A popular counter-trend CRT application targeting the 1-hour candle right before the New York open to capture reversals after sweeping London session liquidity.

[00:03:42] 紐約開盤反轉模式 (New York Session Open Reversal)：常見的逆勢 CRT 應用方式，鎖定紐約時段開盤前的 1 小時 K線，捕捉其掃蕩倫敦時段流動性後的強勢反轉行情。

[00:04:27] 多重匯合 (Confluence)：多個技術分析要素（如 CRT 流動性掃蕩、交易時段特性與大趨勢方向）同時指向同一方向，藉此提高交易勝率。

·        [00:06:19] Fair Value Gap (FVG): A three-candle price imbalance formed on a lower timeframe after a liquidity sweep, utilized as a high-precision entry zone.

[00:06:19] 公允價值缺口 (Fair Value Gap, FVG)：在流動性掃蕩發生後，於較小時間框架上形成的三根 K線價格失衡區間，在本策略中作為精準掛單進場區域。

·        [00:06:35] 1:1 Risk-to-Reward Partial Close (Risk-Free Management): A risk management rule of closing 50% of the position at a 1:1 R:R target to secure profits and eliminate capital risk on the remaining runner.

[00:06:35] 1:1 風險報酬比部分平倉 (1:1 R:R Partial Close)：當價格達到 1:1 風險報酬比時平倉一半部位的風控機制，確保即使後續價格反轉打到止損，整筆交易仍能保本無損。

·        [00:07:06] Discount & Premium Zones: Price regions within an impulsive swing leg; buyers look for entries in the lower half (Discount), while sellers look for entries in the upper half (Premium).

[00:07:06] 折價區與溢價區 (Discount & Premium Zones)：單波推動行情中的價格相對區間；上漲趨勢中於下半部（折價區）尋找買點，下跌趨勢中於上半部（溢價區）尋找賣點。

4. Mental Models & Logic (Why & How)
4. 底層邏輯與思維模型 (Mental Models & Logic)

1. Multi-Timeframe Fractal Compression (Why Single Candles Matter): The speaker uses fractal market logic: a single higher-timeframe (HTF) candlestick is simply a compressed representation of lower-timeframe (LTF) price swings. Therefore, its high and low naturally correspond to LTF swing highs/lows where stop orders accumulate. Observing one HTF candle eliminates LTF noise while pinpointing exact liquidity pools.

1. 多時間框架碎形壓縮思維（為何單根 K線具有意義）：講者運用了市場碎形（Fractal）邏輯：大時間框架（HTF）上的單一 K線，本質上是小時間框架（LTF）波段結構的壓縮呈現。因此，大週期 K線的高低點必然對應小週期的波段高低點，也就是市場止損單（流動性）最密集的區域。透過觀察單根大週期 K線，交易者無需陷入小週期雜訊，即可直接鎖定核心流動性池。

2. Liquidity Inducement & Sweep vs. True Breakout (How Institutional Order Flow Operates): Institutions require opposing liquidity to fill large orders. A wick piercing CRH/CRL followed by a close back inside proves the move was a liquidity grab (stop hunt) rather than genuine breakout momentum, signaling an imminent reversal toward the opposite end of the range.

2. 流動性誘導掃蕩 vs. 真正突破的二元判別（主力資金運作邏輯）：機構主力需要對手盤流動性才能建立大額部位。當價格以影線刺穿 CRH 或 CRL 後迅速收回區間內部，證明該動作僅是「獵殺止損（獲取流動性）」而非真實的突破動能，進而推導出價格將反轉並襲擊區間另一端流動性的高概率結論。

3. Trend-Aligned Confluence Filtering (Why Shift from Counter-Trend to Trend-Following): While standalone CRT works at session opens, trading against the dominant trend lowers win rates. By filtering CRT setups strictly in the direction of the higher-timeframe impulse and requiring pullbacks into Discount/Premium zones, the speaker stacks three independent edges: Trend Direction + Institutional Manipulation + Optimal Pricing.

3. 順勢多重匯合過濾模型（為何從逆勢轉向順勢架構）：雖然獨立的 CRT 在特定開盤時段有效，但逆勢操作容易遭遇趨勢延續的風險。講者透過「大趨勢方向濾網」與「折價/溢價區間定位」，僅在回調的逆勢 K線上尋找掃蕩信號，成功將「趨勢動能 + 主力洗盤 + 優勢成本區」三大優勢疊加，大幅提升策略穩定性。

4. Asymmetric Risk Engineering (How to Eliminate Emotional & Capital Risk): Instead of predicting how far a trend will run, the speaker splits risk management into two phases: securing a 1:1 partial profit to neutralize initial risk (turning worst-case reversals into breakeven trades), while leaving the second half exposed to asymmetric upside.

4. 不對稱風險工程思維（如何消除資金與情緒風險）：講者不盲目預測趨勢終點，而是將出場拆解為兩階段：第一階段在 1:1 風險報酬比平倉 50%，使剩餘部位的風險降為零（即使後續反轉打損也能全身而退）；第二階段讓無風險部位去博取趨勢延續的不對稱高額利潤。

Module 1: Core Principles of Candle Range Theory (00:00:31 - 00:02:20)
模組一：K線區間理論（CRT）的核心原理與定義 (00:00:31 - 00:02:20)

·        【Concept Explanation】: Every candlestick on any timeframe encapsulates a complete trading range. Its highest traded price (CRH) and lowest traded price (CRL) serve as major liquidity magnets for subsequent candles.

【概念解釋】：任何時間框架上的每一根 K線都代表一個完整的交易區間。該期間內成交的最高價（CRH）與最低價（CRL）會成為後續 K線追逐的核心流動性磁鐵。

·        【Key Steps / Workflow】: 1. Select a reference timeframe (e.g., Daily, 1-Hour, or 1-Minute). 2. Identify a single reference candle. 3. Mark its absolute high as Candle Range High (CRH) and absolute low as Candle Range Low (CRL).

【關鍵步驟/操作流程】：1. 選定觀察的時間框架（如日線、1 小時線或 1 分鐘線）。2. 鎖定基準 K線。3. 將該 K線的最高點標記為 K線區間高點（CRH），最低點標記為 K線區間低點（CRL）。

·        【Key Formulas / Terminology】: Candle Range Theory (CRT), Candle Range High (CRH), Candle Range Low (CRL), Liquidity Levels.

【重要公式/專有名詞】：K線區間理論（CRT）、K線區間高點（CRH）、K線區間低點（CRL）、流動性位準（Liquidity Levels）。

·        【Common Pitfalls / Precautions】: Do not overcomplicate CRT by analyzing excessive lower-timeframe candles before marking the range; the single HTF candle already summarizes the turning points.

【常見誤區/注意事項】：切勿在標記區間前過度分析小時間框架的繁雜走勢而自我混亂，單一 HTF K線的高低點本身就已經濃縮了關鍵轉折位。

Module 2: The Three-Candle Mechanics & Setup Validation (00:01:14 - 00:03:30)
模組二：三根 K線運作機制與格局有效性判定 (00:01:14 - 00:03:30)

·        【Concept Explanation】: A valid CRT pattern requires three sequential roles: Candle 1 sets the range boundaries, Candle 2 sweeps liquidity past one boundary and closes back inside, and Candle 3 executes the move toward the opposite boundary.

【概念解釋】：有效的 CRT 型態依賴三根連續 K線的分工：第 1 根設定區間邊界、第 2 根刺穿單邊邊界掃蕩流動性並收回區間內、第 3 根執行向另一端邊界的推進。

·        【Key Steps / Workflow】: 1. Wait for Candle 2 to pierce above CRH (for shorts) or below CRL (for longs). 2. Confirm Candle 2 closes back inside the Candle 1 range. 3. Look to Candle 3 for entry targeting the opposite liquidity pool.

【關鍵步驟/操作流程】：1. 等待第 2 根 K線刺穿 CRH 上方（準備做空）或 CRL 下方（準備做多）。2. 確認第 2 根 K線收盤回到第 1 根 K線的區間內部。3. 在第 3 根 K線尋找進場點，目標直指區間另一端的流動性。

·        【Key Formulas / Terminology】: Range Candle (Candle 1), Sweep Candle (Candle 2), Entry Candle (Candle 3), Setup Invalidation.

【重要公式/專有名詞】：區間 K線（第 1 根）、掃蕩 K線（第 2 根）、進場 K線（第 3 根）、格局失效（Setup Invalidation）。

·        【Common Pitfalls / Precautions】: If Candle 2 closes with its body outside the CRH or CRL, the CRT setup is completely invalid because price is likely continuing in the breakout direction rather than reversing.

【常見誤區/注意事項】：如果第 2 根 K線的實體收盤在 CRH 上方或 CRL 下方，則該 CRT 格局立即失效，因為市場極可能延續突破方向推進，而非反轉。

Module 3: Session Timing vs. Trend-Following Paradigm Shift (00:03:35 - 00:05:03)
模組三：紐約開盤逆勢策略 vs. 順勢交易策略轉型 (00:03:35 - 00:05:03)

·        【Concept Explanation】: While many traders apply CRT to the 1-hour candle prior to the New York session open to catch reversals of London session liquidity, that approach is often counter-trend. Aligning CRT with the prevailing market trend yields higher confidence and win rates.

【概念解釋】：許多交易者習慣將 CRT 應用於紐約開盤前的 1 小時 K線，以捕捉其掃蕩倫敦時段流動性後的反轉行情，但這往往屬於逆勢交易。將 CRT 與市場主趨勢結合，能獲得更高的勝率與交易信心。

·        【Key Steps / Workflow】: 1. Identify a market in a clear uptrend or downtrend. 2. Refrain from chasing breakouts; wait for a corrective pullback phase. 3. Apply CRT strictly in the direction of the dominant trend.

【關鍵步驟/操作流程】：1. 識別處於明確上漲或下跌趨勢的市場。2. 避免追高殺低，耐心等待價格進入回調階段。3. 僅沿著主要趨勢方向尋找並執行 CRT 交易機會。

·        【Key Formulas / Terminology】: New York Session Open, London Session Liquidity, Impulsive Move, Corrective Pullback.

【重要公式/專有名詞】：紐約時段開盤、倫敦時段流動性、推動波（Impulsive Move）、回調波（Correction）。

·        【Common Pitfalls / Precautions】: Trading counter-trend reversals without strong session timing confluence exposes traders to getting run over by dominant institutional momentum.

【常見誤區/注意事項】：在缺乏強烈時段匯合條件下盲目進行逆勢反轉交易，極易被市場的主力趨勢動能碾壓。

Module 4: Trend-Following CRT Execution Blueprint (Bullish & Bearish) (00:05:03 - 00:08:25)
模組四：順勢 CRT 標準化進場藍圖（多空雙向與折溢價區） (00:05:03 - 00:08:25)

·        【Concept Explanation】: In an uptrend, traders wait for pullbacks into the Discount zone and track bearish candles for a CRL sweep. In a downtrend, traders wait for pullbacks into the Premium zone and track bullish candles for a CRH sweep, then refine entries on LTF Fair Value Gaps.

【概念解釋】：上漲趨勢中，等待價格回調至折價區並鎖定陰線（Bearish candles）等待 CRL 被掃蕩；下跌趨勢中，等待回調至溢價區並鎖定陽線（Bullish candles）等待 CRH 被掃蕩，隨後下切小週期利用 FVG 精準進場。

·        【Key Steps / Workflow】: 1. Wait for pullback into Discount (uptrend) or Premium (downtrend). 2. Mark CRH/CRL on each counter-trend candle; if broken by a body close, shift the box to the new counter-trend candle. 3. Once a candle sweeps and closes back inside, drop from 1H to 5M/15M. 4. Place a limit order at the post-sweep FVG with stop-loss beyond the gap.

【關鍵步驟/操作流程】：1. 等待回調進入折價區（做多）或溢價區（做空）。2. 標記每根逆勢 K線的高低點；若被實體收盤跌破/突破，則將區間順延至下一根逆勢 K線。3. 一旦出現影線掃蕩並收回區間內，從 1 小時圖切換至 5 分鐘或 15 分鐘圖。4. 在掃蕩後形成的 FVG 掛限價單，止損設於缺口外側。

·        【Key Formulas / Terminology】: Discount Zone, Premium Zone, Bearish/Bullish Pullback Candle, Fair Value Gap (FVG), Limit Order.

【重要公式/專有名詞】：折價區（Discount Zone）、溢價區（Premium Zone）、逆勢回調 K線、公允價值缺口（FVG）、限價單（Limit Order）。

·        【Common Pitfalls / Precautions】: Do not apply CRT to bullish candles during an uptrend pullback (or bearish candles during a downtrend pullback), and do not enter blindly before checking LTF FVG alignment.

【常見誤區/注意事項】：上漲回調時切勿標記陽線（下跌回調時切勿標記陰線），且在小週期尚未形成有效 FVG 結構前切忌盲目市價追單。
