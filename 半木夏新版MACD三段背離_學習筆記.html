•      [00:00:56] [MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)]: a momentum indicator; the video uses the most common parameters: fast 12, slow 26, signal smoothing 9.

•      [00:01:02] [Top Divergence]: price makes three successive highs while the histogram’s green bars shrink twice in a row.

•      [00:01:27] [Opposite-Color Bar Rule]: a bar of the opposite color must appear (or come very close) between segments, otherwise it is not counted as a separate rise or fall.

[反色柱間隔規則]：各段柱體之間必須出現（或非常接近出現）相反顏色的柱體，否則不算一段獨立的上漲或下跌。
