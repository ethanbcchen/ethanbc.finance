ICT Concepts Deep Study Notes: Buyside & Sellside Liquidity
ICT 交易概念深度學習筆記：買方流動性與賣方流動性

1. Executive Summary
一、核心總結

This video systematically breaks down the foundational mechanics of Buyside Liquidity (BSL) and Sellside Liquidity (SSL) within the Inner Circle Trader (ICT) methodology. The core premise rests on retail trader psychology: traders placing long positions position their stop-loss orders (Sell Stops) beneath Swing Lows, whereas short sellers place their stop-loss orders (Buy Stops) above Swing Highs. Institutional participants, termed Smart Money, actively target these clusters of resting stop orders to pair large institutional orders. Liquidity is categorized into two primary structural formations: single prominent Old Highs/Lows and clustered Relatively Equal Highs/Lows (EQH/EQL). To translate theory into actionable chart execution, traders must consistently mark three critical multi-timeframe liquidity tiers: Previous Week's High/Low (PWH/PWL), Previous Day's High/Low (PDH/PDL), and Session Highs/Lows (Asia and London). Ultimately, these liquidity pools serve either as a magnetic Draw on Liquidity (DOL) propelling price expansion, or as reversal catalysts when swept and followed by market Displacement and entry via a Fair Value Gap (FVG).

本教學影片系統性地拆解了 ICT（Inner Circle Trader，內圈交易者）交易體系中「買方流動性（Buyside Liquidity, BSL）」與「賣方流動性（Sellside Liquidity, SSL）」的底層運作機制。其核心建立在散戶交易心理之上：做多者習慣將止損單（賣出止損單 Sell Stops）設在擺動低點（Swing Low）下方，而做空者則將止損單（買入止損單 Buy Stops）設在擺動高點（Swing High）上方。機構大資金（聰明錢 Smart Money）會主動獵取這些停留止損單的區域，以完成大額訂單的流動性配對。流動性在圖表上主要呈現為兩種結構型態：單一突出的「舊高點與舊低點（Old Highs/Lows）」，以及密集聚集的「相對等高點與等低點（Relatively Equal Highs/Lows, EQH/EQL）」。為了將理論轉化為實戰框架，交易者必須在圖表上固定標記三個關鍵時間週期的流動性水平：前週高低點（PWH/PWL）、前日高低點（PDH/PDL），以及交易時段高低點（亞洲盤與倫敦盤）。最終，這些流動性池在實務上具備雙重功能：既可作為吸引價格推進的「流動性牽引目標（Draw on Liquidity, DOL）」，亦可在被掃蕩（Sweep）並伴隨強勢「位移（Displacement）」與「公允價值缺口（Fair Value Gap, FVG）」回踩後，成為高勝率反轉交易的觸發點。

2. Key Concepts & Definitions (With Timestamps)
二、知識地圖與核心概念（附時間戳記）

[00:00:30] Swing Low (擺動低點): A three-bar price formation consisting of a central low flanked by a higher low on the left and a higher low on the right.

由三根K線組成的價格結構，中間為最低點，且其左側與右側各有一個更高的低點（Higher Low）。

[00:00:45] Swing High (擺動高點): A three-bar price formation consisting of a central high flanked by a lower high on the left and a lower high on the right.

由三根K線組成的價格結構，中間為最高點，且其左側與右側各有一個更低的高點（Lower High）。

[00:01:00 - 00:01:21] Sellside Liquidity (SSL / 賣方流動性): Clusters of resting Sell Stop orders placed by long-biased retail traders beneath Swing Lows, targeted by Smart Money to pair buy orders.

停留在擺動低點下方的「賣出止損單（Sell Stops）」集合（來自多單的止損），是聰明錢（Smart Money）用來配對買單的目標區域。

[00:01:00 - 00:01:21] Buyside Liquidity (BSL / 買方流動性): Clusters of resting Buy Stop orders placed by short-biased retail traders above Swing Highs, targeted by Smart Money to pair sell orders.

停留在擺動高點上方的「買入止損單（Buy Stops）」集合（來自空單的止損），是聰明錢用來配對賣單的目標區域。

[00:04:47] Relatively Equal Highs & Lows (EQH & EQL / 相對等高點與等低點): Two or more distinct swing points positioned at nearly identical or closely clustered price levels, creating concentrated pools of retail stop orders.

兩個或多個彼此高度相近、聚集在同一水平帶的擺動高點或低點，形成散戶止損單高度集中的流動性池。

[00:04:58] Old Highs & Old Lows (舊高點與舊低點): A single, isolated, prominent historical swing high or swing low standing out clearly on the price chart.

圖表上單一獨立、清晰突出的歷史擺動高點或擺動低點。

[00:05:19] Draw on Liquidity (DOL / 流動性牽引目標): An unmitigated pool of Buyside or Sellside liquidity toward which price algorithmically gravitates to fulfill institutional order pairing.

尚未被獵取的買方或賣方流動性池，價格會像磁鐵般被吸引並推進至該處以完成機構訂單配對。

[00:05:37] Fair Value Gap (FVG / 公允價值缺口): A three-candle price imbalance or inefficiency where wicks do not overlap, serving as an institutional retracement and entry zone after a liquidity sweep.

由三根K線構成的價格失衡區（上下影線未重疊的缺口），在流動性被掃蕩後作為機構回踩測試與精準進場的區域。

[00:07:00] Displacement (位移 / 強勢動能): An energetic, impulsive price move characterized by large-bodied candles showing clear institutional commitment and directional intent.

伴隨大實體K線的強勁單邊價格衝擊波，代表機構大資金的明確介入與方向性意圖。

4. Mental Models & Underlying Logic (Why & How)
四、底層邏輯與思維模型

Step 1: Order Pairing Mechanics (Why Smart Money Hunts Stops): Institutional algorithms cannot execute massive positions without sufficient counterparty liquidity. Because retail traders predictably cluster stop-loss orders just beyond obvious Swing Highs and Swing Lows, Smart Money engineers price moves toward these levels to absorb retail stop orders and fill institutional positions.

第一步：訂單配對機制（為何聰明錢獵殺止損）—— 機構演算法若缺乏足夠的對手盤流動性，便無法執行大額倉位。由於散戶習慣將止損單集中設在明顯的擺動高點上方與擺動低點下方，聰明錢會引導價格觸及這些區域，藉此吸收散戶止損單以完成自身大單的配對建倉。

Step 2: Liquidity State Tracking (Dynamic Migration of Pools): Liquidity is dynamic rather than static. Once an existing Swing High or Low is swept (taken out), that specific liquidity pool is neutralized, and the trader's focus must immediately shift to the newly formed outer swing points or remaining untouched pools.

第二步：流動性狀態追蹤（動態轉移思維）—— 流動性並非靜止不變。一旦既有的擺動高點或低點被獵取（Taken Out），該處的流動性池即告失效，交易者必須立即將注意力轉移至新形成的區間外側擺動點，或其他尚未被觸及的流動性池。

Step 3: Top-Down Timeframe Hierarchy (From Macro to Session Narrative): The speaker structures daily chart analysis from higher-timeframe reference points down to intraday sessions: marking PWH/PWL first, then PDH/PDL, and finally Asia/London session extremes. Once session highs and lows are exhausted, price naturally seeks the next higher-tier liquidity pool (e.g., PDH/PDL).

第三步：由上而下的時間週期層級（從宏觀到盤中敘事）—— 講者遵循由大週期到小週期的分析邏輯：先標出前週高低點（PWH/PWL），再標出前日高低點（PDH/PDL），最後標出亞洲盤與倫敦盤高低點。當盤中時段的流動性皆被掃蕩完畢後，價格自然會向更高層級的流動性目標（如前日高低點）推進。

Step 4: Confirmation Filter — Sweep vs. Breakout (Displacement & FVG): Taking out a liquidity level alone is not an automatic entry signal. The analyst evaluates whether price shows genuine Displacement beyond the level (continuation) or rejects the level with Displacement in the opposite direction, confirming an institutional reversal via a 5-minute Fair Value Gap (FVG).

第四步：確認過濾器 — 真突破與假突破掃蕩（位移與 FVG 驗證）—— 單純獵取流動性水平並非直接進場訊號。分析者必須觀察價格突破該水平後，是出現同向的強勢位移（延續走勢），還是遭拒絕並產生反向強勢位移，進而透過回踩 5 分鐘公允價值缺口（FVG）確認機構級反轉。

5. Detailed Breakdown by Core Modules (With Timestamps)
五、詳細重點拆解（核心主題模組）

Module 1: Swing Points & Stop-Loss Psychology [00:00:00 - 00:01:37]
模組一：擺動點定義與止損心理學 [00:00:00 - 00:01:37]

• Concept Explanation: Swing points are structural turning points on a chart where retail stop-loss orders accumulate, forming Buyside Liquidity (BSL) and Sellside Liquidity (SSL).

  【概念解釋】：擺動點是圖表上的結構轉折點，也是散戶止損單密集停留之處，從而形成買方流動性（BSL）與賣方流動性（SSL）。

• Key Operational Steps: (1) Identify a Swing Low by locating a candle low with a higher low immediately to its left and right. (2) Identify a Swing High by locating a candle high with a lower high immediately to its left and right. (3) Map Sell Stops beneath Swing Lows (SSL) and Buy Stops above Swing Highs (BSL).

  【關鍵步驟/操作流程】：(1) 尋找左側與右側各有一個更高低點的K線低點，確認為擺動低點；(2) 尋找左側與右側各有一個更低高點的K線高點，確認為擺動高點；(3) 將擺動低點下方標記為賣出止損區（SSL），擺動高點上方標記為買入止損區（BSL）。

• Key Terms: Swing High (擺動高點), Swing Low (擺動低點), Sell Stops (賣出止損單), Buy Stops (買入止損單), Smart Money (聰明錢).

  【重要公式/專有名詞】：Swing High（擺動高點）、Swing Low（擺動低點）、Sell Stops（賣出止損單）、Buy Stops（買入止損單）、Smart Money（聰明錢）。

• Common Pitfalls: Treating every minor intraday wiggle as significant liquidity; instead, prioritize obvious swing points located on the outer boundaries of a price range.

  【常見誤區/注意事項】：切勿將盤中所有微小的波動都當作重要流動性，應優先專注於價格區間「外側」最明顯突出的高低點。

Module 2: Dynamic Tracking of Swing Liquidity on 4H Charts [00:01:37 - 00:04:33]
模組二：四小時圖表上的流動性動態追蹤演練 [00:01:37 - 00:04:33]

• Concept Explanation: As price evolves across a 4-hour timeframe, liquidity levels continuously shift as old swing points get swept and new swing points form.

  【概念解釋】：隨著四小時（4H）圖表走勢推進，舊的擺動點被獵取、新的擺動點不斷生成，流動性水平呈現動態轉移的循環。

• Key Operational Steps: (1) Mark initial obvious outer Swing Highs and Lows on the 4H chart. (2) Once price trades through a Swing High, remove focus from that swept level and wait for a new lower high to form on the right side to confirm the next BSL. (3) When a new low fails to break a prior low, group them together as an active SSL target.

  【關鍵步驟/操作流程】：(1) 先在四小時圖上標出區間外側最明顯的擺動高低點；(2) 一旦價格突破某個擺動高點，立即解除對該舊高點的關注，等待右側形成新的更低高點以確認下一個 BSL；(3) 當新低點未能跌破前低時，將兩者合併視為有效的 SSL 目標。

• Key Terms: Four-Hour Chart (四小時圖表), Outer Range Highs/Lows (區間外側高低點), Liquidity Sweep / Take Out (獵取流動性).

  【重要公式/專有名詞】：Four-Hour Chart（四小時圖表）、Outer Range Highs/Lows（區間外側高低點）、Take Out（獵取/掃蕩）。

• Common Pitfalls: Continuing to rely on a liquidity level that has already been swept, or prematurely marking a swing point before the right-side confirmation candle closes.

  【常見誤區/注意事項】：切勿繼續執著於已被獵取過的舊流動性位置，也不要在右側確認K線尚未收盤前就過早認定擺動點已成立。

Module 3: Old Highs/Lows vs. Relatively Equal Highs/Lows [00:04:34 - 00:05:11]
模組三：舊高低點與相對等高低點的結構辨識 [00:04:34 - 00:05:11]

• Concept Explanation: Liquidity manifests in two distinct visual patterns on price charts: isolated single swing extremes and horizontally aligned multiple swing points.

  【概念解釋】：流動性在價格圖表上主要呈現兩種視覺型態：單一孤立的擺動極值，以及水平對齊的多重擺動點。

• Key Operational Steps: (1) Scan for single, prominent swing peaks or troughs standing alone without nearby resistance/support—label these as Old Highs or Old Lows. (2) Scan for two or more swing highs or lows clustered at roughly the same price level—label these as Relatively Equal Highs (EQH) or Relatively Equal Lows (EQL).

  【關鍵步驟/操作流程】：(1) 掃描圖表中單獨突出、周圍無相近阻力的單一擺動波峰或波谷，標記為「舊高點（Old High）」或「舊低點（Old Low）」；(2) 掃描兩個以上聚集在幾乎相同價格水平的擺動高點或低點，標記為「相對等高點（EQH）」或「相對等低點（EQL）」。

• Key Terms: Old Highs and Old Lows (舊高點與舊低點), Relatively Equal Highs (EQH / 相對等高點), Relatively Equal Lows (EQL / 相對等低點).

  【重要公式/專有名詞】：Old Highs & Old Lows（舊高點與舊低點）、Relatively Equal Highs/Lows（相對等高點/等低點）。

• Common Pitfalls: Expecting Equal Highs or Equal Lows to act as impenetrable 'double top' or 'double bottom' support/resistance; in ICT logic, they act as high-probability magnets for stop hunts.

  【常見誤區/注意事項】：切勿以傳統技術分析的「雙重頂/雙重底」思維將等高點或等低點視為堅固的支撐阻力；在 ICT 邏輯中，它們反而是吸引機構獵殺止損的強大磁鐵。

Module 4: Mandatory Multi-Timeframe Liquidity Levels [00:05:12 - 00:06:22]
模組四：圖表必標的三層級時間週期流動性水平 [00:05:12 - 00:06:22]

• Concept Explanation: To eliminate guesswork, traders should systematically mark three standardized time-based liquidity tiers on their charts to serve as Draws on Liquidity or reversal zones.

  【概念解釋】：為避免主觀猜測，交易者應在圖表上系統性地標記三個標準化的時間基準流動性層級，作為流動性牽引目標（DOL）或反轉觀察區。

• Key Operational Steps: (1) Mark Previous Week's High and Low (PWH/PWL)—e.g., identifying a Tuesday low and Thursday high from the prior week. (2) Mark Previous Day's High and Low (PDH/PDL). (3) Mark Session Highs and Lows, specifically the Asia Session High/Low and London Session High/Low.

  【關鍵步驟/操作流程】：(1) 標出「前週高點與低點（PWH/PWL）」（例如前一週的週二低點與週四高點）；(2) 標出「前日高點與低點（PDH/PDL）」；(3) 標出「交易時段高低點（Session Highs/Lows）」，特別是亞洲盤高低點與倫敦盤高低點。

• Key Terms: Previous Week's High/Low (PWH/PWL / 前週高低點), Previous Day High/Low (PDH/PDL / 前日高低點), Session Highs and Lows (交易時段高低點), Narrative (市場敘事).

  【重要公式/專有名詞】：Previous Week's High/Low（前週高低點）、Previous Day High/Low（前日高低點）、Session Highs/Lows（交易時段高低點）、Narrative（市場敘事）。

• Common Pitfalls: Cluttering the chart with dozens of arbitrary trendlines while neglecting objective, time-anchored highs and lows (weekly, daily, and session boundaries).

  【常見誤區/注意事項】：避免在圖表上畫滿主觀隨意的趨勢線，卻忽略了具備客觀時間錨點（週、日、交易時段邊界）的核心高低點。

Module 5: Intraday Session Reversal Case Study (New York Open) [00:06:23 - 00:07:48]
模組五：盤中時段反轉實戰案例（紐約開盤獵殺流動性） [00:06:23 - 00:07:48]

• Concept Explanation: Untouched Asia and London session highs/lows provide a complete intraday roadmap for framing high-probability New York session reversals.

  【概念解釋】：尚未被觸及的亞洲盤與倫敦盤高低點，能為紐約盤的高勝率反轉交易提供完整的盤中路徑圖。

• Key Operational Steps: (1) Observe prior session liquidity: Asia low is already swept, while Asia high, London high, and London low (forming equal lows) remain intact. (2) Into the New York open, wait for price to rally above Midnight Open and sweep both London high and Asia high. (3) Wait for bearish Displacement downward (do not enter without displacement). (4) Enter short on a retracement into the 5-minute Fair Value Gap (FVG) where candle bodies respect the gap, targeting the untouched London session equal lows.

  【關鍵步驟/操作流程】：(1) 盤點前期流動性：亞洲盤低點已被獵取，但亞洲盤高點、倫敦盤高點以及倫敦盤低點（形成相對等低點）仍完好無損；(2) 進入紐約開盤時段，等待價格向上突破午夜開盤價（Midnight Open）並同時掃蕩倫敦盤高點與亞洲盤高點；(3) 耐心等待向下的強勢「位移（Displacement）」出現；(4) 當價格回踩 5 分鐘公允價值缺口（FVG）且實體K線收盤均未突破缺口時進場做空，止盈目標鎖定下方的倫敦盤等低點。

• Key Terms: New York Open (紐約開盤), Midnight Open (午夜開盤價), Break of Structure (BOS / 市場結構破壞), Displacement (位移), 5-Minute Fair Value Gap (五分鐘公允價值缺口).

  【重要公式/專有名詞】：New York Open（紐約開盤）、Midnight Open（午夜開盤價）、Break of Structure（市場結構破壞）、Displacement（位移）、5-Minute FVG（五分鐘公允價值缺口）。

• Common Pitfalls: Shorting immediately when London/Asia highs are breached before seeing bearish Displacement, or holding past the primary opposing liquidity target (London session low).

  【常見誤區/注意事項】：切勿在價格剛突破倫敦/亞洲盤高點、尚未出現向下位移時就盲目摸頂做空；同時應在觸及主要反向流動性目標（倫敦盤低點）時及時獲利了結。

Module 6: Sequential Liquidity Exhaustion & Expansion to PDH [00:07:49 - 00:09:19]
模組六：流動性逐級消耗與向前日高點擴張案例 [00:07:49 - 00:09:19]

• Concept Explanation: When both sides of intraday session liquidity (Asia and London) are swept without sustained downside displacement, price shifts its Draw on Liquidity toward higher-timeframe targets such as Previous Day High (PDH).

  【概念解釋】：當盤中交易時段（亞洲盤與倫敦盤）的上下兩側流動性皆被掃蕩，且下方缺乏延續性的向下位移時，價格會將流動性牽引目標轉向更高週期的「前日高點（PDH）」。

• Key Operational Steps: (1) Mark PDH and PDL before the session begins. (2) Observe Asia high getting swept, followed by London session sweeping both London low and Asia low without bearish displacement below the lows. (3) Identify bullish Displacement upward and enter long on the 5-minute FVG retracement. (4) Take initial targets at London session high; once price bases above Midnight Open and sweeps internal lows, hold/target the remaining unmitigated PDH, where strong displacement over PDH confirms continued expansion.

  【關鍵步驟/操作流程】：(1) 開盤前先標記前日高點（PDH）與前日低點（PDL）；(2) 觀察亞洲盤高點先被獵取，隨後倫敦盤向下掃蕩倫敦盤低點與亞洲盤低點，但下方並無真正的向下位移；(3) 確認出現向上的強勢位移後，於 5 分鐘 FVG 回踩處進場做多；(4) 先以倫敦盤高點為第一目標，待價格在午夜開盤價上方盤整並掃蕩內部低點後，由於時段高低點皆已清空，將最終目標鎖定尚未觸及的 PDH，價格隨後以強勢位移突破 PDH 並持續走高。

• Key Terms: Liquidity Exhaustion (流動性消耗完畢), Internal Low Sweep (內部低點掃蕩), Expansion (價格擴張).

  【重要公式/專有名詞】：Liquidity Exhaustion（流動性消耗）、Internal Low Sweep（內部低點掃蕩）、Expansion（價格擴張）。

• Common Pitfalls: Failing to look at higher-timeframe liquidity (PDH/PDL) after intraday session highs and lows have already been cleared, causing traders to miss the primary daily expansion leg.

  【常見誤區/注意事項】：當盤中時段的高低點都已被獵取完畢後，若忘記向上尋找更高層級的流動性目標（如 PDH/PDL），便會錯失當日最主要的趨勢擴張波段。

6. Practical Action Items Checklist
六、實操行動清單（Action Items）

1. Perform Daily Pre-Market Liquidity Mapping: Before every trading session, open your chart and draw horizontal levels for Previous Week's High/Low (PWH/PWL) and Previous Day's High/Low (PDH/PDL).

1. 執行每日盤前流動性地圖標記：在每個交易日開盤前，打開圖表並精準畫出「前週高低點（PWH/PWL）」與「前日高低點（PDH/PDL）」的水平線。

2. Mark Asia and London Session Extremes Before New York Open: At the conclusion of the London session (or prior to the New York open), box out or mark the exact Asia Session High/Low and London Session High/Low to identify which pools remain untouched.

2. 紐約開盤前標定亞洲盤與倫敦盤極值：在紐約開盤前，明確標記出當日亞洲盤高低點與倫敦盤高低點，盤點哪些流動性池已被獵取、哪些仍完好無損。

3. Conduct a 4-Hour Swing Point & EQH/EQL Audit: Switch to the 4-hour timeframe and practice labeling only outer-range Swing Highs/Lows (verified by 3-bar left/right rules) and clustering Relatively Equal Highs/Lows (EQH/EQL).

3. 進行四小時級別擺動點與等高低點盤點：切換至 4 小時圖表，嚴格依據「左右兩側各一高/低點」的規則，僅標記區間外側的明顯擺動點以及相對等高點/等低點（EQH/EQL）。

4. Enforce the 'Sweep + Displacement + 5M FVG' Entry Rule: Do not fade a breakout immediately. Wait for a liquidity level to be swept, confirm an opposite-direction Displacement move on the 5-minute chart, and place limit/market entries only upon a retracement into the resulting 5M Fair Value Gap.

4. 嚴格執行「掃蕩 + 位移 + 5分鐘 FVG」進場鐵律：絕不在價格剛突破高低點時立刻逆勢摸頂抄底；必須等待流動性被掃蕩後，在 5 分鐘圖上確認出現反向強勢「位移（Displacement）」，並於回踩 5 分鐘公允價值缺口（FVG）時才執行進場。

5. Set Take-Profit Targets at Opposing Unmitigated Liquidity Pools: Align your risk-to-reward by setting take-profit targets directly at the nearest untouched opposing liquidity pool (e.g., targeting London Equal Lows after sweeping Asia/London Highs, or targeting PDH once session highs/lows are cleared).

5. 將止盈目標設定在反向未獵取的流動性池：將止盈點直接設在最近一個尚未被觸及的反向流動性池（例如：掃蕩亞洲/倫敦盤高點後，將目標設在倫敦盤等低點；若時段高低點皆已清空，則將目標推進至前日高點 PDH）。
