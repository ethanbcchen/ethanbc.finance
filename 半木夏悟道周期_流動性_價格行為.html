1. Executive Summary
1. 核心總結 (Executive Summary)

This study note deconstructs the evolved trading system of legendary crypto trader Banmuxia (半木夏), who turned 10,000 RMB into nearly 50 million RMB. Over two years, his methodology shifted from relying heavily on single technical indicators like MACD to a comprehensive, top-down framework integrating Market Cycles (週期), Macro & On-Chain Liquidity (流動性), and Price Action Technical Analysis (價格行為技術分析). The core philosophy is 'trading with the major trend' (順大勢而為): first, use Moving Averages (MA30, MA120, MA256) and sentiment extremes to identify the exact stage among eight market cycle phases; second, track macro capital inflows and outflows via the Federal Reserve Balance Sheet, the US Treasury General Account (TGA), major catalysts, exchange order books, and on-chain whale wallets; third, execute precise entries, stop-losses, and take-profits using structural support/resistance (POC, Fibonacci, MA), chart patterns (convergence, head and shoulders), and custom MACD divergence (13, 34). Ultimately, sustainable profitability requires strict risk management—cutting losses decisively to stay proactive, avoiding shorting in bull markets, and utilizing funding rate arbitrage during high-volatility consolidation.

本學習筆記深度拆解傳奇加密貨幣交易員「半木夏」從一萬本金滾動至近五千萬資產的進化版交易體系。在兩年時間內，他的策略從早期高度依賴單一技術指標（如半神 MACD），蛻變為結合「市場週期 (Market Cycle)」、「宏觀與鏈上流動性 (Liquidity)」以及「價格行為技術分析 (Price Action)」的全方位由上而下（Top-Down）決策框架。其核心哲學在於「順大勢而為」：首先，透過三條核心移動平均線（MA30、MA120、MA256）與市場情緒極值，精準定位當前處於八大週期中的哪一階段；其次，透過聯準會資產負債表、美國財政部 TGA 帳戶、重大催化劑、交易所訂單簿及鏈上巨鯨地址，監控驅動價格波動的真實資金流向；最後，回歸圖表技術面，結合關鍵結構位（POC、斐波那契、均線）、價格形態（收斂結構、頭肩形態）與改良參數的 MACD 背離（13, 34）尋找高勝率入場點。最終，交易的本質是「市場出招、交易者接招」，唯有果斷止損以掌握主動權、在牛市中堅持不輕易做空並善用資金費率套利，方能實現長期穩健複利。

2. Key Concepts & Definitions (With Timestamps)
2. 知識地圖與核心概念 (Key Concepts & Definitions)

• [01:10] Eight-Phase Market Cycle (市場八大週期): A granular division of market trends into Bear Market (熊市), Late Bear (熊末), Bear Rally (熊反), Early Bull (牛初), Bull Market (牛市), Late Bull (牛末), Bull Pullback (牛調), and Early Bear (熊初).

• [01:10] Eight-Phase Market Cycle (市場八大週期)：將市場趨勢精細劃分為「熊市、熊末、熊反、牛初、牛市、牛末、牛調、熊初」八個階段，用以界定大勢方向與操作策略。

• [01:30] Triple Moving Average System (三均線系統: MA30, MA120, MA256): Three daily Moving Averages (MA - 移動平均線) with periods 30, 120, and 256 used to objectively determine bull/bear alignment, trend reversals, and pullback support strength.

• [01:30] Triple Moving Average System (三均線系統: MA30, MA120, MA256)：設定參數為 30、120、256 的三條日線級別移動平均線（MA），用於客觀判定多空排列、趨勢轉折點以及回調支撐的強弱程度。

• [02:22] Bad News Stress Test (利空試金石): A market phenomenon in Early Bull (牛初) where asset prices remain resilient despite negative news, proving strong underlying institutional/smart-money buying.

• [02:22] Bad News Stress Test (利空試金石)：牛市初期的典型特徵：市場分歧巨大且頻傳負面消息，但幣價卻異常堅挺，驗證背後有大資金正在持續承接買入。

• [03:40] Federal Reserve Balance Sheet (聯準會資產負債表): A macro liquidity indicator tracking the Fed's financial assets and liabilities; expansion injects USD liquidity into markets, while contraction drains liquidity.

• [03:40] Federal Reserve Balance Sheet (聯準會資產負債表)：記錄聯準會金融資產與負債規模的宏觀指標；擴表代表向市場注入美元流動性，縮表則代表從市場抽離資金。

• [04:15] Treasury General Account / TGA (美國財政部一般帳戶): The US federal government's cash operating account; a declining TGA balance means fiscal spending is injecting liquidity into financial markets, while a rising balance drains it.

• [04:15] Treasury General Account / TGA (美國財政部一般帳戶)：聯邦政府的現金帳戶餘額；TGA 餘額下降代表財政部正在向市場投放資金（增加流動性），餘額上升則代表抽水或減少市場美元。

• [07:05] Point of Control / POC (控制點 / 最密集成交區線): The price level with the highest traded volume within a Fixed Range Volume Profile (固定成交量分布圖), acting as a major structural support or resistance on first retest.

• [07:05] Point of Control / POC (控制點 / 最密集成交區線)：透過「固定成交量分布圖 (Fixed Range Volume Profile)」找出的成交量最集中價格線，價格首次觸及時往往會產生強烈的支撐或阻力效應。

• [07:47] Fibonacci Retracement & Extension (斐波那契回撤與擴展): Technical levels (specifically 0.382 and 0.5 for pullbacks) drawn from trend origin to swing high to project retracement support and profit-taking targets.

• [07:47] Fibonacci Retracement & Extension (斐波那契回撤與擴展)：連接重要趨勢起點與階段高點所繪製的比例線，回撤主要關注 0.382 與 0.5 區域尋找買點，擴展則用於設定止盈目標。

• [08:35] Banshen MACD Divergence (半神 MACD 背離: 13, 34): A customized MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence - 指數平滑異同移動平均線) using fast line 13 and slow line 34 on 1H/4H charts to spot momentum exhaustion via histogram shifts.

• [08:35] Banshen MACD Divergence (半神 MACD 背離: 13, 34)：將快線設為 13、慢線設為 34 的改良版 MACD 指標，在 1 小時或 4 小時級別觀察價格與指標連續背離，並於第 2 或第 3 波峰「實柱轉虛柱」時入場。

• [09:38] Funding Rate Arbitrage (資金費率套利 / 開套保): A delta-neutral hedging strategy used during risky or choppy bull market phases to collect high perpetual contract funding fees (sometimes exceeding 100% APR) without directional exposure.

• [09:38] Funding Rate Arbitrage (資金費率套利 / 開套保)：在牛市高風險或劇烈震盪階段，透過現貨與合約對沖（開套保）鎖定方向性風險，專門賺取極端多頭市場中高達年化 100% 以上的永續合約資金費率。

4. Mental Models & Logic (Why & How)
4. 底層邏輯與思維模型 (Mental Models & Logic)

1. First-Principles Causal Chain (第一性原理因果鏈：資金 → 價格 → K線 → 指標)

1. 第一性原理因果鏈：資金流動 → 買賣行為 → 價格與K線 → 技術指標

Banmuxia de-emphasized pure technical indicators because indicators are lagging derivatives. True causality flows from macro/institutional liquidity (buying and selling power) to price formation, which creates candlesticks, and finally generates chart patterns and indicators. Therefore, liquidity analysis must precede technical entry.

半木夏之所以淡化對單一技術指標的依賴，是因為指標本質上是滯後的衍生品。市場運行的底層因果鏈為：「宏觀與大資金流動性」驅動「買賣行為」，進而形成「價格與 K 線」，最後才衍生出「形態與指標」。因此，分析必須先看資金流動性，再用技術分析找入場點。

2. Top-Down Probabilistic Filtering (由上而下的概率過濾模型)

2. 由上而下的三層概率過濾模型（週期定方向 → 流動性定強度 → 技術定點位）

Instead of predicting random short-term fluctuations, the system filters trades through three concentric layers: (1) Cycle determines directional bias (long-only in bull phases); (2) Liquidity confirms whether enough capital exists to sustain a major trend; (3) Technicals pinpoint high risk-reward entry/exit coordinates.

不盲目預測短期隨機波動，而是透過三層漏斗過濾交易機會：(1) 週期決定大方向（牛市只做多不做空）；(2) 流動性確認是否有足夠資金推動大級別行情；(3) 技術分析負責在圖表上精確定位高盈虧比的買賣點位。

3. Asymmetric Risk & Active Initiative Model (非對稱風險與主動權思維)

3. 試錯成本計算與「主動權」思維模型

Holding a losing position traps capital and forces passive decision-making. By cutting losses immediately upon structural breakdown, a trader preserves capital and psychological clarity, treating the small stop-loss as a calculated 'trial-and-error fee' to re-enter once key support is reclaimed.

扛單被套會讓交易者陷入徹底的被動，喪失後續操作空間。果斷止損雖然會產生小額虧損，但能瞬間拿回資金與心態的主動權；只要事前計算好「止損後重新站穩再接回的差價」，止損就只是可控的試錯成本。

4. Contrarian Sentiment & Structural Resilience Test (逆向情緒與結構抗壓測試)

4. 逆向情緒觀察與「利空抗壓測試」思維

Markets top out when consensus becomes dangerously uniform and retail euphoria peaks (e.g., extreme long/short ratios and hostility toward bears). Conversely, true bull markets are validated not by good news, but by how strongly prices hold up against bad news.

當市場輿論高度一致、排斥任何看空聲音且多空比極端失衡時，往往是牛市見頂的信號；反之，真正的牛市初期並非靠利好推動，而是靠「利空出盡卻跌不下去」的盤面抗壓性來驗證主力資金的存在。

 Module 2: Eight-Phase Cycle & Triple MA Identification [01:10 - 02:45]

[Concept Explanation] The market cycle is divided into 8 phases: Bear, Late Bear, Bear Rally, Early Bull, Bull, Late Bull, Bull Pullback, and Early Bear. Three daily Moving Averages (MA30, MA120, MA256) objectively define transitions between these phases.

【概念解釋】市場週期細分為「熊市、熊末、熊反、牛初、牛市、牛末、牛調、熊初」八個階段，並透過日線級別的三條移動平均線（MA30、MA120、MA256）客觀界定各階段的轉換邊界。

[Key Steps / Workflow] 1. Bear Market: Price stays below downward-sloping MA30 < MA120 < MA256. 2. Bear Rally: MAs turn upward and price breaks above MA256. 3. Early Bull Confirmation: Price pulls back after a large Bear Rally; if the pullback fails to push price back below the key MA boundary (e.g., MA256), Early Bull is confirmed. 4. Entry on Breakout: If missed during early divergence, enter when price breaks the next key resistance into the main Bull phase.

【關鍵步驟/操作流程】1. 熊市確認：價格長時間位於自上而下空頭排列（MA30 < MA120 < MA256）的三均線下方。2. 熊反啟動：均線開始拐頭向上，且價格反彈突破 MA256。3. 牛初確認：較大幅度的熊反後必有大回調，若回調無法將價格壓回關鍵均線（如 MA256）下方，即確立「牛初」到來。4. 突破入場：若在市場分歧階段未上車，可等待突破下一個關鍵阻力位（牛初轉牛市）時擇機入場。

[Key Formulas / Terms] MA30 / MA120 / MA256 (30/120/256日移動平均線), Bullish Alignment (多頭排列: MA30 > MA120 > MA256), Bearish Alignment (空頭排列), Bad News Stress Test (利空試金石).

【重要公式/專有名詞】日線 MA30 / MA120 / MA256、多頭排列 (Bullish Alignment)、空頭排列 (Bearish Alignment)、利空試金石。

【常見誤區/注意事項】未經回調測試就將「熊反」誤判為「牛市」而盲目追高；或在「牛初」分歧期被利空消息嚇出場，忽略了幣價抗跌背後的資金承接信號。

 Module 4: Macro Liquidity Drivers (Fed, TGA & Catalysts) [03:20 - 04:55]
 模組四：宏觀流動性三大核心驅動因子 [03:20 - 04:55]

[Concept Explanation] Liquidity is the primary engine of large price swings. Long-term macro liquidity depends on three pillars: the Federal Reserve Balance Sheet, the US Treasury General Account (TGA), and major structural market catalysts.

【概念解釋】流動性才是影響市場大級別走勢的最核心變量。長期宏觀層面需重點追蹤三大方向：聯準會資產負債表規模、美國財政部 TGA 帳戶餘額，以及重大市場事件。

[Key Steps / Workflow] 1. Track Fed Balance Sheet: Expansion (buying assets during crises) injects USD and boosts liquidity; contraction (selling assets) drains USD. 2.
