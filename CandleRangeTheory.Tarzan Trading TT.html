Deep Learning Study Notes: Candle Range Theory (CRT)
深度學習筆記：蠟燭區間理論（CRT）實戰解析

1. Executive Summary
1. 核心總結

Candle Range Theory (CRT) simplifies price action analysis by viewing every Higher Timeframe (HTF) candlestick as a complete price range bounded by a CRT High and CRT Low. When price sweeps liquidity beyond one boundary and swiftly rejects back inside the range, it signals an impending move toward the opposite boundary. Internally, this HTF range corresponds to the Lower Timeframe (LTF) Power of Three (PO3) model—comprising Accumulation, Manipulation, and Distribution phases. By waiting for LTF manipulation confirmation, traders can pinpoint precise entries, clear invalidation stop losses, and high-probability targets. Furthermore, filtering CRT setups through key institutional Supply and Demand zones provides crucial confluence, elevating trade selection to A-plus setups while maintaining strict risk management.

蠟燭區間理論（CRT）透過將每根高時間週期（HTF）K線視為由 CRT 高點與 CRT 低點界定的完整價格區間，簡化了價格行為分析。當價格掃蕩某側邊界外的流動性並迅速拒絕、收盤回到區間內時，即預示價格將朝另一側邊界移動。在微觀結構上，高週期區間對應低時間週期（LTF）的三階段模型（Power of Three, PO3），包含吸籌（Accumulation）、操縱（Manipulation）與派發（Distribution）階段。交易者透過等待低週期操縱階段確認，能精準定位進場點、明確的停損失效點及高勝率目標位。此外，結合機構供給區（Supply Zone）與需求區（Demand Zone）進行多重佐證（Confluence），可過濾低品質訊號並鎖定 A 級交易機會，同時配合嚴謹的風險控管以保護資金。

[Power of Three (PO3) / 三階段模型] (00:02:59 - 00:03:15): A Smart Money concept dividing lower timeframe price delivery into three sequential phases: Accumulation (consolidation), Manipulation (false breakout/sweep), and Distribution (expansion/acceleration).

[Confluence / 多重佐證（共振）] (00:06:57 - 00:08:17): The alignment of multiple independent technical factors—specifically HTF Supply/Demand zones, CRT liquidity sweeps, and LTF PO3 structure—to validate A-plus setups.

4. Mental Models & Logic
4. 底層邏輯與思維模型

1. Fractal Timeframe Decomposition (分形時間週期拆解思維): Why: Retail traders view candlesticks as static shapes. How: The speaker deconstructs every HTF candle into an LTF micro-market. By pairing 1H with 15M, or Daily with 1H, you gain macro directional clarity from HTF while executing with surgical LTF precision.

1. Fractal Timeframe Decomposition (分形時間週期拆解思維): 為何有效：散戶常將 K 線視為靜態圖形。如何運作：講者將每根高週期 K 線拆解為低週期的微觀市場。透過「1小時圖配15分鐘圖」或「日線圖配1小時圖」，既能從高週期取得宏觀方向指引，又能利用低週期進行精準進場。

2. Liquidity Inducement & Trap Reversal (流動性誘導與陷阱反轉邏輯): Why: Institutional algorithms require liquidity (stop orders and breakout traders) to fill large positions. How: A break beyond CRT High/Low acts as a trap (Manipulation). Once liquidity is absorbed and price re-enters the range, the path of least resistance shifts directly to the opposing boundary.

2. Liquidity Inducement & Trap Reversal (流動性誘導與陷阱反轉邏輯): 為何有效：機構演算法需要足夠的流動性（停損單與追價單）來成交大額部位。如何運作：突破 CRT 高低點是誘導散戶的陷阱（操縱階段）。一旦流動性被吸收且價格重回區間，最小阻力路徑便直接指向區間的另一端。

3. Probabilistic Confluence Filtering (機率疊加與多重佐證過濾): Why: Standalone patterns fail frequently in noisy market conditions. How: Instead of trading every CRT candle, the speaker overlays institutional Supply/Demand zones as a spatial filter, ensuring trades are only taken where institutional order flow already exists.

3. Probabilistic Confluence Filtering (機率疊加與多重佐證過濾): 為何有效：在雜訊較多的市場中，單一型態容易失效。如何運作：講者不盲目交易每一個 CRT 型態，而是疊加機構供需區作為空間濾網，確保僅在已有機構訂單流痕跡的關鍵位置出手。

Module 1: Anatomy of Candle Range Theory (00:00:00 - 00:01:36)
模組一：蠟燭區間理論的基礎結構 (00:00:00 - 00:01:36)

[Concept Explanation]: Every HTF candlestick encapsulates a complete LTF price range bounded by two critical levels: CRT High and CRT Low.

【概念解釋】：每根高週期 K 線都包含一個完整的低週期價格區間，並由 CRT 高點與 CRT 低點兩個關鍵邊界所界定。

[Key Steps / Workflow]: 1. Select a clear reference candle on the Higher Timeframe (e.g., Daily or 1H).
2. Mark the absolute highest wick/price as CRT High.
3. Mark the absolute lowest wick/price as CRT Low.
4. Observe subsequent price interaction around these two horizontal boundaries.

【關鍵步驟/操作流程】：1. 在高時間週期（如日線或 1 小時線）選定一根明確的基準 K 線。
2. 標記該 K 線最高點為 CRT 高點（CRT High）。
3. 標記該 K 線最低點為 CRT 低點（CRT Low）。
4. 耐心觀察後續價格在這兩條水平邊界附近的互動反應。

[Key Terms]: CRT High (CRT 高點), CRT Low (CRT 低點), Higher Timeframe (HTF / 高時間週期), Lower Timeframe (LTF / 低時間週期).

【重要公式/專有名詞】：CRT High (CRT 高點)、CRT Low (CRT 低點)、Higher Timeframe (HTF / 高時間週期)、Lower Timeframe (LTF / 低時間週期)。

[Pitfalls & Notes]: Do not treat the CRT candle as a directional signal by itself; it merely defines the battlefield boundaries. Never enter immediately upon marking the range.

【常見誤區/注意事項】：切勿將基準 CRT K 線本身直接當作進場方向訊號，它僅用來劃定戰場邊界；標記完區間後絕不可立即進場。

Module 2: Bullish & Bearish Liquidity Sweep Mechanics (00:01:37 - 00:02:58)
模組二：看漲與看跌的流動性掃蕩機制 (00:01:37 - 00:02:58)

[Concept Explanation]: The core edge of CRT relies on false breakouts (liquidity sweeps) followed by immediate rejection back into the established candle range.

【概念解釋】：CRT 的核心優勢在於辨識假突破（流動性掃蕩），並捕捉價格遭拒絕後迅速重回既有區間的動能。

[Key Steps / Workflow]: 1. Bullish Setup: Wait for price to dip below CRT Low (Sell-Side Liquidity Sweep), reject, and close back inside the range -> Target CRT High.
2. Bearish Setup: Wait for price to spike above CRT High (Buy-Side Liquidity Sweep), reject, and close back inside the range -> Target CRT Low.

【關鍵步驟/操作流程】：1. 看漲型態：等待價格跌破 CRT 低點（賣方流動性掃蕩）、遭到拒絕並收盤回到區間內 -> 目標看向 CRT 高點。
2. 看跌型態：等待價格突破 CRT 高點（買方流動性掃蕩）、遭到拒絕並收盤跌回區間內 -> 目標看向 CRT 低點。

[Key Terms]: Sell-Side Liquidity Sweep (賣方流動性掃蕩), Buy-Side Liquidity Sweep (買方流動性掃蕩), Rejection (價格拒絕／假突破收回).

【重要公式/專有名詞】：Sell-Side Liquidity Sweep (賣方流動性掃蕩)、Buy-Side Liquidity Sweep (買方流動性掃蕩)、Rejection (價格拒絕／假突破收回)。

[Pitfalls & Notes]: If a candle breaks beyond the CRT boundary and closes strongly outside without rejecting back inside, it is a valid breakout rather than a CRT sweep—do not fade it.

【常見誤區/注意事項】：若價格突破 CRT 邊界後強勢收盤在區間外側且未收回，這代表真實突破而非 CRT 掃蕩，切勿逆勢操作。

Module 3: Connecting CRT with the Power of Three (00:02:59 - 00:05:10)
模組三：CRT 與三階段模型（Power of Three）的連結 (00:02:59 - 00:05:10)

[Concept Explanation]: An HTF CRT range unfolds internally on the LTF as the Smart Money Power of Three (PO3) cycle: Accumulation, Manipulation, and Distribution.

【概念解釋】：高週期的 CRT 區間在低週期圖表上，會完整呈現聰明錢「三階段模型（PO3）」的演變：吸籌、操縱與派發。

[Key Steps / Workflow]: 1. Identify the CRT range and sweep on the 1H chart (e.g., GBP/JPY example).
2. Drop down to the 15M chart to inspect internal structure.
3. Spot Accumulation (sideways consolidation) and Manipulation (sweep of CRT Low/High).
4. Enter at the close of the 15M candle confirming the return inside the range, placing SL below the manipulation low and TP at the 1H CRT High.

【關鍵步驟/操作流程】：1. 在 1 小時圖上辨識 CRT 區間與流動性掃蕩（如 GBP/JPY 案例）。
2. 切換至 15 分鐘圖檢視內部微觀結構。
3. 確認吸籌階段（橫盤整理）與操縱階段（掃蕩 CRT 高點或低點）。
4. 在 15 分鐘確認收回區間內的 K 線收盤時進場，停損設於操縱極值外側，停利設於 1 小時圖的另一側 CRT 邊界。

[Key Terms]: Power of Three / PO3 (三階段模型), Accumulation (吸籌／累積階段), Manipulation (操縱／假突破階段), Distribution (派發／加速階段).

【重要公式/專有名詞】：Power of Three / PO3 (三階段模型)、Accumulation (吸籌／累積階段)、Manipulation (操縱／假突破階段)、Distribution (派發／加速階段)。

[Pitfalls & Notes]: Entering prematurely during the Manipulation wick before the confirmation candle closes inside the range exposes traders to continued stop hunts.

【常見誤區/注意事項】：在操縱階段的影線形成過程中、確認 K 線尚未收盤回到區間內之前就過早進場，極易暴露在持續獵殺停損的風險中。

【關鍵步驟/操作流程】：1. 在黃金 1 小時圖標記一根強勢看漲 K 線作為 CRT 區間。

[Key Terms]: Buy-Side Liquidity (買方流動性), Short Entry (空單進場), Invalidation High (失效高點).

【常見誤區/注意事項】：不要因為基準 CRT K 線本身是強勢陽線就預設行情必然續漲；一旦 CRT 高點被掃蕩並遭拒絕，短線偏見即轉為看跌。

Module 5: Filtering A-Plus Setups with Supply & Demand Zones (00:06:36 - 00:09:39)

[Concept Explanation]: Combining CRT with higher-timeframe institutional Supply or Demand zones eliminates low-probability trades and creates high-conviction confluence.

【關鍵步驟/操作流程】：1. 在高週期圖表辨識清晰的機構區域（如 NZD/USD 日線的「下跌-盤整-下跌」供給區）。
2. 等待價格回測該區域並在區內形成 CRT 基準 K 線。
3. 確認價格在供給區內向上掃蕩 CRT 高點並收回。
4. 切換至 1 小時圖執行 PO3 做空型態，將停損安全地設於操縱高點與供給區雙重上方。

[Key Terms]: A-Plus Setup (A 級高品質型態), Supply Zone (供給區), Drop-Base-Drop (下跌-盤整-下跌型態), Confluence (多重佐證).

【重要公式/專有名詞】：A-Plus Setup (A 級高品質型態)、Supply Zone (供給區)、Drop-Base-Drop (下跌-盤整-下跌型態)、Confluence (多重佐證)。

[Pitfalls & Notes]: Avoid trading CRT setups formed in 'no-man's-land' (middle of nowhere without HTF structure), as they lack institutional backing.

【常見誤區/注意事項】：避免交易形成於「三不管地帶」（缺乏高週期結構支撐的半空中）的 CRT 型態，因為這類訊號缺乏機構訂單流背書。
