深度學習筆記：精通流動性概念（機械化交易策略）

1. Executive Summary
1. 核心總結

This tutorial deconstructs institutional Liquidity concepts into a repeatable, mechanical trading framework rather than discretionary chart drawings. The core premise is that institutional players ('Big Money') require massive opposing resting orders to fill large positions with minimal slippage. By integrating a top-down multi-timeframe structure (HTF for market phase, MTF for immediate directional bias and POI selection, and LTF for execution), traders can filter high-probability Supply and Demand zones. Specifically, valid zones must either form via a historical Liquidity Sweep (proving institutional involvement) or possess clear Liquidity Inducement (to the left or right) ahead of the Point of Interest (POI) to fuel the next move. Finally, evaluating High vs. Low Resistance Liquidity enables traders to anticipate pullback depth and execute systematically using three distinct mechanical entry models.

本教學將機構「流動性 (Liquidity)」概念拆解為可重複執行的機械化交易框架，摒棄主觀隨意的圖表畫線。其核心前提在於：機構大資金（Big Money）必須仰賴龐大的反向掛單來撮合大額倉位，藉此將滑點（Slippage）降至最低。透過由上而下的多時間週期結構（高時間週期 HTF 判別市場階段、中時間週期 MTF 確立日內方向與興趣點 POI、低時間週期 LTF 執行進場），交易者能精準過濾高勝率供需區（Supply & Demand Zones）。具體而言，高機率區域必須具備歷史「流動性獵取 (Liquidity Sweep)」作為機構介入證據，或在 POI 前方（左側或右側）累積明確的「流動性誘導 (Inducement)」以提供推進動能。最後，透過評估高阻與低阻流動性，交易者可預判回調深度，並運用三種機械化進場模型嚴格執行交易。

 

2. Key Concepts & Definitions (With Timestamps)
2. 知識地圖與核心概念（附時間戳記）

**[00:01:46] Multi-Timeframe (MTF) Hierarchy**: Higher Timeframe (HTF) identifies pro-trend run vs. pullback phases; Medium Timeframe (MTF) defines immediate directional bias and Points of Interest (POIs); Lower Timeframe (LTF) refines execution.

**多時間週期階層架構**：高時間週期 (HTF) 用於判斷順勢推進或回調階段；中時間週期 (MTF) 確立即時方向偏見與興趣點 (POI)；低時間週期 (LTF) 則負責精細化進場執行。

**[00:03:24] Liquidity & Slippage**: Liquidity is the volume of resting supply and demand at each price level. Institutions require massive opposing liquidity to execute large orders without suffering severe slippage.

**流動性與滑點**：流動性是指各價格水平上掛單的供需總量。機構大資金需要龐大的反向流動性來撮合大額訂單，以避免產生嚴重的價格滑點 (Slippage)。

**[00:06:07] Buy-Side Liquidity (BSL) & Sell-Side Liquidity (SSL)**: Buy-Side Liquidity (BSL) rests above structural highs (short stop-losses and breakout buy orders), while Sell-Side Liquidity (SSL) rests below structural lows (long stop-losses and breakdown sell orders).

**買方流動性 (BSL) 與賣方流動性 (SSL)**：買方流動性 (BSL) 位於結構高點上方（空單止損與突破買單），而賣方流動性 (SSL) 則位於結構低點下方（多單止損與跌破賣單）。

**[00:06:21] Liquidity Inducement**: Chart patterns (such as equal highs/lows or minor structure breaks) that persuade retail traders to enter early, thereby generating resting opposing liquidity in front of an institutional POI.

**流動性誘導**：誘使散戶過早進場的圖表型態（如等高/等低點或次級結構突破），藉此在機構興趣點 (POI) 前方累積可供大資金吸收的反向流動性。

**[00:08:52] Liquidity Sweep Zones**: Supply or demand zones created immediately after sweeping a prior high or low, serving as historical proof that institutional capital absorbed opposing orders at that origin.

**流動性獵取區域**：在獵取（掃蕩）前期高點或低點後立即形成的供需區域，作為機構資金曾在此處吸收反向訂單、強勢介入的歷史證據。

**[00:13:33] Left-Side vs. Right-Side Inducement**: Available liquidity located either in the prior impulse leg to the left of a POI or built during the corrective pullback to the right of a POI; if neither exists, the POI itself becomes the liquidity target.

**左側誘導與右側誘導**：位於 POI 左側前波推進腿中的既有流動性，或是在 POI 右側回調過程中新建的流動性；若左右兩側皆無流動性，該 POI 本身就會成為被獵取的目標。

**[00:16:03] Three Mechanical Entry Models**: Model 1: Limit order at refined POI after inducement sweep; Model 2: Trailing candles after liquidity sweep; Model 3: Waiting for an LTF Market Structure Shift (MSS) and entering on the pullback.

**三種機械化進場模型**：模型一：誘導被獵取後在精煉 POI 掛限價單；模型二：流動性獵取後逐根追蹤 K 線進場；模型三：等待低週期市場結構轉變 (MSS) 後於回調時進場。

**[00:18:03] High vs. Low Resistance Liquidity**: Corrective pullbacks leaving multiple un-swept lows/highs create Low Resistance Liquidity (shallow pullbacks), whereas a strong sweep at the swing extreme signals institutional reversal and a deep pullback.

**高阻力與低阻力流動性**：修正性回調若留下多個未被掃蕩的高低點，會形成「低阻力流動性」（預示淺回調）；反之，波段極值若發生強力獵取，則預示機構介入並將引發深度回調。

4. Mental Models & Underlying Logic (Why & How)
4. 底層邏輯與思維模型

1. Order-Matching & Slippage Constraint Model (Why Institutions Hunt Stops)

1. 訂單撮合與滑點限制模型（為何機構必須獵取止損）

Every buy order requires an equal and opposite sell order. Because institutions deploy massive capital, entering in an illiquid area causes severe slippage. Therefore, 'Big Money' engineers or waits for retail stop-losses and breakout orders (Liquidity Pools) to accumulate at obvious highs/lows so they can execute large positions at optimal prices.

每一筆買單都必須有等量的賣單撮合。由於機構部署的資金極為龐大，在缺乏流動性的區域進場會導致嚴重滑點。因此，大資金必須等待或誘導散戶在明顯的高低點累積止損單與突破單（流動性池），藉此在理想價位完成大額建倉。

2. The 'Fuel vs. Target' Zone Validation Logic (How to Avoid Liquidity Traps)

2. 「燃料 vs. 目標」區域驗證邏輯（如何避開流動性陷阱）

Before trading any Point of Interest (POI), ask: 'Where is the available liquidity?' If liquidity exists to the left (prior leg) or to the right (pullback) in front of the POI, that liquidity acts as FUEL for the POI to hold. If NO liquidity exists in front of the POI, the POI itself becomes the TARGET and will likely be swept to fuel a deeper extreme zone.

在交易任何興趣點 (POI) 之前，必須先問：「可用的流動性在哪裡？」若 POI 前方的左側（前波段）或右側（回調中）存在流動性，該流動性將成為支撐 POI 生效的「燃料」；若 POI 前方完全沒有流動性，則該 POI 本身就會成為被獵取的「目標」，價格將貫穿它以觸發更深層的極值區。

3. Confluence Portfolio & Mechanical Rule-Set (Eliminating Discretionary Bias)

3. 多重共振組合與機械化規則集（消除主觀偏見）

Liquidity is never traded in isolation; it acts as a timing and refinement filter layered on top of Market Structure (Direction) and Supply/Demand (Location). Traders must define strict mechanical rules (e.g., exact proximity of inducement, valid timeframes, invalidation criteria) because consistent actions yield consistent results.

流動性絕不能單獨使用，它必須疊加在「市場結構（決定方向）」與「供需區域（決定位置）」之上，作為進場時機與區域精煉的過濾器。交易者必須制定嚴格的機械化規則（如誘導距離 POI 的精確範圍、適用週期與失效條件），因為唯有「一致的行動」才能帶來「一致的結果」。

Module 1: Top-Down Multi-Timeframe Strategy Framework [00:01:15 - 00:03:24]

模組一：由上而下的多時間週期策略框架 [00:01:15 - 00:03:24]

【Concept】A three-tier timeframe system that separates market phase identification, directional bias/zone selection, and execution timing to align trades with institutional order flow.

【概念解釋】透過三層時間週期系統，將「市場階段判別」、「方向偏見與區域篩選」以及「進場時機精煉」徹底分離，確保交易順應機構資金流向。

【Key Steps】1) Check Higher Timeframe (HTF) to determine if price is in a pro-trend run or a pullback phase. 2) Use Medium Timeframe (MTF, e.g., M15) to confirm immediate directional bias and mark Supply/Demand POIs. 3) Drop to Lower Timeframe (LTF, e.g., M5) to apply liquidity rules for refined entry.

【關鍵步驟】1) 觀察高時間週期 (HTF)，判斷當前處於順勢推進階段或回調階段。2) 利用中時間週期 (MTF，如 M15) 確認即時方向偏見並標記供需 POI。3) 切換至低時間週期 (LTF，如 M5) 運用流動性規則精煉進場點。

【Key Terms】Pro-Trend Run (順勢推進), Pullback Phase (回調階段), Point of Interest / POI (興趣點), Directional Bias (方向偏見).

【重要專有名詞】Pro-Trend Run（順勢推進階段）、Pullback Phase（回調階段）、Point of Interest / POI（興趣點/關鍵供需區）、Directional Bias（方向偏見）。

【Common Pitfalls】Drawing liquidity lines randomly across every swing on a single timeframe without anchoring to HTF/MTF market structure.

【常見誤區】未先錨定高、中時間週期的市場結構，就在單一圖表上隨意畫滿各種流動性線條，導致分析充滿主觀性而無法執行。

Module 2: Liquidity Mechanics & Inducement Generation [00:03:24 - 00:08:21]

模組二：流動性本質與誘導生成機制 [00:03:24 - 00:08:21]

【Concept】Liquidity represents resting orders at specific price levels. Inducement occurs when deceptive price patterns lure retail traders into placing stops and breakout orders right where institutions need opposing liquidity.

【概念解釋】流動性代表特定價位上的掛單量。當具欺騙性的價格型態誘使散戶進場，並將止損單與突破單集中在機構需要反向撮合的位置時，即形成「流動性誘導」。

【Key Steps】1) Identify structural highs (Buy-Side Liquidity / BSL) and structural lows (Sell-Side Liquidity / SSL). 2) Spot inducement patterns such as equal highs/lows (double tops/bottoms) or minor structure breaks forming directly in front of your POI. 3) Wait for institutions to sweep that inducement before considering an entry.

【關鍵步驟】1) 辨識結構高點（買方流動性 BSL）與結構低點（賣方流動性 SSL）。2) 尋找在 POI 前方形成的誘導型態，例如等高/等低點（雙頂/雙底）或次級結構突破。3) 等待機構掃蕩該誘導流動性後再考慮進場。

【Key Terms】Slippage (滑點), Buy-Side Liquidity / BSL (買方流動性), Sell-Side Liquidity / SSL (賣方流動性), Inducement (流動性誘導).

【重要專有名詞】Slippage（滑點）、Buy-Side Liquidity / BSL（買方流動性）、Sell-Side Liquidity / SSL（賣方流動性）、Inducement（流動性誘導）。

【Common Pitfalls】Buying support at a double bottom or selling resistance at a double top directly in front of an unmitigated institutional POI, becoming the liquidity yourself.

【常見誤區】在尚未測試的機構 POI 前方，直接於雙底支撐做多或雙頂阻力做空，結果讓自己的止損單成為機構建倉的流動性燃料。

Module 3: Historical Validation via Liquidity Sweep Zones [00:08:21 - 00:11:10]

模組三：透過「流動性獵取區」驗證歷史機構介入 [00:08:21 - 00:11:10]

【Concept】Looking backward in price action, a Supply or Demand zone that swept prior liquidity during its creation provides strong evidence of institutional backing.

【概念解釋】從歷史價格行為回顧，若某個供給區或需求區在「誕生過程中」曾掃蕩過前期高/低點的流動性，這便是有力的證據，證明該區域背後有機構資金撐腰。

【Key Steps】1) Inspect candidate MTF/LTF Supply or Demand zones. 2) Verify whether the candle formation that created the zone first took out a prior swing low (for demand) or swing high (for supply). 3) Prioritize sweep-backed zones over non-sweep zones when selecting POIs.

【關鍵步驟】1) 檢視候選的 MTF/LTF 供給區或需求區。2) 驗證形成該區域的 K 線結構是否先獵取了前期波段低點（需求區）或波段高點（供給區）。3) 篩選 POI 時，優先選擇具備流動性獵取背景的區域。

【Key Terms】Liquidity Sweep Zone (流動性獵取區域), Strong Low / Strong High (強勢低點/強勢高點), Supply-to-Demand Flip (供需轉換).

【重要專有名詞】Liquidity Sweep Zone（流動性獵取區域）、Strong Low / Strong High（強勢低點/強勢高點）、Supply-to-Demand Flip（供需轉換）。

【Common Pitfalls】Treating all order blocks or supply/demand zones as equal without checking whether they actually absorbed opposing liquidity upon creation.

【常見誤區】將所有訂單塊或供需區域一視同仁，未檢查該區域在形成時是否真正吸收了反向流動性。

Module 4: Future Filtering — Left vs. Right Inducement Rules [00:11:10 - 00:15:53]

模組四：未來區域過濾——左側與右側誘導判別法則 [00:11:10 - 00:15:53]

【Concept】Looking forward, a POI requires resting liquidity in front of it to hold. This liquidity must exist either in the prior impulse leg (Left) or be constructed during the pullback (Right).

【概念解釋】展望未來走勢，一個 POI 要能成功支撐或壓制價格，其前方必須有可用流動性。這些流動性必須存在於前波推進腿（左側）中，或是在回調過程（右側）中建立。

【Key Steps】1) Check the impulse leg to the left of your POI for structural highs/lows. 2) If the push was a straight line (no left liquidity), wait for the pullback on the right to build structural inducement (e.g., equal highs/lows or minor BOS) before price reaches the POI. 3) If neither left nor right liquidity exists, skip the first POI and anticipate it being swept into the Extreme POI.

【關鍵步驟】1) 檢查 POI 左側的推進腿是否留有結構高低點。2) 若前波為直線推進（無左側流動性），則必須等待右側回調過程中建立結構性誘導（如等高低點或次級突破）後再觸及 POI。3) 若左右兩側皆無流動性，果斷放棄第一個 POI，預判它將被掃蕩並轉向極值 POI (Extreme POI)。

【Key Terms】Straight Push (直線推進/無流動性腿), Extreme POI (極值興趣點), Mechanical Proximity Rule (機械化距離規則).

【重要專有名詞】Straight Push（直線推進腿）、Extreme POI（極值興趣點）、Mechanical Proximity Rule（機械化距離規則）。

【Common Pitfalls】Entering on the first unmitigated zone after a straight impulsive move when no inducement has been built, getting stopped out as price sweeps the zone.

【常見誤區】在價格直線推進後、完全沒有建立任何誘導的情況下，盲目在第一個供需區掛單進場，結果直接成為機構掃損的犧牲品。

Module 5: Three Mechanical Entry Models [00:15:53 - 00:18:03]

模組五：三種機械化進場模型 [00:15:53 - 00:18:03]

【Concept】Three standardized execution protocols balancing Risk-to-Reward (R:R) against confirmation certainty once price sweeps inducement into a valid POI.

【概念解釋】當價格掃蕩誘導並進入有效 POI 後，提供三種標準化執行協議，讓交易者在「高盈虧比 (R:R)」與「高確認度」之間做出機械化抉擇。

【Key Steps】Model 1 (Aggressive Limit): Place a limit order directly on the refined POI once inducement is established, maximizing R:R. Model 2 (Candle Trailing): Wait for the inducement sweep, then trail candle highs/lows mechanically to enter as momentum shifts. Model 3 (Conservative MSS): Wait for a clear LTF Market Structure Shift (MSS) after the sweep, then enter on the pullback (using extreme demand/supply or Fibonacci 0.618).

【關鍵步驟】模型一（激進限價單）：確認誘導存在後，直接在精煉 POI 掛限價單，追求最大盈虧比。模型二（K 線追蹤進場）：等待誘導被掃蕩後，按機械規則逐根追蹤 K 線高低點突破進場。模型三（保守結構轉變）：等待掃蕩後出現明確的 LTF 市場結構轉變 (MSS)，再於回調時（如極值區或斐波那契 0.618）進場。

【Key Terms】Risk-to-Reward Ratio / R:R (盈虧比), Candle Trailing (K線追蹤), Market Structure Shift / MSS (市場結構轉變), Fibonacci Retracement (斐波那契回撤).

【重要專有名詞】Risk-to-Reward Ratio / R:R（盈虧比）、Candle Trailing（K線追蹤）、Market Structure Shift / MSS（市場結構轉變）、Fibonacci Retracement（斐波那契回撤）。

【Common Pitfalls】Switching impulsively between entry models mid-session without backtesting which model aligns best with your personal psychology and invalidation rules.

【常見誤區】未經回測驗證哪種模型最契合自身心理素質與失效規則，就在盤中隨意切換進場模型。

Module 6: High vs. Low Resistance Liquidity & Advanced Cycle [00:18:03 - 00:24:15]

模組六：高低阻力流動性與進階流動性循環 [00:18:03 - 00:24:15]

【Concept】Evaluating how a pullback forms reveals whether institutions have fueled a deep counter-trend move or left easy 'low-resistance' targets for rapid trend continuation.

【概念解釋】評估回調結構的形成方式，可揭示機構是否已為深度逆勢回調注入動能，抑或是留下了易於攻破的「低阻力」目標以延續原趨勢。

【Key Steps】1) If a pullback leaves multiple corrective highs/lows without sweeping the swing extreme, classify it as Low Resistance Liquidity—expect a shallow pullback (discount/less well-priced POI) and rapid continuation. 2) If the swing extreme experiences a strong liquidity sweep followed by structure break, expect a deep pullback into premium/discount extremes. 3) Combine all phases into the full cycle: Inducement -> Sweep into Extreme POI -> MSS -> Target resting double tops/bottoms.

【關鍵步驟】1) 若回調留下多個修正性高低點且未掃蕩波段極值，判定為「低阻力流動性」——預期淺回調（折價區 POI）並快速延續趨勢。2) 若波段極值發生強力流動性獵取並伴隨結構突破，則預期將出現深入溢價/折價極值區的深回調。3) 串聯完整循環：誘導形成 -> 獵取進入極值 POI -> 結構轉變 -> 瞄準對向雙頂/雙底流動性目標。

【Key Terms】Low Resistance Liquidity (低阻力流動性), High Resistance Liquidity (高阻力流動性), Premium & Discount Zones (溢價區與折價區).

【重要專有名詞】Low Resistance Liquidity（低阻力流動性）、High Resistance Liquidity（高阻力流動性）、Premium & Discount Zones（溢價區與折價區）。

【Common Pitfalls】Waiting passively for deep premium/discount prices during a low-resistance corrective pullback, missing the entire continuation move.

【常見誤區】在低阻力修正性回調中死等深度的溢價/折價極值區，導致完全錯過順勢延續行情。

6. Practical Action Items
6. 實操行動清單

1. Audit Your Chart Template to Enforce a 3-Timeframe Hierarchy

1. 審視並規範你的圖表模板，嚴格執行三時間週期階層

Configure your trading workspace into three fixed timeframes (e.g., H4 for Market Phase, M15 for Directional Bias & POIs, M5/M1 for Liquidity Execution) and forbid taking trades without HTF/MTF alignment.

將交易介面固定為三個時間週期（例如：H4 判別市場階段、M15 確立方向與 POI、M5/M1 執行流動性進場），嚴禁在未獲得高、中週期共振的情況下單憑低週期訊號下單。

2. Apply the 'Left-or-Right Inducement' Checklist Before Marking Any POI

2. 標記任何 POI 前，強制執行「左側或右側誘導」檢查清單

Before setting a limit order or alert on any Supply/Demand zone, verify that there is either a clear structural swing in the left impulse leg or inducement built on the right pullback; if neither exists, shift your alert to the Extreme POI behind it.

在任何供需區設定掛單或警報前，先確認左側推進腿留有結構高低點，或右側回調已建立誘導；若兩者皆無，立即將警報移至其後方的極值 POI (Extreme POI)。

3. Filter Historical Zones Using the 'Sweep-Origin' Rule

3. 運用「獵取起源」法則過濾歷史供需區域

Review your last 20 marked POIs and tag only those whose creation candles swept a prior swing high or low. Make 'liquidity sweep origin' a mandatory criterion for high-probability zones.

回顧過去標記的 20 個 POI，僅保留那些「誕生 K 線曾掃蕩過前期波段高低點」的區域，將「具備流動性獵取起源」列為篩選高勝率區域的硬性標準。

4. Select and Backtest One Single Entry Model for 50 Trades

4. 選定單一進場模型並進行 50 筆歷史回測

Choose either Model 1 (Limit Order), Model 2 (Candle Trailing), or Model 3 (LTF MSS + Pullback) based on your schedule and psychology, and backtest 50 consecutive setups with fixed mechanical invalidation rules.

根據你的看盤時間與心理承受度，從模型一（限價單）、模型二（K 線追蹤）或模型三（LTF 結構轉變回調）中擇一，並以固定且機械化的失效規則連續回測 50 筆交易。

5. Tag Pullbacks as 'Low Resistance' vs. 'Extreme Sweep' to Calibrate Targets

5. 標記回調類型為「低阻力」或「極值獵取」以校準進場深度

During live market analysis, label corrective pullbacks leaving un-swept highs/lows as Low Resistance Liquidity (favoring shallower POIs) versus pullbacks triggered by an extreme sweep (favoring deeper premium/discount POIs).

在日常盤面分析中，將留下未掃蕩高低點的修正性回調標記為「低阻力流動性」（優先關注淺層 POI），而由極值強力掃蕩引發的回調則標記為深回調（耐心等待深層溢價/折價區 POI）。
