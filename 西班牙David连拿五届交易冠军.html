4. Underlying Logic & Mental Models
4. 底層邏輯與思維模型

1. Multi-Timeframe Resonance (Top-Down Spatial & Temporal Filtering): David separates 'Where to trade' from 'When to trade'. Higher timeframes (Daily/1H) filter out market noise to identify institutional liquidity walls (Support/Resistance), while micro timeframes (30s/1m/5m) pinpoint exact momentum exhaustion, shrinking stop-loss distances to just a few ticks while aiming for macro-level retracement targets.
1. 多週期共振模型（空間與時間的雙重過濾）：David 將「在哪裡交易」與「何時交易」徹底分離。大週期（日線、1 小時圖）負責過濾雜訊並鎖定機構流動性密集的支撐與阻力區；小週期（30 秒、1 分鐘、5 分鐘圖）則用於精準捕捉微觀結構的動能衰竭，從而實現「微觀極窄止損、宏觀波段止盈」的高盈虧比結構。

2. Asymmetric Risk & Conviction-Weighted Sizing: Instead of fixed lot sizing, David scales risk dynamically based on multi-factor confluence. MACD divergence acts as the mandatory binary gatekeeper (1 lot), while independent confirmations from RSI (momentum) and Volume (participation) justify adding a 2nd and 3rd lot. Higher statistical probability directly translates into larger exposure.
2. 非對稱風險與信念加權倉位模型（Conviction-Weighted Sizing）：他摒棄固定手數，改以「多維度指標共振」動態調整風險敞口。MACD 背離是具備一票否決權的基礎門檻（下 1 手），當不同維度的指標——RSI（價格動能）與成交量（資金參與度）——同步背離時，勝率顯著提升，才依序加碼至第 2 手與第 3 手。

3. Momentum Exhaustion & Mean Reversion Physics: When price pushes to a new extreme without corresponding expansion in MACD, RSI, or Volume, the move is driven by thin liquidity or stop-hunting rather than genuine institutional accumulation. Once exhausted, price naturally reverts toward the 38.2% to 66% (two-thirds) equilibrium zone of the prior impulse wave.
3. 動能衰竭與均值回歸物理模型：當價格創新高或新低，但 MACD、RSI 與成交量均未跟隨時，代表推升價格的並非真實的機構買盤，而是流動性稀薄下的慣性衝刺或掃損行為。一旦動能耗盡，價格必然向前期推動浪的 38.2% 至 66%（三分之二）均衡區間進行均值回歸。

4. Anti-Greed & Defensive Capital Preservation: David treats trading as a probability business rather than a lottery. By moving stops to breakeven (plus commission buffer) immediately upon favorable movement and taking partial/full profits at 38.2% before trailing to 66%, he eliminates the psychological trap of turning winning trades into losers.
4. 反貪婪與防禦性保本思維：David 將交易視為機率經營而非賭博。透過「獲利後立即推保本（預留手續費緩衝）」以及「先拿近端 38.2% 利潤、走順再博 66% 遠端目標」的機制，徹底杜絕貪婪心態，防止帳面浮盈轉為虧損。

Module 1: Multi-Timeframe Positioning & Divergence Foundation [00:00:49 - 00:01:47]
模組一：大週期定位與背離核心判定 [00:00:49 - 00:01:47]

【Concept Explanation】 MACD divergence is the absolute prerequisite of the strategy. Price making a higher high while the MACD fast line makes a lower high signals a Bearish Divergence (short setup); price making a lower low while the MACD fast line makes a higher low signals a Bullish Divergence (long setup).
【概念解釋】 MACD 背離是整套策略的絕對前提。價格創新高但 MACD 快線高點低於前高，為「頂背離」（尋找做空機會）；價格創新低但 MACD 快線低點高於前低，為「底背離」（尋找做多機會）。

【Key Steps & Workflow】 1) Open Daily and 1-Hour charts to mark major Support and Resistance levels. 2) Wait patiently for price to reach these zones—never place blind limit orders ahead of time. 3) Drop down to 30-second, 1-minute, or 5-minute charts to observe if MACD forms a divergence at the key level.
【關鍵步驟與操作流程】 1) 先在日線圖與 1 小時圖標註重要支撐與阻力位。2) 耐心等待價格觸及這些關鍵區域，絕不提前掛限價單盲目接刀或摸頂。3) 價格到位後，切換至 30 秒、1 分鐘或 5 分鐘小週期圖表，觀察是否出現 MACD 背離。

【Formulas & Key Terms】 MACD Default Parameters: Fast EMA = 12, Slow EMA = 26, Signal Line = 9. Higher Timeframe (HTF: Daily/1H) for structure; Lower Timeframe (LTF: 30s/1m/5m) for execution.
【重要公式與專有名詞】 MACD 預設參數：快線 12、慢線 26、訊號線 9。大週期（HTF：日線/1小時）定結構位置；小週期（LTF：30秒/1分/5分）定進場時機。

【Common Pitfalls & Warnings】 Never trade based on RSI overbought/oversold crossovers or MACD golden/death crosses alone. Without MACD divergence, even perfect-looking RSI and Volume signals must be skipped.
【常見誤區與注意事項】 嚴禁單獨依據 RSI 超買超賣區轉折或 MACD 金叉/死叉下單。只要沒有出現 MACD 背離，即便 RSI 與成交量訊號再完美也必須放棄。

Module 2: Three-Factor Confluence & Position Sizing [00:01:47 - 00:02:23]
模組二：三重指標共振與階梯式倉位管理 [00:01:47 - 00:02:23]

【Concept Explanation】 Position size is dynamically determined by the number of diverging indicators. MACD divergence unlocks the base 1 lot; each additional divergence from RSI or Volume adds 1 more lot, up to a standard maximum of 3 lots.
【概念解釋】 倉位大小由背離指標的共振數量動態決定。MACD 出現背離先下基礎 1 手；若 RSI 與成交量也同步出現背離，每多一項背離便多加 1 手，標準配置最多下 3 手。

【Key Steps & Workflow】 1) Confirm MACD divergence -> 1 lot. 2) Check RSI (14): if price makes a new extreme but RSI fails to exceed its prior extreme (especially from >70 overbought or <30 oversold zones) -> +1 lot. 3) Check Volume: if the new extreme candle has lower volume than the prior extreme candle -> +1 lot.
【關鍵步驟與操作流程】 1) 確認 MACD 背離 → 基礎 1 手。2) 檢查 RSI（參數 14）：若價格創新極值但 RSI 未同步創新極值（尤其在 70 以上超買區或 30 以下超賣區轉折）→ 加 1 手。3) 檢查成交量：若創新極值該根 K 線的成交量小於前一個極值點成交量 → 再加 1 手。

【Formulas & Key Terms】 Position Sizing Rule: Total Lots = 1 (MACD) + 1 (if RSI diverges) + 1 (if Volume diverges). RSI (14) Thresholds: >70 Overbought, <30 Oversold.
【重要公式與專有名詞】 倉位計算公式：總手數 = 1（MACD 背離必備）+ 1（若 RSI 背離）+ 1（若成交量背離）。RSI（14）閾值：70 以上超買，30 以下超賣。

【Common Pitfalls & Warnings】 Do NOT compare pre-market volume with regular market open volume, as the first hour after market open naturally has massive volume spikes that distort volume divergence readings.
【常見誤區與注意事項】 切勿將盤前時段的成交量與美股開盤後的成交量進行對比，因為開盤第一個小時的成交量本來就極大，直接比較會導致成交量背離失真。

Module 3: Micro Fibonacci Entry & Ultra-Tight Stop-Loss [00:02:23 - 00:03:09]
模組三：微觀斐波那契精準進場與極窄止損 [00:02:23 - 00:03:09]

【Concept Explanation】 Instead of guessing the exact top or bottom, David uses a small Fibonacci retracement on the LTF divergence swing to enter on confirmed momentum reversal (23.6% breakout) while placing the stop-loss just 1 tick beyond the extreme.
【概念解釋】 David 不去猜測絕對的頂點或底點，而是利用小週期背離波段的「微觀斐波那契回撤」，在價格確認掉頭突破 23.6% 時掛單進場，並將止損緊貼於極值外側僅 1 跳（Tick）的位置。

【Key Steps & Workflow】 For Shorts: Draw Fibonacci from the lowest trough between the two divergent highs (100%) to the new highest peak (0%). Place a sell stop order 1 tick below the 23.6% line, and set stop-loss 1 tick above the 0% peak. Reverse the process for Longs (from highest peak between two lows to the new lowest trough).
【關鍵步驟與操作流程】 做空步驟：從兩個背離高點之間的「最低點（100%）」拉向最新的「最高點（0%）」。在 23.6% 線下方 1 跳預掛空單（價格觸及自動成交），止損設於 0% 新高上方 1 跳。做多則完全反向操作（從兩低點間的最高點拉向新低點）。

【Formulas & Key Terms】 Tick (最小跳動單位): For Nasdaq Futures (NQ), 1 tick = 0.25 points. Entry = 23.6% level ± 1 tick; Stop-Loss = 0% extreme ± 1 tick (typically only a few to a dozen points).
【重要公式與專有名詞】 Tick（最小跳動點位）：以納斯達克期貨（NQ）為例，1 跳為 0.25 點。進場點 = 23.6% 位置 ± 1 跳；止損點 = 0% 極值位置 ± 1 跳（通常僅幾個點到十幾個點）。

【Common Pitfalls & Warnings】 If price makes a new high/low before your 23.6% entry order fills, cancel the order immediately and redraw Fibonacci to the new extreme. Even if stopped out, if divergence remains intact, redraw and re-enter.
【常見誤區與注意事項】 若進場掛單尚未成交前價格又創新高（或新低），必須立即撤單並以新極值重新拉斐波那契。即使止損被打掉，只要背離結構仍在，就重新拉線再次進場。

Module 4: Macro Fibonacci Two-Tier Targets & Breakeven Management [00:03:10 - 00:04:08]
模組四：宏觀斐波那契兩檔止盈與保本風控 [00:03:10 - 00:04:08]

【Concept Explanation】 While entry uses a micro swing, profit targets are projected across the entire prior impulse wave using a second, macro Fibonacci retracement. Stop-loss is moved to breakeven only after the trade moves into profit.
【概念解釋】 進場看的是小週期的微觀波段，但止盈目標必須放大格局，透過第二條「宏觀斐波那契」測量整波前期趨勢的回撤幅度。止損則嚴格規定「只在獲利後才挪動至保本」。

【Key Steps & Workflow】 1) Draw Macro Fibonacci from the start of the entire prior trend wave to the extreme high/low. 2) Set Tier 1 Target at 38.2% (beginners exit 100% here; experienced traders close majority and leave 1 lot running). 3) Set Tier 2 Target at 66% (2/3 level, manually added). Place take-profit orders 1 tick ahead of the line. 4) Once profitable, move stop-loss to entry price + 1 to 1.5 points buffer to cover commissions.
