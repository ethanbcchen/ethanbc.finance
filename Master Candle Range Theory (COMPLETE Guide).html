1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

1. 核心總結

 

Most retail traders fail because they memorize superficial candlestick patterns and treat every bar equally. Candlestick Range Theory (CRT) shifts the perspective from static shapes to institutional Order Flow and Liquidity Boundaries. Every candlestick establishes a governing price range defined by its High and Low. Rather than applying CRT to every random bar, traders must isolate specific CRT candles forming at Higher Time Frame Points of Interest (HTF POI), Premium/Discount zones, pullback endings, or Liquidity Zones. The core mechanism relies on the Accumulation, Manipulation, and Distribution (AMD) cycle: waiting patiently until price sweeps one side of the CRT range (Manipulation) and rejects back inside, signaling a Liquidity Sweep. Traders then drop to a Lower Time Frame (LTF) to execute either an Aggressive Entry (immediately upon V-shaped rejection) or a Conservative Entry (waiting for retracement into an Imbalance/FVG or Liquidation Candle zone), placing stop-losses strictly beyond the Liquidation Candle to target the opposing range boundary. Ultimately, CRT is only effective when paired with Daily Bias, Market Mechanics, and strict A+ setup selection.

大多數散戶交易者之所以持續虧損，是因為過度死記硬背表面的 K 線型態，並將每根 K 線視為同等重要。K 線區間理論（Candlestick Range Theory, CRT）將交易視角從靜態圖形轉向機構訂單流（Order Flow）與流動性邊界（Liquidity Boundaries）。每根 K 線的高點與低點皆構成一個主導價格運行的區間，但實戰中絕不能盲目套用，而必須鎖定出現在高時區關注區域（HTF POI）、溢價與折價區、回調末端或流動性區域的關鍵 CRT K 線。其核心運作遵循「累積、操盤、派發（AMD）」週期：交易者需耐心等待價格獵取並掃蕩 CRT 區間的某一側（操盤階段），隨後迅速收回區間內部形成有效流動性掃蕩（Liquidity Sweep）。確認掃蕩後，切換至低時區（LTF）執行「激進進場」（V 型反轉確認後立即進場）或「保守進場」（等待價格回調填補不平衡缺口 FVG 或清算 K 線區域），並將止損嚴格設於清算 K 線極值之外，目標直指區間對向端點。最終，唯有結合每日偏向（Daily Bias）、市場機械結構（Market Mechanics）與嚴格篩選 A+ 級別交易機會，CRT 才能發揮最大威力。

4. MENTAL MODELS & UNDERLYING LOGIC

4. 底層邏輯與思維模型

1. Asymmetric Information & Institutional Liquidity Engineering (Why): Large institutions cannot fill massive orders without counterparty liquidity. They deliberately engineer false breakouts beyond obvious candlestick highs/lows (Manipulation) to trigger retail stop-losses and breakout orders, harvesting liquidity before driving price toward the true objective (Distribution).

1. 資訊不對稱與機構流動性工程（為何如此運作）： 大型機構若沒有足夠的對手盤流動性，便無法成交巨額訂單。因此，機構會刻意製造突破 K 線高低點的假動作（操盤階段），觸發散戶的止損單與追價突破單，在掠奪充足流動性後才朝真實目標發動趨勢行情（派發階段）。

2. Contextual Filtering Over Universal Pattern Recognition (How to Filter): Because every timeframe contains infinite candles, applying CRT indiscriminately leads to analysis paralysis. The speaker uses a top-down filter: Location (HTF POI) comes first, Range Identification (CRT candle) comes second, and Trigger (LTF sweep) comes third.

2. 情境過濾優於全圖型態辨識（如何過濾雜訊）： 由於每個時區都有無數根 K 線，無差別套用 CRT 只會導致分析癱瘓。講者採用「自上而下」的過濾漏斗：第一層先看位置（高時區關注區域 POI），第二層才標記區間（CRT 母線），第三層最後等待觸發（低時區掃蕩）。

3. Timeframe Fractality & Structural Alignment (How to Execute): A single HTF wick rejection is actually a complete liquidity sweep and market structure shift on the LTF. By defining the battlefield on the HTF (e.g., 15m/1H/4H) and executing with microscopic precision on the LTF (e.g., 5m/1m), traders achieve tight stop-losses and high Risk-to-Reward ratios.

3. 時區分形與結構對齊（如何精準執行）： 高時區的一根長影線拒絕，在低時區實際上是一個完整的流動性掃蕩與結構反轉。透過在高時區（如 15m/1H/4H）劃定戰場邊界，並在低時區（如 5m/1m）進行顯微鏡級別的精準進場，交易者能實現極小止損與高風報酬比。

4. Invalidation-Based Risk Placement & Data-Driven Adaptation (How to Manage Risk): Stop-losses are never placed arbitrarily; they are anchored beyond the Liquidation Candle because breaking that extreme proves the sweep failed. Furthermore, choosing between aggressive and conservative entries is dictated strictly by backtested statistical data rather than emotion.

4. 基於邏輯失效點的風控與數據驅動（如何管理風險）： 止損絕非隨意設定，而是精準錨定在「清算 K 線」極值之外，因為一旦突破該點位即證明掃蕩邏輯失效。此外，選擇激進或保守模型完全取決於歷史回測的統計數據，而非主觀情緒。

模組五：激進型與保守型雙軌進場模型詳解 [00:11:58 – 00:16:48]

• [Concept Explanation] Once an LTF liquidity sweep is confirmed, traders can choose between two execution models tailored to their risk tolerance: immediate entry upon confirmation (Aggressive) or waiting for a discounted pullback into an LTF POI (Conservative).

【概念解釋】一旦低時區確認完成流動性掃蕩，交易者可根據自身風險承受度選擇兩種執行模型：確認瞬間立即進場（激進型），或等待價格回調至低時區關注區域再掛單（保守型）。

• [Key Steps / Workflow] • Aggressive Model: Spot V-shaped sweep + strong displacement candle -> Enter immediately at market -> Target opposing CRT boundary.
• Conservative Model: Wait for price to retrace into either (a) the Imbalance / Fair Value Gap (FVG) created by the displacement move, or (b) the extreme Liquidation Candle Supply/Demand zone -> Enter upon mitigation -> Target opposing boundary or 3R.

【關鍵步驟 / 操作流程】• 激進型模型：觀察到 V 型掃蕩＋強勢反向推進 K 線 → 立即市價進場 → 目標看向 CRT 對向邊界。
• 保守型模型：等待價格二次回調至 (a) 推進波段創造的不平衡缺口（Imbalance / FVG），或 (b) 極端清算 K 線供需區 → 價格回補觸及時進場 → 目標看向對向邊界或 3R。

• [Key Terms & Formulas] Imbalance / Fair Value Gap (FVG) = Gap between Candle 1 High and Candle 3 Low; Mitigation (price returning to fill an imbalance/zone); Risk-to-Reward (3R).

【重要公式 / 專有名詞】Imbalance / FVG（不平衡缺口 = 第 1 根 K 線高點與第 3 根 K 線低點間的價差）；Mitigation（價格回測填補缺口/訂單區）；風報酬比（3R）。

• [Common Pitfalls / Warnings] Entering at an FVG can sometimes be slightly aggressive if price decides to pull back deeper into the extreme Liquidation Candle zone; conversely, waiting for the extreme zone may occasionally result in missed trades.

【常見誤區 / 注意事項】在 FVG 掛單有時仍偏激進，價格可能進行更深度回調觸及極端清算 K 線區域；反之，死守極端區域則偶爾會錯失直接起飛的行情。
