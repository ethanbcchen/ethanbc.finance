1. Executive Summary

1. 核心總結 (Executive Summary)

This tutorial introduces two high-win-rate quantitative trend-following strategies built on TradingView using an adaptive composite oscillator (Multi Oscillator Adaptive Kernel, abbreviated as MAC in the transcript) that merges Stochastic RSI (隨機強弱指標), RSI (相對強弱指標), MFI (資金流量指標), and CCI (順勢指標) to filter market noise and sideways consolidation.

本教學影片介紹兩套建構於 TradingView 的高勝率順勢交易策略，核心採用自適應複合震盪指標（Multi Oscillator Adaptive Kernel，逐字稿簡稱 MAC），該指標融合了 Stoch RSI（隨機強弱指標）、RSI（相對強弱指標）、MFI（資金流量指標）與 CCI（順勢指標），能多重過濾震盪行情並消除走勢噪音。

In Strategy 1, setting all four MAC parameters to 200 establishes macro trend direction (blue above midline for bullish, red below midline for bearish), while the Multi Oscillator Adaptive Oscillator (AEO) provides precise momentum crossover entry triggers on higher timeframes such as the BTC 4-hour chart.

策略一將 MAC 四項參數均設為 200 以界定大週期趨勢方向（中線上方藍色為多頭、下方紅色為空頭），並搭配趨勢波動指標（AEO）的雙線交叉作為精準進場訊號，特別適用於比特幣（BTC）4 小時級別圖表。

In Strategy 2, tuning the MAC parameters to 144 pairs with the Demigod MACD Ultimate (半神 MACD) indicator on the 1-hour (or 15-minute+) timeframe, utilizing histogram divergence (bullish/bearish divergence) aligned with the macro trend to pinpoint high-probability entries.

策略二則將 MAC 參數調整為 144，結合「半神 MACD（Demigod MACD Ultimate）」能量柱背離（頂背離與底背離）捕捉順勢啟動點，適用於 1 小時及 15 分鐘以上週期。

Finally, the presenter emphasizes strict risk management—targeting an initial 1:1.5 risk-to-reward ratio, scaling out 50% at Take Profit 1 while moving Stop Loss to breakeven, and backtesting at least 100 trades per asset and timeframe before live execution.

最後，講者強調嚴格的風控與分批止盈紀律：初始盈虧比設定為 1:1.5，達標後平倉一半並將止損移至保本位，且實盤前必須針對特定交易標的與週期完成至少 100 次歷史回測驗證。

4. Mental Models & Underlying Logic (Why & How)

4. 底層邏輯與思維模型 (Mental Models & Logic)

·        1. Multi-Factor Consensus Filtering (多因子共識過濾模型 — Why & How): Single momentum oscillators frequently fail in choppy markets due to false breakouts. By synthesizing four distinct dimensions—Stoch RSI (momentum velocity), RSI (price strength), MFI (volume-weighted money flow), and CCI (statistical price deviation)—the system requires multi-dimensional agreement before signaling a trend, drastically reducing false positives.

1. 多因子共識過濾模型（Why & How）：單一震盪指標在盤整區間極易產生假突破訊號。講者透過整合四種不同維度的指標——Stoch RSI（動能加速度）、RSI（純價格強弱）、MFI（納入成交量的資金流向）與 CCI（價格統計偏離度），要求多維度數據達成共識後才確認趨勢，從底層解決了震盪市頻繁止損的痛點。

·        2. Dual-Layer Architecture: Regime Identification vs. Execution Timing (雙層架構：大勢定調與精準擇時分離): The presenter separates 'where the market is going' from 'when to pull the trigger.' Long-period smoothing (200 or 144) on the MAC prevents emotional overreaction to short-term volatility spikes, while fast oscillators (AEO crossovers or MACD divergences) pinpoint exact entry candles only in the direction of the dominant flow.

2. 雙層架構思維：大勢定調（Regime）與精準擇時（Timing）分離：講者將「判斷趨勢方向」與「尋找進場扳機」拆分為兩個獨立模組。透過將 MAC 參數拉長至 200 或 144，使趨勢線具備高慣性、不因短期劇烈波動而頻繁翻轉；再由靈敏度較高的 AEO 交叉或半神 MACD 背離在順勢方向上捕捉精確進場點，實現「大週期定方向、小結構找買點」。

·        3. Asymmetric Risk-Free Scaling Model (非對稱無風險推保護倉位模型): Rather than an all-or-nothing exit, the strategy locks in guaranteed mathematical expectancy at a 1:1.5 risk-to-reward threshold by closing 50% of the position and shifting the stop-loss to breakeven. This converts the remaining 50% into a 'free trade' capable of capturing outsized 1:2+ trend extensions with zero principal risk.

3. 非對稱無風險推保護倉位模型：放棄「一次性全倉止盈」的二元博弈，改採「分段收割 + 風險歸零」機制。當行情達到 1:1.5 盈虧比時先平倉一半落袋為安，同時將止損移動至入場保本價，使剩餘半倉成為「零本金風險部位」，讓交易者能無心理壓力地持有至 1:2 甚至更高的趨勢紅利。

·        4. Sample-Size Statistical Validation (大數法則回測驗證思維): Recognizing that indicator sensitivity varies across asset volatility profiles (e.g., BTC 4H vs. 1H) and timeframes, the presenter mandates a minimum sample size of 100 historical trades to verify positive expectancy before committing capital.

4. 大數法則樣本驗證思維：講者明確指出不同交易品種與時間週期的波動特性存在差異，拒絕盲目套用參數，要求學習者在特定標的與週期上至少完成 100 次交易機會的歷史回測，以統計學大數法則驗證策略適配性。

Module 6: Risk Management, Scaling & Backtesting Protocol [00:04:08 - 00:05:17]

模組六：風控止損、分批止盈推保護與百次歷史回測規範 [00:04:08 - 00:05:17]

·        [Concept Explanation] Establishes structured stop-loss placement, two-stage take-profit scaling with breakeven protection, and mandatory 100-trade empirical backtesting.

【概念解釋】建立標準化止損位置、兩段式分批止盈與保本推保護機制，並要求在實盤前完成至少 100 次交易機會的回測。

·        [Key Steps / Workflow] 1. Set Stop Loss at swing low/high, key support/resistance, or using ATR (Average True Range). 2. Set Take Profit 1 at a 1:1.5 Risk-to-Reward ratio. 3. Once TP1 hits, close 50% of the position and move Stop Loss to the entry price (breakeven). 4. Hold the remaining 50% to target 1:2 or higher. 5. Backtest at least 100 trades on your chosen asset and timeframe before live trading.

【關鍵步驟/操作流程】1. 將止損設在波段低點/高點附近、關鍵支撐/阻力位，或參考 ATR 指標設定。 2. 初始止損與首個止盈目標的比值保持在 1:1.5 左右。 3. 價格到達 1:1.5 止盈位後，平掉一半（50%）倉位，同時將止損移動至入場價（保本位）。 4. 剩餘一半倉位繼續持有，博取 1:2 甚至更高的盈虧比。 5. 正式使用前，務必在自己常做的交易品種與週期上回測至少 100 次交易機會。

·        [Key Formulas / Terminology] ATR / Average True Range (平均真實波幅), 1:1.5 Risk-to-Reward Ratio (1:1.5 盈虧比), Partial Take Profit / Scaling Out (分批平倉), Breakeven Stop Loss (保本止損), 100-Trade Backtest (百次交易回測).

【重要公式/專有名詞】ATR（平均真實波幅）、1:1.5 盈虧比、Partial Take Profit（平調一半倉位）、Breakeven Stop Loss（止損移至入場價）、100-Trade Backtest（百次交易回測）。

·        [Common Pitfalls / Precautions] Forgetting to move the stop-loss to breakeven after taking 50% profit can turn a winning trade into a net loss if the market reverses.

【常見誤區/注意事項】在達到 1:1.5 止盈並平掉半倉後，若忘記將止損移動至入場價，一旦行情反轉將導致剩餘倉位虧損、吞噬已得利潤。
