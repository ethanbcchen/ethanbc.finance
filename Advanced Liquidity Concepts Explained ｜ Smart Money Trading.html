1. Executive Summary

1. 核心總結

The video argues that traders should read price through liquidity rather than rely on a bullish/bearish guess. First define a valid higher-timeframe trading range from a confirmed break-of-structure impulse. Then distinguish external liquidity beyond the range from internal liquidity within it. Their interaction forms dynamic liquidity: price rotates between internal pools such as FVGs and external pools around major highs/lows. FVG reactions—especially around the midpoint—can help infer which external side may be targeted. A high-quality sweep takes liquidity beyond a key level and closes back inside, often around major session opens. The speaker prioritizes external sweeps and treats internal liquidity mainly as fuel, while noting a specific FVG-related exception.

影片主張，交易者應以流動性解讀價格，而不是先入為主猜測多空。首先，從已確認的結構突破推動波段，在較高時間框架定義有效交易區間；接著區分區間外的外部流動性與區間內的內部流動性。兩者互動形成動態流動性：價格在 FVG 等內部流動性池，以及主要高低點附近的外部流動性池之間輪動。觀察價格對 FVG、尤其是其中點的反應，可協助推測下一個外部目標。高品質掃蕩會先越過關鍵價位取得流動性，再收回該價位區間內，且常出現在主要時段開盤附近。講者優先關注外部掃蕩，並將內部流動性視為推動價格的燃料；但也提出一種與 FVG 有關的例外情境。

4. Mental Models & Logic

4. 底層邏輯與思維模型

·        Range first, then interpretation.

先定區間，再做解讀。

·        Use a higher timeframe to avoid defining the market structure from the same noisy chart used for entry.

使用較高時框，避免在入場用的雜訊較多圖表上定義整體結構。

·        Validate the impulse: the speaker requires a meaningful retracement into discount (below the 50% level) and a later break of the inducement/internal swing low to confirm completion.

驗證推動波段：講者要求價格曾有意義地回撤至折價區（50% 以下），並在之後跌破誘導／內部波段低點，以確認波段完成。

·        Think in targets, not labels: replace “bullish or bearish?” with “which liquidity pool is price likely to seek next?”

以目標取代標籤：不要只問「看多還是看空」，而要問「價格下一步可能尋找哪個流動性池？」

·        Treat internal liquidity as a waypoint/fuel and external liquidity as the major destination in the speaker’s model.

在講者模型中，將內部流動性視為中途站／燃料，將外部流動性視為主要目的地。

·        Read reaction, not just location: FVG respect, CE midpoint response, clean displacement through an FVG, and IFVG retests are used as directional evidence.

觀察反應而不只看位置：FVG 是否受尊重、CE 中點反應、是否乾淨貫穿 FVG，以及 IFVG 回測，都是方向判讀線索。

·        Demand confirmation for a sweep: taking a high/low alone is insufficient; the defining feature is the close back inside the level/range.

掃蕩必須有確認：單純刺穿高低點並不足夠；定義性特徵是收盤重新回到該價位／區間內。

·        Prioritize context: external level, session timing, and higher-timeframe alignment strengthen the setup; internal-only sweeps are generally lower quality except for the stated FVG condition.

重視情境：外部價位、時段時機與高時框方向一致可強化型態；單純內部掃蕩通常品質較低，除非符合講者提出的 FVG 例外條件。

·        Concept: A range is the active dealing area where liquidity builds, not merely any visible consolidation box.

Module 3 — Dynamic liquidity and price rotation

模組三：動態流動性與價格輪動

[00:06:20–00:08:24] Original transcript time range

[00:06:20–00:08:24] Original transcript time range（時間定位；原始字幕未提供影片網址，因此無法建立可點擊的外部影片連結。）

·        Concept: Dynamic liquidity is the interaction between internal and external pools. Price repeatedly rotates from highs/lows toward FVGs and from FVGs toward highs/lows.

概念：動態流動性是內外部流動性池之間的互動。價格會反覆由高低點移向 FVG，也會由 FVG 移向高低點。

·        Process: (1) Price seeks an internal target. (2) Inducement forms as price approaches it. (3) Internal liquidity is swept or the FVG imbalance is filled. (4) Focus shifts outward to the next external pool. (5) Old liquidity is removed and a fresh external draw develops.

流程：（1）價格先尋找內部目標。（2）接近目標時形成誘導。（3）內部流動性被掃蕩，或 FVG 不平衡被填補。（4）焦點轉向下一個外部流動性池。（5）舊流動性被清除，新的外部吸引目標形成。

·        Terms: Dynamic Liquidity, Rotation, Inducement, FVG Mitigation, External Target.

名詞：Dynamic Liquidity、Rotation、Inducement、FVG Mitigation、External Target。

·        Caution: If internal liquidity is absent and price action is clean/efficient, the speaker says price may move directly between external BSL and SSL until a new inefficiency, FVG or inducement appears.

注意：若內部流動性不存在、價格行為乾淨且有效率，講者認為價格可能直接在外部 BSL 與 SSL 之間移動，直到新的不平衡、FVG 或誘導出現。

Module 5 — Identify a valid liquidity sweep

·        Definition: Price takes liquidity above/below a key level and then closes back inside that level’s range; the speaker expects movement toward liquidity on the opposite side.

定義：價格先取得關鍵價位上方／下方的流動性，隨後收盤回到該價位區間內；講者預期價格接著朝對側流動性移動。

·        Two types: External sweeps occur at key external levels and are prioritized. Internal sweeps occur at internal swing highs/lows and are described as lower probability in general.

·        Session context: High-quality sweeps often occur just before or after major session opens. Examples include a move above Asia High followed by reversal through Asia Low, and a move above London High at New York open followed by a move toward London Low.

時段情境：高品質掃蕩常出現在主要時段開盤前後。例子包括價格突破亞盤高點後反轉並跌破亞盤低點，以及紐約開盤時突破倫敦高點後轉向倫敦低點。
