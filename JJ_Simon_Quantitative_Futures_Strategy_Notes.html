Deep Learning Study Notes: JJ Simon's Quantitative Futures Trading Strategy

深度學習筆記：JJ Simon 的量化期貨交易策略（純價格行為與均值回歸模型）

1. Executive Summary
1. 核心總結

This video analyzes the quantitative futures trading methodology of JJ Simon, a computational finance honors graduate from the University of Washington who earned over $1.8 million in proprietary trading firm payouts within roughly a year starting from $5,000, while spending over $500,000 on evaluation fees. Focusing exclusively on the 1-minute chart of Nasdaq 100 Futures (NQ) without technical indicators, his core framework combines an initial 5-minute opening momentum trade with session-based Mean Reversion back toward a dynamically defined Fair Value. Fair Value is anchored to the 9:29 AM pre-open candle, pre-news price levels, or post-news consolidation ranges. Entries rely strictly on candle closes confirming Displacement Candles or Market Structure Breaks, paired with fixed point-based Stop Loss and Take Profit targets calibrated to a 1:1.5 Risk-to-Reward Ratio tailored to prop firm evaluation rules. Ultimately, the video highlights that while this fixed-parameter model thrives under prop firm capital structures, backtesting and user feedback indicate it merely breaks even on personal accounts without discretionary order-flow adjustments.

本影片深入解析華盛頓大學計算金融學優等畢業生 JJ Simon 的量化期貨交易方法論。他以 5,000 美元起家，在一年多內透過自營交易公司（Prop Firm）分潤賺取超過 180 萬美元，但也投入了逾 50 萬美元購買考核帳戶。其策略專注於納斯達克 100 期貨（NQ）的 1 分鐘 K 線圖，完全不使用技術指標，核心架構結合「開盤前 5 分鐘順勢單」與「盤中均值回歸（Mean Reversion）單」，交易目標是讓價格回歸動態定義的「公允價值（Fair Value）」。公允價值依情境錨定於開盤前 9:29 K 線、重要經濟數據公布前價格或突發新聞後的橫盤區間。進場嚴格等待 K 線收盤確認「位移 K 線（Displacement Candle）」或「市場結構突破（Market Structure Break）」，並配合專為自營考核設計的 1:1.5 盈虧比固定點數停損與停利。最後影片指出，該固定點數模型高度依賴自營公司帳戶規則，若用於個人資金帳戶往往僅能打平，需依盤面靈活調整。

2. Key Concepts & Definitions (with Timestamps)
2. 知識地圖與核心概念（附時間戳記）

·        [00:00:07] Proprietary Trading Firm (Prop Firm / 自營交易公司): Firms that provide traders with funded capital after passing paid evaluation challenges, sharing a percentage of profits while enforcing strict drawdown limits.

提供資金給通過付費考核的交易員進行操作，雙方按比例分成利潤，並設有嚴格最大虧損限制的機構。

·        [00:00:32] Nasdaq 100 Futures (NQ / 納斯達克 100 期貨): The sole futures instrument traded in this strategy, valued at $20 per point for standard contracts (NQ) and $2 per point for micro contracts (MNQ).

本策略唯一交易的期貨品種；標準合約（NQ）每跳動 1 點價值 20 美元，微型合約（MNQ）每點價值 2 美元。

·        [00:00:54] Fair Value (公允價值 / 公允價): The equilibrium price zone where buyers and sellers agree prior to an aggressive order-flow imbalance; prices pushed away from this zone tend to revert to it.

在大型買賣訂單流造成失衡前，市場買賣雙方認可的均衡價格區間；價格偏離後具有向該區域回歸的傾向。

·        [00:01:02] Opening Momentum Trade (開盤順勢交易): A directional scalp executed within the first 5 minutes of the market open based on the breakout strength of the initial 9:30 AM 1-minute candle.

在美股 9:30 開盤後前 5 分鐘內，依據第一根 1 分鐘 K 線的突破力道所執行的單向順勢短線交易。

·        [00:01:06] Mean Reversion (均值回歸): The core statistical assumption that extreme short-term price spikes caused by opening orders or scheduled news releases will retrace back to Fair Value.

核心統計假設，認為因開盤訂單湧入或預定經濟數據公布所引發的短期價格過度衝刺，最終會回落至公允價值。

·        [00:02:58] Displacement Candle (位移 K 線 / 吞沒形態): A strong momentum candle (or two consecutive candles) whose body is larger than the prior opposing candle and closes beyond its extreme high or low.

實體長度大於前一根反向 K 線，且收盤價突破前一根 K 線最高點或跌破最低點的強動能 K 線（或連續兩根同向 K 線）。

·        [00:03:13] Market Structure Break (MSB / 市場結構突破): A reversal confirmation occurring when a 1-minute candle closes below a prior swing low during an upswing (or above a prior swing high during a downswing).

反轉確認訊號，指價格上衝過程中，某根 1 分鐘 K 線收盤價跌破前一個波段小低點（做多則為收盤突破前波段小高點）。

·        [00:04:48] News Drift (新聞漂移): A sustained, multi-hour directional trend triggered by unexpected breaking news (e.g., surprise central bank remarks or political posts) that permanently shifts Fair Value.

由市場未預料到的突發新聞（如央行官員意外講話或政要發文）引發的持續數小時單邊趨勢，會永久改變原有的公允價值。

·        [00:02:25] Fixed Point Risk-to-Reward Ratio (1:1.5 固定點數盈虧比): A rigid position-sizing framework typically risking 25 points to gain 38 points, calibrated directly to prop firm profit targets and maximum drawdown rules.

嚴格的固定點數風控框架，標準配置為停損 25 點、停利 38 點（盈虧比 1:1.5），專為匹配自營考核的獲利目標與虧損上限而設計。

4. Mental Models & Logic (Why & How)
4. 底層邏輯與思維模型

1. Order-Flow Imbalance & Equilibrium Gravity (訂單流失衡與均衡引力模型): Why: At major session opens (9:30 AM EST) or scheduled macro releases (8:30 AM), institutional algorithms and overnight retail orders flood the order book simultaneously, causing temporary liquidity vacuums and price overshoots. How: Rather than chasing emotional spikes, the trader anchors the pre-imbalance equilibrium price (Fair Value) and waits for momentum exhaustion to capture the snap-back.

原理（Why）：在美股開盤（9:30）或預定宏觀數據公布（8:30）瞬間，機構演算法與隔夜散戶訂單同時湧入，造成短暫流動性失衡與價格過度衝刺。做法（How）：不盲目追逐情緒性大 K 線，而是錨定失衡前的均衡價格（公允價值），等待動能衰竭後捕捉價格彈回的過程。

2. Information Expectation vs. Surprise Dichotomy (資訊預期差二分法模型): Why: Markets price in scheduled events (CPI, NFP) beforehand, making initial spikes mere liquidity sweeps; conversely, unscheduled shocks (unexpected Fed speeches or political posts) alter fundamental valuation. How: Scheduled news is faded via Mean Reversion back to the 8:29 AM price, whereas unscheduled news is traded with the trend via News Drift until a new consolidation base forms.

原理（Why）：市場對預定數據（CPI、非農）早已提前定價，公布瞬間的劇烈波動多屬過度反應；反之，突發性意外事件（如聯準會主席突然講話）會真正改變基本面定價。做法（How）：預定數據採用「均值回歸」做回 8:29 價格；突發新聞則採用「新聞漂移」順勢跟進，直到價格重新橫盤築底。

3. Constant Dollar-Risk Volatility Normalization (固定金額風險下的波動率標準化模型): Why: Fixed-point stops (25 points) fail during high-volatility expansions, leading to premature stop-outs. How: When 1-minute volatility doubles (e.g., an opening candle exceeds 25 points), the trader doubles the stop distance (to 50 points) and halves the position size (e.g., switching from 1 standard NQ to 5 micro MNQ), keeping maximum dollar loss identical ($500).

原理（Why）：固定點數停損（25 點）在市場波動率急劇放大時極易被掃損。做法（How）：當 1 分鐘波動率倍增（如開盤首根 K 線超過 25 點），交易員將停損距離放寬一倍（至 50 點）並將合約口數減半（如從 1 口標準 NQ 改為 5 口微型 MNQ），確保單筆最大虧損金額完全不變（皆為 500 美元）。

4. Prop Firm Payout Asymmetric Exploitation (自營考核規則不對稱套利模型): Why: A typical $50,000 prop firm challenge requires a $3,000 profit target against a $2,000 maximum drawdown—an exact 1.5:1 ratio. How: By hardcoding a 1:1.5 Risk-to-Reward Ratio (25-point SL vs. 38-point TP) and executing across multiple evaluation accounts, the strategy turns trading into a statistical pass-rate optimization problem tailored to prop firm rules.

原理（Why）：典型的 5 萬美元自營考核帳戶要求獲利 3,000 美元過關、虧損 2,000 美元淘汰，比例正好是 1.5:1。做法（How）：透過將盈虧比寫死為 1:1.5（停損 25 點對停利 38 點）並在多帳戶同步操作，將交易轉化為高度貼合自營考核規則的統計過關率優化遊戲。

Module 2: Defining Dynamic Fair Value Across Market Regimes [00:00:42 - 00:02:02]
模組二：不同市場情境下的動態公允價值定義 [00:00:42 - 00:02:02]

·        Concept Explanation: Fair Value represents the consensus price before aggressive orders push the market out of balance. Because market catalysts differ by day, the Fair Value reference line must adapt across three distinct scenarios.

【概念解釋】：公允價值（Fair Value）代表大量買賣訂單將價格推離均衡前的共識價格區間。由於每日市場催化劑不同，公允價值基準線必須根據三種情境動態調整。

·        Key Steps & Workflow: (1) Normal Days: Box out the 9:29 AM 1-minute candle (the final minute before market open) and extend a horizontal line rightward across the entire session. If price immediately breaks back into a multi-hour pre-market consolidation range and holds, shift Fair Value to that range; (2) Scheduled News Days (8:30 AM CPI, NFP, PPI): Use the 8:29 AM 1-minute candle prior to data release; if price returns to the 8:29 AM level by 9:30 AM, ignore the 9:30 AM open price; (3) Unexpected Breaking News: Abandon prior Fair Value, wait for the spike to stop and consolidate sideways, and mark the new range as Fair Value.

【關鍵步驟/操作流程】：(1) 一般交易日：框出開盤前最後一分鐘（9:29）的 1 分鐘 K 線並向右延伸橫線作為全天基準；若開盤後直接衝回盤前橫盤已久的區間並站穩，則改用該橫盤區間；(2) 預定數據日（8:30 公布通膨 CPI、非農 NFP、PPI）：改用數據公布前 8:29 的 K 線作為公允價；若 9:30 開盤時價格已回到數據前價格，則忽略開盤價；(3) 突發新聞日：舊公允價失效，等價格衝刺停止並橫盤整理後，以新橫盤區間作為公允價。

·        Key Terms: Fair Value (公允價值), Pre-Open Consolidation (盤前橫盤區間), Scheduled Macro Releases (預定宏觀數據：CPI / NFP / PPI).

【重要公式/專有名詞】：Fair Value（公允價值）、Pre-Open Consolidation（盤前橫盤區間）、CPI（消費者物價指數）、NFP（非農就業數據）、PPI（生產者物價指數）。

·        Pitfalls & Precautions: Never cling to an outdated 9:30 AM opening price when an 8:30 AM data print or sudden political/Fed announcement has structurally repriced the market.

【常見誤區/注意事項】：當早盤 8:30 數據公布或突發政治/聯準會講話已改變市場定價時，切勿死守 9:30 開盤價作為回歸目標。

Module 3: Opening 5-Minute Momentum Trade Execution [00:02:02 - 00:02:45]
模組三：開盤前 5 分鐘順勢單執行規則 [00:02:02 - 00:02:45]

·        Concept Explanation: Once Fair Value is established, the trader takes a single directional momentum trade within the first 5 minutes of the 9:30 AM open to capitalize on initial order-flow follow-through.

【概念解釋】：定好公允價值後，交易員會在 9:30 開盤後的前 5 分鐘內執行一筆順勢單，旨在捕捉開盤瞬間訂單流湧入的第一波慣性動能。

·        Key Steps & Workflow: (1) Observe the first 1-minute candle at 9:30 AM; (2) Go Long if it closes as a bullish candle above the highs of the preceding few candles; go Short if it closes bearish below recent lows; (3) Filter for highest probability: check the 6-to-12-hour overnight trend—if overnight drifted up, opening bearish reversals are stronger (and vice versa); (4) Set standard 38-point TP and 25-point SL; if the 9:30 AM candle body exceeds 25 points, widen SL to 50 points, TP to 76 points, and halve contract size; (5) Cancel all momentum setups if no entry triggers within the first 5 minutes (by 9:35 AM).

【關鍵步驟/操作流程】：(1) 觀察 9:30 開盤第一根 1 分鐘 K 線；(2) 收陽線且收盤高於前幾根 K 線高點則做多；收陰線且收盤低於前幾根低點則做空；(3) 結合過去 6–12 小時隔夜大方向過濾：隔夜一路上漲則開盤更容易向下（反之亦然），當順勢方向與回歸公允價方向一致時勝率最高；(4) 標準停利 38 點、停損 25 點；若首根 K 線開盤到收盤已走超過 25 點，則將停損放寬至 50 點、停利 76 點，並將口數減半；(5) 開盤過了 5 分鐘（9:35 後）不再做任何順勢單。

·        Key Formulas & Terms: Standard Risk/Reward = 25-pt SL / 38-pt TP (1:1.5 R:R). Volatility Adjustment: Candle Range > 25 pts -> 50-pt SL / 76-pt TP with 0.5x Size.

【重要公式/專有名詞】：標準盈虧比 ＝ 停損 25 點 / 停利 38 點（1:1.5）。波動率調整公式：首根 K 線波幅 > 25 點 → 停損 50 點 / 停利 76 點，倉位口數 × 0.5。

·        Pitfalls & Precautions: Chasing opening momentum after 9:35 AM exposes the trader to the exact snap-back reversal targeted in Phase B.

【常見誤區/注意事項】：絕對禁止在開盤 5 分鐘（9:35）之後才追進順勢單，否則極易買在最高點或賣在最低點，剛好成為階段 B 均值回歸的對手盤。

Module 4: Mean Reversion Entry Signals — Displacement & Structure Break [00:02:45 - 00:03:42]
模組四：均值回歸進場訊號——位移 K 線與結構突破 [00:02:45 - 00:03:42]

·        Concept Explanation: After the first 5 minutes, the trader switches exclusively to Mean Reversion: looking only for Shorts when price is above Fair Value, and only for Longs when price is below Fair Value, confirmed strictly by 1-minute candle closes.

【概念解釋】：開盤 5 分鐘過後，剩餘時間只做往公允價值回歸的反向單：價格在公允價上方只等做空訊號，在公允價下方只等做多訊號，且必須等待 1 分鐘 K 線正式收盤才算數。

·        Key Steps & Workflow: (1) Monitor the countdown timer at the bottom-right of the screen so entries occur precisely as a new minute begins; (2) Signal Type 1 — Displacement Candle: For shorts, a bearish candle (or two consecutive bearish candles) must have a larger body than the prior bullish candle and close below the prior bullish candle's lowest point; (3) Signal Type 2 — Market Structure Break (MSB): During an upward push, identify a swing low (a candle whose low is lower than both adjacent candles). Once a subsequent candle closes below this swing low, enter Short (MSB is considered more reliable than Displacement, especially if the level was tested multiple times); (4) Reverse both rules for Long setups below Fair Value.

【關鍵步驟/操作流程】：(1) 緊盯螢幕右下角秒數倒數，在新的一分鐘跳出瞬間確認前一根收盤是否破位並立即進場；(2) 訊號一「位移 K 線（Displacement Candle）」：以做空為例，一根陰線（或連續兩根陰線）的實體必須長於前一根陽線，且收盤價跌破前一根陽線的最低點；(3) 訊號二「結構突破（Market Structure Break）」：價格上衝時會留下小低點（某根 K 線最低點比左右兩根都低），一旦後續有 K 線收盤跌破該小低點即做空（講者認為結構突破比位移 K 線更穩健，若該低點曾被多次測試未破後跌破則更可靠）；(4) 做多訊號則將上述兩種邏輯完全反轉。

·        Key Terms: Displacement Candle (位移 K 線/吞沒形態), Swing Low/High (波段小低點/小高點), Market Structure Break (MSB / 市場結構突破), Candle Close Confirmation (收盤確認).

【重要公式/專有名詞】：Displacement Candle（位移 K 線）、Swing Low/High（波段小低點/小高點）、Market Structure Break（MSB / 市場結構突破）、Candle Close Confirmation（K 線收盤確認）。

·        Pitfalls & Precautions: Never enter mid-candle before the 60-second bar closes; wicks frequently fake breakouts and reverse before the minute ends.

【常見誤區/注意事項】：嚴禁在 1 分鐘 K 線尚未走完收盤前提前搶進，盤中上下影線經常出現假跌破或假突破，必須等收盤確認。

Module 5: Fixed-Point Targets, Distance Ratio & News Drift Handling [00:03:42 - 00:05:08]
模組五：固定點數停損停利、距離比例與新聞漂移應對 [00:03:42 - 00:05:08]

·        Concept Explanation: Even though Mean Reversion trades aim toward Fair Value, exits use fixed point tiers rather than exact Fair Value lines because price movement beyond Fair Value is random. Special rules apply to extreme data surprises and breaking news.

【概念解釋】：儘管均值回歸是朝著公允價值交易，但停利與停損仍採用固定點數而非直接掛在公允價線上，因為價格回到公允價後的走勢全憑運氣。此外，針對極端數據與突發新聞另有專屬應對邏輯。

·        Key Steps & Workflow: (1) Measure the distance from entry to Fair Value and select a fixed TP tier (38, 50, 75, or 100 points); (2) Enforce the 80% Distance Rule: actual distance to Fair Value must be at least 80% of the chosen TP (e.g., at least 30 points away for a 38-point TP); (3) Select fixed SL tier (25, 37.5, 50, or 75 points) and scale contracts inversely (e.g., 1 NQ @ 25-pt SL = $500 risk; 5 MNQ @ 50-pt SL = $500 risk); (4) Scheduled Data Rule: Never chase the initial 8:30 AM spike candle; wait for Displacement/MSB back to 8:29 AM price. If data deviates wildly from consensus and trends non-stop without reversal signals, skip trading; (5) Unexpected Breaking News (News Drift): Enter early in the direction of the sudden spike, place SL below the pre-spike consolidation range, trail exits until a candle closes below a prior swing low, and only trade Mean Reversion after a new sideways range forms.

【關鍵步驟/操作流程】：(1) 測量進場點到公允價的距離，挑選最接近的固定停利檔位（常用 38 點，較遠則選 50、75 或 100 點）；(2) 遵守「80% 距離法則」：進場點離公允價的距離至少要有目標停利點數的八成（例如想賺 38 點，距離至少需 30 點）；(3) 挑選固定停損檔位（25、37.5、50 或 75 點）並反向調整口數（如 25 點停損做 1 口 NQ 虧 500 美元；放寬至 50 點停損則改做 5 口微型 MNQ，每點 2 美元 × 5 口＝10 美元，虧損仍為 500 美元）；(4) 預定數據處理：不追 8:30 第一根大 K 線，等訊號往 8:29 價格做回；若數據遠超預期導致單邊暴走無訊號則觀望；(5) 突發新聞（新聞漂移）：早點順勢上車，停損放起跳前橫盤下方，不設固定停利，等收盤跌破前一個小低點再出場，待價格重新橫盤後再做回歸。

·        Key Formulas & Terms: 80% Distance Rule: Distance to Fair Value >= 0.80 * Take Profit Points. Constant Risk Sizing: Contracts = Max Dollar Risk / (Stop Loss Points * Point Value).

【重要公式/專有名詞】：80% 距離法則：進場點至公允價距離 ≥ 0.80 × 目標停利點數。等額風險口數公式：交易口數 ＝ 單筆最大虧損金額 ÷（停損點數 × 每點價值）。

·        Pitfalls & Precautions: Do not treat unexpected breaking news (like a sudden Trump tweet or Fed speech) as a Mean Reversion fade; fading News Drift leads to catastrophic consecutive losses.

【常見誤區/注意事項】：切勿將未預期的突發新聞（如政要突然發文或聯準會主席意外講話）當作普通數據進行反向做空/做多，逆勢對抗「新聞漂移」會導致嚴重連虧。

Module 6: Session Windows, Circuit Breakers, Case Studies & Reality Check [00:05:08 - 00:08:48]
模組六：90 分鐘交易窗口、熔斷機制、實戰案例與策略侷限 [00:05:08 - 00:08:48]

·        Concept Explanation: Liquidity and Mean Reversion validity decay rapidly after the first 90 minutes of each major market session. Combining strict time windows, loss limits, and real-world case studies reveals both the execution precision and the structural boundaries of this system.

【概念解釋】：各主要交易時段開盤 90 分鐘後，市場流動性與公允價值回歸的有效性會大幅衰減。透過時間窗口限制、連虧熔斷與兩個實戰案例，能完整看清此系統的操作細節與現實侷限。

·        Key Steps & Workflow: (1) Trade only the first 90 minutes of each session: NY Open (9:30–11:00 AM EST), Futures Reopen (6:00 PM EST), and Asian Open; avoid the low-volume 11:00 AM–2:00 PM EST dead zone; (2) Enforce the 3-Loss Circuit Breaker: 3 consecutive Mean Reversion losses in one session proves Fair Value has shifted—stop trading until the next session; (3) Move SL to breakeven before a new session open or scheduled speeches (e.g., 9:45 / 10:00 AM); (4) Case Study 1 (July 14 CPI): 8:29 AM Fair Value at 29694 -> 8:31 AM spike to 29920+ -> 8:35 AM MSB short at 29863 (175-pt TP, 25-pt SL) + 9:30 AM bearish open half-size short (50-pt SL, 76-pt TP) -> both hit TP by 9:35 AM at 29685; (5) Case Study 2 (Aug 25 Pre-Market Range): Open reclaimed 1-hour pre-market range (29328.25) -> shorted spike rejection at 29392.75 across accounts for $4,700+ profit, then stopped out twice on overextended lows and halted trading; (6) Critical Evaluation: Fixed points match Prop Firm 1:1.5 math ($3K target vs $2K drawdown), but backtests show minimal edge on personal accounts without discretionary order-flow targets.

【關鍵步驟/操作流程】：(1) 每個時段僅做開盤後 90 分鐘：美股早盤 9:30–11:00（美東時間）、期貨傍晚重開盤及亞洲盤開盤，避開 11:00–14:00 無量垃圾時間；(2) 單一時段連虧 3 次立即收手，代表公允價已轉移，等下一時段再重新錨定；(3) 手上有單遇新時段開盤或 9:45/10:00 數據講話前，先將停損移至進場保本價；(4) 案例一（7/14 CPI）：8:29 公允價 29694 → 8:31 衝至 29920 多 → 8:35 收在 29863 結構突破做空（停利 175 點、停損 25 點）＋ 9:30 開盤大陰線減半口數順勢空（停損 50 點、停利 76 點）→ 9:35 跌至 29685 雙雙停利；(5) 案例二（8/25 盤前橫盤）：開盤衝回盤前橫盤區（29328.25），9:50 於衝高回落（29392.75）分帳戶做空獲利逾 4,700 美元；隨後跌過頭兩次抄底做多皆停損，確認結構下破後果斷停手；(6) 客觀反思：固定點數專為自營公司 1:1.5 考核規則設計，網傳回測與本人亦坦言若用個人資金操作，僅能打平且需看盤面靈活設定停損停利。

·        Key Terms: 90-Minute Session Window (90 分鐘黃金窗口), 3-Loss Circuit Breaker (三連虧熔斷機制), Breakeven Stop (保本停損).

【重要公式/專有名詞】：90-Minute Session Window（開盤 90 分鐘窗口）、3-Loss Circuit Breaker（單時段三連虧熔斷）、Breakeven Stop（保本停損）。

·        Pitfalls & Precautions: Do not blindly copy fixed-point parameters onto a personal cash account, and remember that taking two full-size losses in a row can blow a tight prop firm drawdown limit if risk per trade is oversized.

【常見誤區/注意事項】：切勿將固定點數參數直接照搬至個人自有資金帳戶；此外，若單筆風險金額設得過高（如達帳戶最大回撤的一半），連續兩筆虧損就會直接導致自營帳戶爆倉。
