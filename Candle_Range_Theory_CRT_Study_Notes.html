1. Executive Summary
一、 核心總結

Candle Range Theory (CRT) is an objective, mechanical Inner Circle Trader (ICT) trading framework that treats every higher-timeframe candlestick as a complete lower-timeframe price range. Rather than guessing market direction, traders align with institutional money by tracking the market's perpetual three-phase cycle known as the Power of Three (AMD): Accumulation, Manipulation, and Distribution. In a classic three-candle CRT sequence, the first candle establishes a range (CRH and CRL), the second candle manipulates price by sweeping liquidity beyond the range via a wick before closing back inside, and the third candle distributes toward the opposite liquidity pool. By filtering setups through key structural levels, specific high-probability killzone times (such as 1 AM, 5 AM, 9 AM, 1 PM, 3 PM, 6 PM, and 9 PM), and strict multi-timeframe alignment (e.g., 1H CRT with 5M/1M Order Block entries), traders can predict upcoming candle expansions and achieve high reward-to-risk setups.

蠟燭區間理論 (Candle Range Theory, CRT) 是一套客觀且機械化的 ICT 交易框架，其核心觀點在於「每一根大時區 K 線，在小時區皆為一段完整的價格波動區間」。交易者無需主觀猜測方向，而是透過追蹤市場永恆的三階段循環——三位一體力量 (Power of Three / AMD)：累積 (Accumulation)、操縱 (Manipulation) 與派發 (Distribution)，與機構資金站同一陣線。在經典的三根 K 線 CRT 模型中，第一根 K 線確立區間高點 (CRH) 與低點 (CRL)；第二根 K 線以影線掃掠區間外側的流動性並拉回區間內收盤（完成操縱）；第三根 K 線則向對側流動性池展開派發。透過結合關鍵支撐阻力位、特定高勝率時間點（凌晨 1、5 點，上午 9 點，下午 1、3、6 點及晚間 9 點）以及嚴格的多時區搭配（例如 1 小時 CRT 配合 5 分鐘或 1 分鐘訂單塊進場），交易者能精準預判後續 K 線走勢並獲取高盈虧比機會。

4. Mental Models & Underlying Logic
四、 底層邏輯與思維模型

1. Fractal Timeframe Equivalence (Top-Down Decomposition) — Why: Retail traders view candlesticks as isolated static shapes, missing the internal order flow. How: By recognizing that one HTF candle is simply a container for an LTF range, you use the HTF to define directional bias and liquidity targets while using the LTF to pinpoint low-risk structural entries.

1. 碎形時區等價思維（由上而下拆解） — 原因 (Why)：散戶常將單根 K 線視為靜態孤立圖形，忽略內部訂單流。方法 (How)：認知到 1 根大時區 K 線本質上就是小時區完整波動的容器，藉此用大時區定方向與流動性目標，用小時區精準定位低風險結構進場點。

2. Institutional Liquidity Engineering (Stop-Hunt Before Expansion) — Why: Smart money needs counterparty liquidity to fill large institutional orders before driving price toward its true target. How: Never chase the initial breakout of a range candle; wait for the fake breakout (Manipulation/Sweep) to trigger retail stops and close back inside the range before entering in the opposite direction.

2. 機構流動性工程思維（先獵殺停損、再真突破） — 原因 (Why)：聰明錢在推動真實趨勢前，需要對手盤流動性來消化龐大訂單。方法 (How)：絕不追逐區間 K 線的初次突破；耐心等待假突破（操縱／掃掠）觸發散戶停損並重新收盤回區間內部後，再順著真實派發方向進場。

3. Dynamic Session Adaptation (Conditional If-Then Logic) — Why: Markets do not follow rigid schedules blindly; if volatility expands early, the AMD sequence shifts forward. How: Evaluate the Asian session first. If Asia accumulates, expect London to manipulate and NY to distribute; if Asia expands directionally, expect London to consolidate (accumulate) and shift manipulation to NY.

3. 盤面動態適應模型（條件式 If-Then 推導邏輯） — 原因 (Why)：市場不會死板照表操課，若波動提前爆發，AMD 序列便會順延。方法 (How)：每日優先檢視亞盤表現。若亞盤橫盤累積，則預期倫敦盤操縱、紐約盤派發；若亞盤已走單邊擴展，則預期倫敦盤轉為累積，並將操縱階段順延至紐約盤。

4. Mechanical Rule Reduction (Minimizing Discretionary Noise) — Why: Emotional hesitation and excessive indicator confluence lead to analysis paralysis. How: Standardize trading into four binary checkpoints—Key Level, Key Time, Confirmed Wick Sweep, and LTF Order Block—with fixed targets at 50% equilibrium and 100% range extreme.

4. 機械化降噪決策模型（極簡標準作業程序） — 原因 (Why)：情緒猶豫與過多指標疊加會導致分析癱瘓。方法 (How)：將交易標準化為四個明確檢查點——關鍵位置、關鍵時間、確認影線掃掠、小時區訂單塊，並固定將止盈設於 50% 平衡點與 100% 區間極值。

Module 3: Anatomy of 3-Candle & Multi-Candle CRT Models [00:03:40 - 00:06:46]
模組三：三根 K 線與多根 K 線 CRT 模型解剖 [00:03:40 - 00:06:46]

Concept Explanation
【概念解釋】

A standard CRT model compresses AMD into a three-candle sequence on 1H+ charts, though prolonged consolidations can stretch the sequence across four or more candles while preserving identical mechanics.

標準 CRT 模型將 AMD 濃縮於 1 小時以上時區的三根 K 線序列中；若盤整時間較長，序列也可能延伸至四根或更多 K 線，但其底層運作邏輯完全一致。

Execution Workflow
【關鍵步驟/操作流程】

1. Candle 1 (Range Candle): Forms a clear high and low. 2. Candle 2 (Manipulation Candle): Wicks beyond Candle 1's low (for bullish CRT) or high (for bearish CRT) to sweep liquidity, then closes back inside Candle 1's range. 3. Candle 3 (Distribution Candle): Expands aggressively toward the opposite extreme of Candle 1. (In multi-candle variants, inside bars appear between Candle 1 and the sweep candle).

1. 第 1 根（區間 K 線）：確立明確的高低點邊界。2. 第 2 根（操縱 K 線）：以影線刺穿第 1 根低點（看漲 CRT）或高點（看跌 CRT）掠奪流動性後，收盤拉回第 1 根區間內。3. 第 3 根（派發 K 線）：強勢向第 1 根區間的另一端擴展。（多根變體則在區間 K 線與掃掠 K 線之間夾雜孕線盤整）。

Key Terms & Formulas
【重要公式/專有名詞】

Three-Candle CRT, Inside Bar, Sweeping Candle, Sell-Side Liquidity Pool (SSL), Buy-Side Liquidity Pool (BSL).

三根 K 線 CRT、孕線 (Inside Bar)、掃掠 K 線 (Sweeping Candle)、賣方流動性池 (SSL)、買方流動性池 (BSL)。

Common Pitfalls
【常見誤區/注意事項】

If the second candle closes solidly outside the range candle instead of leaving a rejection wick and closing back inside, it signals a true breakout rather than a CRT manipulation sweep.

若第二根 K 線實體直接收盤在區間 K 線外側，而非留下影線並收盤拉回區間內部，則代表真突破而非 CRT 操縱掃掠，不可逆勢操作。
