1. Executive Summary
1. 核心總結 (Executive Summary)

This study note breaks down the intraday Order Flow trading methodology of 26-year-old American trader Chris Creamer, who won the Robbins World Cup Intraday Trading Championship in July and achieved a 31-day winning streak with over a 60% win rate.

本學習筆記深度拆解 26 歲美國交易員 Chris Creamer 的日內訂單流（Order Flow）交易方法論，他於 7 月奪得羅賓斯世界盃日內交易賽（Robbins World Cup）冠軍，並創下超過 60% 勝率與最長連勝 31 天的紀錄。

The core philosophy of his strategy is waiting for one side of the market to exhaust and fail at key institutional levels before entering in the opposite direction to capture trapped traders' stop-loss momentum.

該策略的核心哲學在於「等待市場一方在關鍵機構價位發力失敗後進場」，藉此捕捉被套牢交易者觸發止損（Stop-Loss）所引發的反向推升動能。

He structures his trading into a strict four-step workflow: Trend, Location, Confirmation, and Execution, where the first two steps are completed pre-market using Value Area (VA) and Gamma Exposure (GEX).

他將交易嚴格劃分為「趨勢（Trend）、位置（Location）、確認（Confirmation）、執行（Execution）」四個步驟，其中前兩步必須在開盤前透過價值區（Value Area）與 Gamma 敞口（GEX）規劃完畢。

During the New York open (first 1.5 hours), he uses 5-minute Footprint Charts to confirm absorption, candle reversal, and aggressive Delta imbalance (>4x) within Fibonacci discount/premium zones (0.75–0.886), maintaining a 1.5R to 2R risk-reward ratio and pyramiding strictly on winning trades.

在紐約開盤後的前 1.5 小時內，他利用 5 分鐘足跡圖（Footprint Chart）在費波那契（Fibonacci）0.75 至 0.886 折價/溢價區間確認「吸收現象、K 線反轉與主動買賣盤失衡（4 倍以上）」，維持 1.5 至 2 倍盈虧比，並僅在獲利部位上進行順勢加倉。

2. Key Concepts & Definitions (With Timestamps)
2. 知識地圖與核心概念 (Key Concepts & Definitions)

[00:00:21] Order Flow (訂單流): A real-time trading analysis method that tracks actual buy and sell market orders executed at each price level rather than relying solely on lagging price indicators.

[00:00:21] **[Order Flow（訂單流）]**：一種即時交易分析方法，直接追蹤每個價位實際成交的主動買單與主動賣單，而非僅依賴滯後的價格技術指標。

[00:00:52] Value Area (VA / 價值區): The price range in which 70% of a trading day's total volume was traded, used on 1H and 4H charts to determine daily market structure (uptrend, downtrend, or consolidation).

[00:00:52] **[Value Area（VA / 價值區）]**：單日 70% 成交量所集中的價格區間，在 1 小時與 4 小時圖上用於判斷大級別結構是逐日墊高（上升）、逐日降低（下降）或橫盤震盪。

[00:01:10] Gamma Exposure (GEX / Gamma 敞口): An options-derived metric estimating market makers' hedging direction; Positive GEX suppresses volatility (sell high, buy low), while Negative GEX amplifies volatility (buy high, sell low).

[00:01:10] **[Gamma Exposure（GEX / Gamma 敞口）]**：根據期權持倉推算的造市商（Market Maker）對沖方向；正 Gamma 會壓抑波動（高賣低買），負 Gamma 則會放大波動並加速單邊行情（追漲殺跌）。

[00:01:32] Call Wall, Put Wall & Zero Gamma Line (看漲期權牆、看跌期權牆與零 Gamma 線): Key pre-market option levels where Call/Put open interest concentrates (acting as major resistance/support) and the inflection line separating positive and negative Gamma regimes.

[00:01:32] **[Call Wall / Put Wall / Zero Gamma Line（看漲期權牆 / 看跌期權牆 / 零 Gamma 線）]**：開盤前必標的三大期權價位，分別代表期權倉位最集中的阻力位、支撐位，以及正負 Gamma 波動狀態的分界線。

[00:01:54] Discount Zone & Premium Zone (折價區與溢價區): Price regions below the Value Area (Discount, for buying) and above the Value Area (Premium, for selling), refined using Fibonacci retracement levels (0.75, 0.786, 0.886).

[00:01:54] **[Discount Zone & Premium Zone（折價區與溢價區）]**：位於價值區下方的低估區間（折價區，只做多）與價值區上方的高估區間（溢價區，只做空），並結合費波那契（Fibonacci）0.75、0.786、0.886 精確定位。

[00:02:23] Footprint Chart & Delta (足跡圖與 Delta 淨主動成交量): An order flow candlestick chart displaying aggressive market buy vs. market sell volume at every price tick; Delta equals Aggressive Buys minus Aggressive Sells.

[00:02:23] **[Footprint Chart & Delta（足跡圖與淨主動成交量）]**：一種顯示每根 K 線內部各價位主動買入與主動賣出數量的訂單流圖表；Delta 即為「主動買單減去主動賣單」的淨差額。

[00:02:46] Absorption (訂單吸收): A phenomenon where massive aggressive selling (heavy negative Delta) occurs at the bottom wick of a candle, yet price refuses to drop further because passive limit buy orders absorb all supply.

[00:02:46] **[Absorption（訂單吸收）]**：K 線下影線出現大量負 Delta（賣方瘋狂砸盤），但價格卻跌不下去的現象，代表下方有大型被動限價買單將所有賣壓全數承接吸收。

[00:03:00] Diagonal Imbalance / Acceleration (斜對角失衡 / 數字加速): An order flow confirmation trigger where aggressive buying at a specific price level exceeds diagonal aggressive selling by more than 4 times (400%).

[00:03:00] **[Diagonal Imbalance（斜對角失衡 / 數字加速）]**：訂單流進場觸發條件，指某個價位的主動買單數量達到斜對角主動賣單的 4 倍（400%）以上，顯示買方動能正在急速爆發。

[00:03:27] Point of Control (POC / 控制點): The single price level within the Value Area that recorded the highest traded volume, serving as the primary take-profit target when price returns inside the Value Area.

[00:03:27] **[Point of Control（POC / 控制點）]**：價值區內成交量最大的單一價位，當價格從折價區或溢價區重返價值區時，POC 作為首要止盈目標。

1. Trapped Traders & Forced Liquidation Model (被套牢者與強制平倉燃料模型)
1. 被套牢者與強制平倉燃料模型 (Trapped Traders & Forced Liquidation Model)

Why: Markets move fastest when aggressive participants are proven wrong. How: Instead of predicting bottoms, Chris waits for sellers to dump heavy volume at support without breaking it. Once absorbed, those short sellers are trapped; their mandatory buy-to-cover stop orders act as rocket fuel for the upside reversal.

【底層邏輯 Why】：市場走勢最凌厲的時刻，往往發生在主動進場的一方被證明判斷錯誤之時。【分析方式 How】：他不盲目猜底，而是等空頭在支撐位大幅砸盤卻跌不下去；一旦賣單被完全吸收，這批空頭即陷入套牢，他們被迫觸發止損的買回平倉單，就會成為推動價格向上反轉的天然燃料。

2. Auction Market Theory & Value Migration (拍賣市場理論與價值遷移模型)
2. 拍賣市場理論與價值遷移模型 (Auction Market Theory & Value Migration)

Why: Price is merely an advertising mechanism seeking fair value where 70% of volume occurs (Value Area). How: By tracking whether daily Value Areas are stepping higher or lower on 1H/4H charts, he aligns with institutional directional bias and only buys at an unfair low (Discount) expecting a reversion back to fair value (POC).

【底層邏輯 Why】：價格只是市場尋找公允價值（即 70% 成交量所在的價值區）的媒介。【分析方式 How】：透過觀察 1 小時與 4 小時圖上每日價值區的移動軌跡，確認機構大資金的趨勢方向，並堅持只在低於公允價值的「折價區」買入，賺取價格回歸價值中心（POC）的高勝率波段。

3. 造市商對沖與波動率環境模型 (Dealer Hedging & Volatility Regime Filter)

Why: Options market makers must dynamically delta-hedge their books, directly dictating intraday volatility. How: GEX tells him 'how fast' price will move, not 'which direction'. In Positive GEX, he expects mean reversion and fades extremes; in Negative GEX, he prepares for rapid directional expansion and avoids catching falling knives prematurely.

【底層邏輯 Why】：期權造市商必須對其部位進行動態對沖，這直接決定了日內行情的波動特性。【分析方式 How】：GEX 用於判斷「波動大小」而非「漲跌方向」。在正 Gamma 環境下預期震盪回歸（突破易失敗）；在負 Gamma 環境下則防範單邊急漲急跌，順勢追蹤快速行情。

Why: Doubling an account in a trading competition requires capturing outsized winners while capping drawdowns. How: He initiates positions with tiny risk (e.g., $100), moves stop-loss to breakeven once in profit, and adds size exclusively to winning trades—never averaging down on losers.

Module 2 [00:00:48 – 00:01:48]: Step 1 — Pre-Market Trend & Gamma Exposure (GEX)
模組二 [00:00:48 – 00:01:48]：第一步（開盤前）— 判斷趨勢與 Gamma 敞口分析

Concept Explanation: Trend determination combines Auction Market structure (Value Area migration) with Options Market Maker positioning (GEX) to forecast both directional bias and expected intraday volatility.

【概念解釋】：趨勢判斷結合了拍賣市場結構（價值區移動軌跡）與期權造市商持倉結構（GEX），藉此同時掌握「交易方向」與「日內波動劇烈程度」。

Key Steps / Workflow: 1) Check daily Value Areas (70% volume zone) on 1H/4H charts: rising VAs = look only for longs; falling VAs = look only for shorts; 2) Check GEX: Positive GEX means dealers sell highs/buy lows (low volatility, breakouts fail); Negative GEX means dealers chase moves (high volatility, fast trends); 3) Mark Call Wall, Put Wall, and Zero Gamma Line; 4) Write down concrete Bullish, Bearish, and Range plans before the open.

【關鍵步驟/操作流程】：1) 在 1H/4H 圖觀察每日價值區（70% 成交量區間）：逐日墊高只找做多機會，逐日降低只找做空機會；2) 判讀 GEX：正 Gamma 時造市商高賣低買（波動被壓縮、突破易失敗），負 Gamma 時造市商追漲殺跌（波動放大、行情極快）；3) 標出看漲期權牆、看跌期權牆與零 Gamma 線；4) 開盤前寫好上漲、下跌、震盪三種應對劇本。

Key Terms: Value Area (VA / 價值區 = 70% Daily Volume), Gamma Exposure (GEX / Gamma 敞口), Call Wall (看漲期權牆), Put Wall (看跌期權牆), Zero Gamma Line (零 Gamma 分界線).

【重要公式/專有名詞】：Value Area（價值區 = 單日 70% 成交量區間）、GEX（Gamma 敞口）、Call Wall（看漲期權牆）、Put Wall（看跌期權牆）、Zero Gamma Line（零 Gamma 線）。

Common Pitfalls: Misinterpreting GEX as a directional indicator—Positive or Negative GEX only measures volatility magnitude, not whether price will go up or down.

【常見誤區/注意事項】：切勿將正負 Gamma 誤當作漲跌方向指標——正負 Gamma 僅反映波動率大小（震盪或單邊加速），與多空方向無關。

Module 3 [00:01:48 – 00:02:18]: Step 2 — Pre-Market Location & Fibonacci Zones
模組三 [00:01:48 – 00:02:18]：第二步（開盤前）— 尋找進場位置與費波那契折價區

Concept Explanation: Even in a strong uptrend, buying inside or above the Value Area offers poor risk-reward. You must wait for price to pull back into an institutional Discount Zone (or Premium Zone for shorts).

【概念解釋】：即使處於明確的上升結構，在價值區內部或上方追高也會導致盈虧比惡化。必須耐心等待價格回調至機構「折價區」（做空則等反彈至「溢價區」）。

Key Steps / Workflow: 1) Identify Discount Zone (below Value Area) for longs, or Premium Zone (above Value Area) for shorts; 2) Draw Fibonacci retracement from the recent swing low to swing high; 3) Highlight the 0.75, 0.786, and 0.886 zone; 4) Verify this zone sits outside the Value Area and aligns with a Low Volume Node (LVN) where price previously moved rapidly; 5) Cancel the trade setup if price breaks below 0.886.

【關鍵步驟/操作流程】：1) 鎖定價值區下方的折價區（做多）或上方的溢價區（做空）；2) 從近期波段低點拉費波那契回撤至波段高點；3) 框出 0.75、0.786、0.886 之間的黃金進場帶；4) 確認該區間必須位於價值區外部，且最好是先前價格快速衝過、成交量稀疏的低量區（LVN）；5) 若價格直接跌破 0.886，該筆交易計畫立即作廢。

Key Terms: Discount Zone (折價區), Premium Zone (溢價區), Fibonacci Retracement Levels (0.75 / 0.786 / 0.886), Low Volume Node (LVN / 低成交量節點).

【重要公式/專有名詞】：Discount Zone（折價區）、Premium Zone（溢價區）、Fibonacci（0.75 / 0.786 / 0.886 關鍵回撤位）、Low Volume Node（低成交量區）。
