[00:01:01] Stair-Step Pattern & High Tight Flag (階梯式上升與高緊旗形): 強勢領漲股在 1 至 3 個月內大漲 20%–100% 後，進入為期 2 週至 2 個月的橫盤整理，期間低點逐步抬高、波動收窄，隨後沿著 EMA 10/20/50（指數移動平均線）多頭排列突破起漲。

·        [00:02:45] Average Daily Range / Average True Range (ADR / ATR, 平均日波動幅度 / 平均真實波幅): A volatility metric used to cap maximum stop-loss width; a trade's stop-loss distance must not exceed 1x ADR/ATR to prevent distorted risk-reward ratios.

[00:02:45] Average Daily Range / Average True Range (ADR / ATR, 平均日波動幅度 / 平均真實波幅): 衡量標的每日波動幅度的指標，用於限制最大止損寬度；單筆交易的止損距離絕不應超過 1 倍 ADR/ATR，以防止盈虧比失衡。

4. Mental Models & Underlying Logic (Why & How)
4. 底層邏輯與思維模型

·        Asymmetric Expectancy over Win Rate (不對稱期望值優先於勝率思維): Why: Markets spend most of their time in noise, making high win rates unnecessary for massive wealth. How: Traders like Kristjan (35% win rate) and Bit Langlang (42% win rate) accept frequent small stop-outs (0.25%–1% account risk) in exchange for capturing multi-standard-deviation trend extensions where single winners cover dozens of losses.

Asymmetric Expectancy over Win Rate (不對稱期望值優先於勝率思維): 【為何如此】：市場多數時間處於無序雜訊中，追求高勝率往往導致「贏小錢、虧大錢」。【如何運作】：Kristjan（勝率 35%）與比特浪浪（勝率 42%）主動接受頻繁的小額止損（單筆風險 0.25%–1%），以此換取捕捉數倍波幅的趨勢主升浪，單筆大賺即可覆蓋數十次試錯成本。

·        Volatility Contraction as Supply Exhaustion (波動收縮即賣壓枯竭模型): Why: Explosive breakouts occur only when floating supply is absorbed by institutional demand. How: Both Kristjan's tight flags and Minervini's VCP look for progressively shallower pullbacks and drying volume—proving sellers are exhausted before triggering entries on volume expansion.

Volatility Contraction as Supply Exhaustion (波動收縮即賣壓枯竭模型): 【為何如此】：唯有當市場上的浮動籌碼被主力資金吸收殆盡時，價格才會出現爆發性突破。【如何運作】：無論是 Kristjan 的收窄整理或 Minervini 的 VCP 模型，皆透過觀察回調幅度逐次縮小與成交量極度萎縮，確認賣方拋壓枯竭後，才在放量突破瞬間切入。

·        Multi-Timeframe Alignment & Momentum Exhaustion (多週期共振與動能衰竭模型): Why: Price reversals rarely happen while higher-timeframe momentum is still accelerating. How: Banmu Xia (3-segment MACD divergence after 5 Elliott waves) and Jack Corsellis (slow Stochastic 60 > 85 confirming macro trend while fast Stochastic 9 hits < 20) align macro direction with micro inflection points.

Multi-Timeframe Alignment & Momentum Exhaustion (多週期共振與動能衰竭模型): 【為何如此】：當大週期動能仍在加速時，價格極難直接反轉。【如何運作】：半木夏利用五浪結構後的 MACD 三段背離確認趨勢動能徹底耗盡；Jack Corsellis 則以慢速隨機指標（60 週期 > 85）鎖定宏觀多頭，同時等待快速隨機指標（9 週期 < 20）回調超賣，實現順大勢、逆小勢的精準擊發。

·        Capital Partitioning & Behavioral Insulation (資金隔離與情緒絕緣模型): Why: Psychological tilt caused by oversized drawdowns destroys execution consistency. How: Bit Langlang splits trading capital into three equal parts (withdrawing profits or topping up losses to maintain constant position size), while Umar mandates step-wise scaling (+20–30%) and cooling-off breaks after winning streaks to prevent overconfidence.

Capital Partitioning & Behavioral Insulation (資金隔離與情緒絕緣模型): 【為何如此】：單次重倉回撤引發的情緒失控是交易員破產的主因。【如何運作】：比特浪浪將合約資金分為三等份（賺了提走、虧了補足，維持固定開倉額度），從物理層面隔離爆倉風險並穩定心態；Umar 則規定連勝後主動減倉或休整，並以階梯式（每次增加 20%–30%）放大倉位，防止過度自信導致利潤回吐。
