深度學習筆記：簡化流動性概念（聰明錢概念 SMC 與價格行為 Price Action）

1. Executive Summary
1. 核心總結 (Executive Summary)

This tutorial demystifies Liquidity (流動性) within Smart Money Concepts (SMC, 聰明錢概念) and Price Action (價格行為), explaining how major financial institutions manipulate retail traders to execute massive orders. Because institutions require substantial counterparty orders to enter or exit positions, they intentionally engineer price movements to trigger retail Stop Loss (止損單) pools before resuming the true market trend. The video categorizes liquidity into Dynamic Liquidity (動態流動性)—the continuous price cycle between External Liquidity (外部流動性, swing highs/lows) and Internal Liquidity (內部流動性, Fair Value Gaps 公平價值缺口)—and Static Liquidity (靜態流動性), which represents resting order pools at predictable chart structures. Specifically, the presenter breaks down two high-probability retail traps: Equal Highs/Lows (等高點/等低點, forming double/triple tops and bottoms) and Trendline Liquidity (趨勢線流動性). The ultimate actionable takeaway is patience: traders must avoid entering prematurely on classic retail patterns, treat un-swept zones as Inducement (誘導區), and execute trades at extreme Supply or Demand Zones (供給區/需求區) only after a confirmed Liquidity Sweep (流動性獵取) has cleared retail stops.

本教學深入剖析「聰明錢概念」（Smart Money Concepts, SMC）與「價格行為」（Price Action）中的核心要素——「流動性」（Liquidity），揭示大型金融機構如何利用散戶心理來執行龐大訂單。由於機構進出場需要海量對手盤，他們會刻意製造價格假象，觸發散戶密集的「止損單」（Stop Loss）池以獲取動能，隨後才恢復真實趨勢。影片將流動性劃分為兩大類：一是「動態流動性」（Dynamic Liquidity），即價格在「外部流動性」（External Liquidity，波段高低點）與「內部流動性」（Internal Liquidity，「公允價值缺口」Fair Value Gaps, FVG）之間不斷循環的驅動力；二是「靜態流動性」（Static Liquidity），指停留在特定圖表結構的掛單與止損池。講者重點拆解了兩種最常見的散戶陷阱：「等高點/等低點」（Equal Highs/Lows，即雙重與三重頂底）與「趨勢線流動性」（Trendline Liquidity）。最終的核心結論是「耐心等待確認」：交易者應避免在傳統形態出現時過早進場，而應將未經獵取的區域視為「誘導區」（Inducement），唯有在確認「流動性獵取」（Liquidity Sweep）完成、散戶止損被清算後，方可在極端的「供給區或需求區」（Supply/Demand Zones）進場交易。

 

2. Key Concepts & Definitions (With Timestamps)
2. 知識地圖與核心概念 (Key Concepts & Definitions)（附時間戳記）

·        [00:01:21] Smart Money (聰明錢): Major financial institutions and central banks with massive capital capable of moving markets, requiring large pools of retail orders as counterparties to fill their positions.

[00:01:21] Smart Money（聰明錢）：指擁有龐大資金、足以推動市場走勢的大型金融機構與銀行，他們需要大量散戶訂單作為對手盤才能完成建倉或平倉。

·        [00:01:39] Liquidity (流動性): The availability of resting orders (especially retail Stop Loss orders and pending orders) in the market that institutions absorb to execute large buy or sell transactions.

[00:01:39] Liquidity（流動性）：市場中可供成交的停留訂單（特別是散戶的止損單與掛單），機構透過吸收這些訂單來執行大額買賣交易。

·        [00:02:39] Dynamic Liquidity (動態流動性): The market forces that drive price direction and movement, operating as a continuous cycle between internal liquidity and external liquidity.

[00:02:39] Dynamic Liquidity（動態流動性）：驅動市場方向與價格波動的內在力量，表現為價格在內部流動性與外部流動性之間不斷循環的過程。

·        [00:02:55] External Liquidity (外部流動性): Liquidity resting above major swing highs and below major swing lows within a price wave.

[00:02:55] External Liquidity（外部流動性）：位於價格波段中主要波段高點上方與主要波段低點下方的流動性。

·        [00:03:02] Internal Liquidity / Fair Value Gaps (FVG) (內部流動性 / 公允價值缺口): Price inefficiencies or gaps located inside the range between a swing high and swing low, which price returns to fill for market balance.

[00:03:02] Internal Liquidity / Fair Value Gaps, FVG（內部流動性 / 公允價值缺口）：位於波段高點與低點區間內部的價格失衡區或缺口，價格會回調填補這些缺口以尋求市場平衡。

·        [00:04:41] Static Liquidity (靜態流動性): Specific chart zones where large pools of money (stop losses and pending orders) rest passively, such as equal highs/lows, trendlines, and support/resistance levels.

[00:04:41] Static Liquidity（靜態流動性）：市場中大量資金被動停留的特定圖表區域，例如等高點/等低點、趨勢線以及支撐與阻力位。

·        [00:04:55] Liquidity Sweep (流動性獵取): A price movement that intentionally pierces a key high, low, or pattern to trigger retail stop losses and absorb liquidity before reversing in the true trend direction.

[00:04:55] Liquidity Sweep（流動性獵取）：價格刻意刺穿關鍵高點、低點或圖表形態，以觸發散戶止損單並吸收流動性，隨後反轉朝真實趨勢前進的行為。

·        [00:04:55] Point of Interest (POI) / Supply & Demand Zones (興趣點 / 供給區與需求區): High-probability institutional order zones (Order Blocks) where traders look to enter positions, which are only valid if preceded by a liquidity sweep.

[00:04:55] Point of Interest, POI / Supply & Demand Zones（興趣點 / 供給區與需求區）：交易者尋求進場的高勝率機構訂單區（訂單塊），但前提是價格到達該區前必須先發生流動性獵取。

·        [00:06:25] Equal Lows (EQL) & Equal Highs (EQH) (等低點與等高點): Horizontal price levels where the market forms double/triple bottoms or tops, attracting massive retail stop-loss pools just beyond the level.

[00:06:25] Equal Lows, EQL & Equal Highs, EQH（等低點與等高點）：市場在同一水平價位形成雙重/三重底或頂的結構，在該水平線外側吸引龐大的散戶止損池。

·        [00:07:12] Break of Structure (BOS) (結構突破): A decisive price break beyond a previous structural high (in an uptrend) or low (in a downtrend), confirming trend continuation.

[00:07:12] Break of Structure, BOS（結構突破）：價格強勢突破前一個結構高點（上升趨勢）或跌破前一個結構低點（下降趨勢），用以確認趨勢延續。

·        [00:10:55] Inducement Area (誘導區): An un-swept decisional order block or minor structure designed to lure early retail traders in, acting as fuel (liquidity) before price reaches the true extreme POI.

[00:10:55] Inducement Area（誘導區）：未經流動性獵取的決策性訂單塊或次要結構，旨在引誘散戶過早進場，成為價格抵達真正極端興趣點（POI）前的動能燃料。

·        [00:14:40] Trendline Liquidity (趨勢線流動性): A series of closely spaced minor highs or lows forming a diagonal trendline, accumulating a dense cluster of retail stop losses right behind the line.

[00:14:40] Trendline Liquidity（趨勢線流動性）：由一連串緊密排列的次要高點或低點所形成的斜向趨勢線，在趨勢線外側累積了密集的散戶止損單。

4. Mental Models & Logic
4. 底層邏輯與思維模型 (Mental Models & Logic)

1. The Counterparty & Liquidity Fuel Model (對手盤與流動性燃料模型): Why: Markets do not move simply because of chart patterns; they move because institutions need liquidity to match their massive order sizes. How: Institutions identify where retail traders cluster their Stop Loss orders (which are market orders waiting to be triggered) and push price into those pools to absorb liquidity before driving the real trend.

1. 對手盤與流動性燃料模型（Why & How）：【底層原因】市場波動並非源於傳統圖表形態的魔力，而是因為機構需要龐大的對手盤才能成交巨額訂單。【運作方式】機構會精準鎖定散戶止損單（本質上是待觸發的市價單）最密集的區域，先將價格推向該流動性池進行吸收，獲取足夠動能後再發動真正的主趨勢。

2. The Market Balance Pendulum: Internal vs. External Liquidity (市場平衡鐘擺：內外部流動性循環): Why: Institutional algorithmic delivery seeks both liquidity acquisition and price efficiency. How: Price operates in a closed loop—after sweeping External Liquidity (swing highs/lows), price becomes inefficient and must retrace to Internal Liquidity (Fair Value Gaps) to rebalance, and vice versa.

2. 市場平衡鐘擺：內外部流動性循環：【底層原因】機構演算法報價同時追求「獲取流動性」與「價格效率平衡」。【運作方式】價格在閉環中運行——當價格獵取完「外部流動性」（波段高低點）後，會留下價格失衡，因此必須回調至「內部流動性」（FVG）進行再平衡，反之亦然。

3. First-Order vs. Second-Order Thinking (Inducement Filter) (一階思維 vs. 二階思維：誘導過濾器): Why: Retail traders use first-order thinking (e.g., 'Double bottom means buy now'), making their behavior predictable. How: Smart Money traders apply second-order thinking—asking 'Where are the retail traders trapped?' Any Order Block or Supply/Demand zone without a prior Liquidity Sweep is reclassified as an Inducement Area rather than a valid entry.

3. 一階思維 vs. 二階思維（誘導過濾器）：【底層原因】散戶習慣一階思維（例如「看到雙重底就立刻買進」），導致行為高度可預測。【運作方式】聰明錢交易者運用二階思維，自問「散戶的止損設在哪裡？」。任何在價格抵達前沒有發生「流動性獵取」的訂單塊或供需區，都會被重新定義為「誘導區」（Inducement），而非有效進場點。

Module 1: Institutional Psychology & The Essence of Liquidity [00:01:09 - 00:02:29]
模組一：機構心理學與流動性的本質 [00:01:09 - 00:02:29]

·        Concept Explanation: Smart Money (major financial institutions) cannot execute large buy or sell positions without sufficient counterparty orders. Retail Stop Loss orders provide this exact liquidity.

【概念解釋】：聰明錢（大型金融機構）若沒有足夠的對手盤訂單，便無法執行大額買賣部位。散戶設置的止損單（Stop Loss）正好提供了這筆所需的流動性。

·        Operational Process: 1) Institutions engineer a false price move that lures retail traders into thinking they understand Price Action. 2) Price reverses toward retail stop-loss clusters. 3) Once stops are triggered and absorbed, the market resumes its true trend.

【關鍵步驟/操作流程】：1) 機構製造虛假價格走勢，誘導散戶誤以為看懂了價格行為並進場；2) 價格反向朝散戶止損密集區移動；3) 一旦止損被觸發並吸收完畢，市場便恢復原本的主趨勢。

·        Key Terminology: Smart Money (聰明錢), Stop Loss Orders (止損單), Price Action (價格行為), Retail Illusion (散戶錯覺).

【重要公式/專有名詞】：Smart Money（聰明錢）、Stop Loss Orders（止損單）、Price Action（價格行為）。

·        Common Pitfalls: Entering trades based purely on surface-level chart patterns without asking where the market's liquidity pool is resting.

【常見誤區/注意事項】：僅憑表面圖表形態就盲目進場，卻沒有思考市場當前的流動性池停留在哪個位置。

Module 2: Dynamic Liquidity — The Internal & External Cycle [00:02:29 - 00:04:13]
模組二：動態流動性——內部與外部流動性的循環 [00:02:29 - 00:04:13]

·        Concept Explanation: Dynamic liquidity governs market direction through a continuous loop between External Liquidity (swing highs and lows) and Internal Liquidity (Fair Value Gaps / FVG inside the wave).

【概念解釋】：動態流動性透過「外部流動性」（波段高點與低點）與「內部流動性」（波段內部的公允價值缺口 FVG）之間的持續循環，主導著市場的運行方向。

·        Operational Process: 1) Price rallies to sweep External Liquidity above a previous swing high. 2) Seeking balance, price retraces inward to fill the Internal Liquidity (FVG). 3) From the FVG, price launches again to sweep the next External Liquidity high.

【關鍵步驟/操作流程】：1) 價格上漲並獵取前波高點上方的外部流動性；2) 為尋求市場平衡，價格向內回調並填補內部流動性（FVG）；3) 獲得支撐後，價格再度啟動並獵取下一個外部流動性高點。

·        Key Formula / Logic: Market Cycle = External Liquidity (Swing High/Low) <-> Internal Liquidity (Fair Value Gap, FVG).

【重要公式/專有名詞】：市場循環公式 ＝ 外部流動性（波段高低點）↔ 內部流動性（公允價值缺口 FVG）。

·        Common Pitfalls: Chasing breakouts immediately after External Liquidity is swept, right before price pulls back to fill the FVG.

【常見誤區/注意事項】：在外部流動性剛被獵取後立刻追高突破，結果正好買在價格準備回調填補 FVG 的起跌點。

Module 3: Static Liquidity & The Confirmation Rule [00:04:41 - 00:06:20]
模組三：靜態流動性與進場確認法則 [00:04:41 - 00:06:20]

·        Concept Explanation: Static liquidity refers to passive pools of money resting at well-known technical levels. A Liquidity Sweep before reaching a Point of Interest (POI) is mandatory to validate that zone.

【概念解釋】：靜態流動性指停留在知名技術價位的被動資金池。價格在抵達興趣點（POI）之前必須先發生「流動性獵取」，才能驗證該區域的有效性。

·        Operational Process: 1) Map static liquidity zones: Equal Highs/Lows, Swing Highs/Lows, Trendlines/Channels, Order Blocks, Support/Resistance, Fibonacci levels, and Daily candle bodies/wicks. 2) Check if price sweeps liquidity prior to tapping your Supply/Demand POI.

【關鍵步驟/操作流程】：1) 在圖表標記靜態流動性區域（等高低點、波段高低點、趨勢線/通道、訂單塊、支撐阻力、斐波那契位、日線實體與影線）；2) 檢查價格在觸及供給/需求 POI 之前是否已先獵取流動性。

·        Key Terminology: Static Liquidity (靜態流動性), Point of Interest (POI, 興趣點), Order Blocks (訂單塊), Fibonacci Retracement (斐波那契回撤).

【重要公式/專有名詞】：Static Liquidity（靜態流動性）、Point of Interest / POI（興趣點）、Order Blocks（訂單塊）。

·        Common Pitfalls: Trading a Supply or Demand zone that has no preceding liquidity sweep—price will likely smash right through your POI to use it as liquidity fuel.

【常見誤區/注意事項】：交易前方沒有發生流動性獵取的供需區——價格極可能會直接穿破你的 POI，將該區域本身當作流動性燃料。

Module 4: Bullish Equal Lows (Double/Triple Bottoms) & Inducement [00:06:20 - 00:12:46]
模組四：看漲等低點（雙重底/三重底）與誘導區實戰 [00:06:20 - 00:12:46]

·        Concept Explanation: After a bullish Break of Structure (BOS), price forms two or three internal lows at the same level (Equal Lows). Retail traders buy the double/triple bottom and place stops underneath, creating a Sell-side Liquidity pool above the true Extreme Demand Zone.

【概念解釋】：在看漲結構突破（BOS）後，價格在同一水平形成兩個或三個內部低點（等低點）。散戶在此買進雙重底或三重底並將止損設在下方，從而在真正的極端需求區上方創造出賣方流動性池。

·        Operational Process: 1) Identify bullish BOS and two demand zones (Decisional OB vs. Extreme OB). 2) Recognize Equal Lows and the un-swept Decisional OB as an Inducement Area. 3) Wait patiently for price to drop, sweep the Equal Lows and Decisional OB, and tap the Extreme Demand Zone. 4) Enter long after stops are cleared.

【關鍵步驟/操作流程】：1) 辨識看漲 BOS 及兩個潛在需求區（決策性訂單塊 vs. 極端訂單塊）；2) 將等低點與未獵取流動性的決策性訂單塊視為「誘導區」；3) 耐心等待價格下跌、獵取等低點下方止損並觸及極端需求區；4) 止損清算後進場做多。

·        Key Terminology: Equal Lows (EQL, 等低點), Double/Triple Bottom (雙重底/三重底), Decisional Order Block (決策性訂單塊), Inducement Area (誘導區), Extreme Order Block (極端訂單塊).

【重要公式/專有名詞】：Equal Lows / EQL（等低點）、Decisional Order Block（決策性訂單塊）、Inducement Area（誘導區）、Extreme Order Block（極端訂單塊）。

·        Common Pitfalls: Buying at the Decisional Order Block or immediately on the second/third bottom touch, getting stopped out right before the real rally begins.

【常見誤區/注意事項】：在決策性訂單塊或第二、三次觸底時過早買進，結果在真正大漲啟動前被精準掃損出場。

Module 5: Bearish Equal Highs (Double/Triple Tops) [00:12:46 - 00:14:33]
模組五：看跌等高點（雙重頂/三重頂）獵取策略 [00:12:46 - 00:14:33]

·        Concept Explanation: In a bearish trend following a downward BOS, price forms two or three Equal Highs just below an Unmitigated Supply Zone. Retail short-sellers place their buy-stop losses right above these equal highs.

【概念解釋】：在向下 BOS 的看跌趨勢中，價格在「未緩解供給區」下方形成兩個或三個等高點。進場做空的散戶將買入止損單密集設在這些等高點的正上方。

·        Operational Process: 1) Confirm bearish BOS and mark the upper Unmitigated Supply Zone. 2) Spot Double Top or Triple Top (Equal Highs) forming below the supply zone. 3) Wait for price to spike above the Equal Highs to sweep retail buy-side liquidity and tap the Supply Zone. 4) Execute a short position once liquidity is absorbed.

【關鍵步驟/操作流程】：1) 確認看跌 BOS 並標記上方的未緩解供給區；2) 觀察供給區下方形成的雙重頂或三重頂（等高點）；3) 等待價格向上刺穿等高點、獵取散戶止損並觸及供給區；4) 流動性吸收完畢後進場做空。

·        Key Terminology: Equal Highs (EQH, 等高點), Double/Triple Top (雙重頂/三重頂), Unmitigated Supply Zone (未緩解供給區), Buy-side Liquidity (買方流動性).

【重要公式/專有名詞】：Equal Highs / EQH（等高點）、Unmitigated Supply Zone（未緩解供給區）、Buy-side Liquidity（買方流動性）。

·        Common Pitfalls: Shorting prematurely on the double/triple top neckline or peak without waiting for the stop-hunt spike into the upper Supply Zone.

【常見誤區/注意事項】：未等待價格向上獵取止損並觸及上方供給區，就在雙重頂或三重頂的頂部過早做空。

Module 6: Trendline Liquidity — Bullish & Bearish Scenarios [00:14:33 - 00:17:46]
模組六：趨勢線流動性——看漲與看跌實戰情境 [00:14:33 - 00:17:46]

·        Concept Explanation: After a strong BOS that leaves behind a large price inefficiency (FVG), price often moves slowly in a choppy series of tiny BOS, forming a clean diagonal trendline. Retail traders repeatedly buy/sell trendline touches and stack stop losses right behind the trendline.

【概念解釋】：在留下巨大價格失衡區（FVG）的強勢 BOS 之後，價格常以微小且震盪的連續小 BOS 緩慢推進，形成一條明顯的斜向趨勢線。散戶每次觸及趨勢線便反覆進場，並將止損單密集排列在趨勢線外側。

·        Operational Process: 1) Identify a major BOS with an unmitigated extreme zone and an FVG/Inefficiency. 2) Spot a clean diagonal trendline formed by minor choppy structures without any liquidity sweeps. 3) Wait for price to aggressively break the trendline, sweep all aligned stops, fill the FVG, and tap the extreme Demand/Supply zone. 4) Enter in the dominant trend direction.

【關鍵步驟/操作流程】：1) 辨識帶有未緩解極端區與價格失衡區（FVG）的主要 BOS；2) 發現由微小震盪結構組成、且未經任何流動性獵取的斜向趨勢線；3) 等待價格強勢跌破/突破趨勢線，清算沿線止損、填補 FVG 並觸及極端供需區；4) 順應主趨勢進場。

·        Key Terminology: Trendline Liquidity (趨勢線流動性), Price Inefficiency (價格失衡區), Minor Break of Structure (次要結構突破).

【重要公式/專有名詞】：Trendline Liquidity（趨勢線流動性）、Price Inefficiency（價格失衡區）、Minor BOS（次要結構突破）。

·        Common Pitfalls: Treating a trendline breakout as a true trend reversal (e.g., shorting the breakdown of a bullish trendline) instead of recognizing it as a liquidity sweep into an extreme Demand Zone.

【常見誤區/注意事項】：將趨勢線的跌破或突破誤判為真實趨勢反轉（例如在看漲趨勢線跌破時追空），而未能識破這其實是價格向極端需求區邁進的流動性獵取。

6. Practical Action Items
6. 實操行動清單（Action Items）

1. Map Internal & External Liquidity Daily: Before placing any trade, mark the current swing high/low (External Liquidity) and highlight any unmitigated Fair Value Gaps (Internal Liquidity) to determine where price is currently gravitating.

1. 每日標記內外部流動性：在進行任何交易前，先在圖表標出當前的波段高點與低點（外部流動性），並框出未填補的公允價值缺口（內部流動性），以判斷價格當前的引力方向。

2. Apply the 'No Sweep, No Trade' Filter: Audit every Supply or Demand Point of Interest (POI) on your watchlist; if price has not swept liquidity prior to reaching that zone, reclassify it as an Inducement Area and do not place limit orders there.

2. 執行「無獵取、不交易」過濾法則：檢視觀察清單上的每一個供給或需求興趣點（POI）；若價格抵達該區前未發生流動性獵取，請將其重新標記為「誘導區」，切勿在該處掛限價單。

3. Hunt for Equal Highs/Lows Before Extreme Zones: Actively scan charts for double/triple tops and bottoms sitting just in front of unmitigated Order Blocks, using them as high-probability confirmation that institutional stop-hunts are imminent.

3. 在極端區前方尋找等高點/等低點：主動掃描圖表中位於未緩解訂單塊前方的雙重/三重頂與底，將它們作為機構即將發動止損獵取的高勝率確認訊號。

4. Use Retail Trendlines as Reverse Targets: Whenever you see a clean diagonal trendline formed by small, choppy price steps leaving an FVG behind, avoid trading trendline bounces and instead set alerts at the extreme Order Block beyond the trendline.

4. 將散戶趨勢線作為反向目標：每當看到由微小震盪步伐組成、且後方留有 FVG 的斜向趨勢線時，避免在趨勢線反彈時進場，改為在趨勢線外側的極端訂單塊設置價格警報。

5. Backtest 20 Historical Liquidity Sweeps: Open your charting platform, review 20 past reversals on your primary trading pair, and document how many occurred immediately after sweeping Equal Highs/Lows or Trendline Liquidity.

5. 回測 20 個歷史流動性獵取案例：打開看盤軟體，回顧主要交易品種過去 20 次重大反轉，記錄其中有多少次是緊接在獵取等高低點或趨勢線流動性之後發生的。
