How to Mark Key Liquidity Levels on the Chart

如何在圖表上標記關鍵流動性位階：深度學習筆記

Source: Video transcript (SRT) ・ Duration ≈ 14:23 ・ 來源：影片逐字稿，片長約 14 分 23 秒

1. 核心總結 Executive Summary

This video explains why price is drawn to liquidity and how to mark key liquidity levels. Smart money needs many counterparties to execute large orders, so price first gravitates toward clusters of stop losses and pending orders, sweeps them, and only then makes the major move. Liquidity is split into buy-side liquidity (above highs) and sell-side liquidity (below lows). Four levels should be marked: swing highs/lows, equal highs/lows, turning points, and daily highs/lows. The shared playbook is to wait for the sweep, look for reversal confirmation, enter, place the stop beyond the sweep, and target the next key level. Liquidity and market structure are inseparable, and rules plus risk management matter because the future is unpredictable.

本影片說明價格為何會被流動性（Liquidity）吸引，以及如何在圖表上標記關鍵流動性位階。聰明錢（Smart Money）需要大量對手單才能成交大單，因此價格會先被拉向止損單與預掛單密集處，掃除後才展開主要行情。流動性分為位於高點上方的買方流動性（BSL）與低點下方的賣方流動性（SSL），並需標記四類位階：波段高低點、等高低點、轉折點、每日高低點。共通的操作邏輯是：等待掃蕩（Sweep）、確認反轉、進場、止損設於掃蕩極值之外，並以下一個關鍵位階為目標。講者強調流動性與市場結構密不可分，且因未來不可預測，必須嚴守交易規則與風險管理。

2. 知識地圖與核心概念 Key Concepts & Definitions

[00:00:00] Liquidity: Areas with many willing buyers/sellers; price is drawn to them. Smart money needs them to fill large orders.

流動性：市場中有大量買家或賣家的區域，價格會被吸引過去；聰明錢需要它來成交大單。

[00:00:25] Smart Money: Large institutional participants that need many counterparties to sell or buy an asset.

聰明錢（機構資金）：需要大量對手單才能買賣資產的大型機構參與者。

[00:01:25] Liquidity Level: Chart areas where stop losses or pending orders are likely concentrated; they act as price magnets before a major move.

流動性位階：圖表上可能集中止損單或預掛單的區域，在重大行情前會像磁鐵般吸引價格。

[00:02:05] Buy-side Liquidity (BSL): Liquidity resting above a high or a group of highs (short stops, breakout buy orders).

買方流動性：位於單一高點或一組高點上方的流動性（空單止損與突破買單）。

[00:02:49] Sell-side Liquidity (SSL): Liquidity resting below a low or a group of lows (long stops, breakout sell orders).

賣方流動性：位於單一低點或一組低點下方的流動性（多單止損與突破賣單）。

[00:02:30] Buy-side / Sell-side Delivery: A sudden price move designed to grab buy-side (up) or sell-side (down) liquidity.

買方／賣方流動性交付：為獵取買方（向上）或賣方（向下）流動性而出現的突發價格走勢。

[00:02:39] Algorithmic Price Delivery: Price delivery that traps traders on both sides while engaging liquidity, before smart money takes control.

演算法價格交付：在聰明錢接管前，先觸發流動性並誘捕雙邊交易者的價格運行方式。

[00:02:13] Breakout Traders: Traders who enter when a key level is broken (long above highs, short below lows).

突破交易者：在關鍵位階被突破時進場的交易者（突破高點做多、跌破低點做空）。

[00:03:27] Liquidity Sweep / Grab: Price takes out the orders beyond a level; the market then tends to move away from it and toward levels still holding liquidity.

流動性掃蕩／掠奪：價格觸發位階外側的訂單；之後市場傾向遠離該位階，並移向仍有流動性的位階。

[00:04:03] Swing High / Swing Low: Three-candle turning points: the middle candle has the highest high (or lowest low), neighbors have lower highs and lows (or higher). Complete at the third candle's close.

波段高點／波段低點：由三根 K 線構成的轉折點：中間 K 線高點最高（或低點最低），前後兩根高低點皆較低（或較高），第三根收盤時完成。

[00:04:45] Five-candle Swing: A stricter swing pattern that makes swing points more powerful and reliable.

五根 K 線波段：更嚴格的波段型態，使波段點更強大且可靠。

[00:06:43] Equal Highs / Equal Lows: Highs or lows formed at about the same level (double or triple tops/bottoms); rich in stop-loss liquidity.

等高點／等低點：高點或低點落在大致相同位階（雙頂／雙底、三頂／三底），聚集大量止損流動性。

[00:05:31] Demand Zone / Supply Zone: Areas where buying (demand) or selling (supply) interest is expected; used as entry zones after a sweep.

需求區／供應區：預期出現買盤（需求）或賣盤（供應）的區域，常作為掃蕩後的進場區。

[00:08:43] Fair Value Gap (FVG): A price imbalance treated as an extreme demand zone; suitable for placing a limit order.

公允價值缺口：價格失衡區，被視為極端需求區，適合放置限價單。

[00:08:43] Buy Limit Order: A pending order to buy at a lower price, placed at the demand zone with the stop below it.

限價買單：在較低價位掛出的預掛買單，放在需求區並將止損設在其下方。

[00:10:02] Change of Character (CHoCH): A structure break normally signaling the trend is no longer intact; can be invalid when it is merely a liquidity sweep.

性格改變：通常代表原趨勢不再成立的結構破壞；若只是流動性掃蕩，則可能無效。

[00:10:15] Market Structure: The sequence of swings that defines trend; in SMC it is inseparable from liquidity.

市場結構：由波段構成、用來定義趨勢的結構；在 SMC 中與流動性密不可分。

[00:10:26] Turning Points: Areas where price reversed after a long-term trend; seen as overbought/oversold and rich in liquidity.

轉折點：價格在長期趨勢後反轉的區域，被視為超買／超賣，且流動性豐富。

[00:11:09] Market Makers' Manipulation: Tactics used to move price around turning points to trigger stops.

做市商操縱：在轉折點附近推動價格以觸發止損的手法。

[00:11:52] Fibonacci Retracement: A tool for refining take-profit levels.

斐波那契回撤：用來提高止盈目標精準度的工具。

[00:12:13] Daily High / Daily Low: Highest and lowest prices of the day; heavily watched, so stops and pending orders cluster around them.

每日高點／每日低點：當天最高價與最低價，受到高度關注，因此止損單與預掛單常聚集於此。

4. 底層邏輯與思維模型 Mental Models & Logic

1. Counterparty model (Why)

1. 對手盤模型（Why）

Large players cannot trade size in empty areas, so they go where orders are dense, just as a food truck moves to a stadium.

大資金無法在無人區成交大單，必須前往訂單密集處，就像餐車要搬到體育場外。

2. Magnet and fuel model

2. 磁鐵與燃料模型

Liquidity pulls price in, and the sweep supplies the fuel for the next move; if fuel is insufficient, price may drop further to collect more sell-side liquidity.

流動性吸引價格，而掃蕩提供下一波行情的燃料；若燃料不足，價格可能再下探收集更多賣方流動性。

3. Crowd-behavior mirror

3. 群眾行為映射模型

Predict where others place orders: long stops below lows, short stops above highs, breakout entries beyond levels. Where the crowd is predictable, liquidity is stored.

預測他人把單放在哪裡：多單止損在低點下、空單止損在高點上、突破單在位階外。群眾行為可預測之處就是流動性所在。

4. Sweep-then-reverse sequence (How)

4. 掃蕩後反轉的流程（How）

Do not enter at the obvious level; let retail stops be collected, then enter at a demand/supply zone after confirmation.

不在顯而易見的位階進場；等散戶止損被收集後，再於需求／供應區並待確認後進場。

5. Liquidity-over-structure rule

5. 流動性優先於結構

A break of equal lows normally looks like a CHoCH, but when it only serves to grab liquidity ahead of a demand zone, the CHoCH is invalid.

跌破等低點通常像 CHoCH，但若只是為了在需求區前獲取流動性，該 CHoCH 無效。

6. Directional bias filter

6. 方向偏誤過濾

In a bullish market, sell-side liquidity sweeps offer long entries in line with the trend; bearish scenarios mirror this.

看漲市場中，賣方流動性被掃蕩可提供順勢做多的機會；看跌則反向對稱。

7. Probabilistic mindset

7. 機率思維

Losses and missed trades are inevitable, so rules and risk management are what allow capturing winning opportunities.

虧損與錯失機會難以避免，唯有規則與風險管理才能把握獲利機會。

講者的分析步驟 How the Speaker Analyzes

1.      Identify where orders likely cluster.

判斷訂單可能集中的位置。

2.      Decide which side (BSL or SSL) is relevant to the dominant trend.

決定與主趨勢相關的是 BSL 還是 SSL。

3.      Anticipate the sweep and ignore the obvious retail entry.

預期掃蕩的發生，不在散戶明顯進場處進場。

4.      Find a demand/supply zone or FVG beyond the liquidity for entry.

在流動性之外尋找需求／供應區或 FVG 作為進場區。

5.      Confirm with reversal signs, protect with a stop beyond the sweep, and target the next key level.

以反轉訊號確認、將止損置於掃蕩之外，並瞄準下一個關鍵位階。

Module 2: Buy-side and Sell-side Liquidity

模組二：買方與賣方流動性　[00:01:34 – 00:03:58]

【概念解釋】Concept Explanation

[00:02:05] Traders enter pullbacks with stops below swing lows and take profits or short near swing highs, creating liquidity below lows and above highs.

交易者在回調時進場並把止損放在波段低點下方，並在波段高點附近止盈或做空，因而在低點下方與高點上方形成流動性。

[00:02:30] After a buy-side delivery, smart money takes control and goes short; after a sell-side delivery, it typically goes long toward buy-side liquidity.

買方交付後聰明錢掌控並做空；賣方交付後通常做多並瞄準買方流動性。

【關鍵步驟／操作流程】Key Steps / Workflow

1.      Mark BSL above highs and SSL below lows.

標記高點上方 BSL 與低點下方 SSL。

2.      Watch for delivery that engages the liquidity.

觀察是否出現觸發流動性的交付走勢。

3.      Trade in the direction smart money takes control after the sweep.

在掃蕩後順著聰明錢接管的方向交易。

【重要公式／專有名詞】Key Terms & Formulas

•      BSL / SSL, Buy-side / Sell-side Delivery, Algorithmic Price Delivery.

BSL／SSL、買賣方流動性交付、演算法價格交付。

【常見誤區／注意事項】Pitfalls & Notes

•      Price tends to move away from recently swept levels and toward levels that still contain liquidity.

價格傾向遠離剛被掃蕩的位階，並移向仍有流動性的位階。

•      In a bullish market, SSL is more important than BSL because a sweep below lows can signal continuation (00:03:38).

看漲時 SSL 比 BSL 更重要，因為低點下方被掃蕩可能預示延續上漲（00:03:38）。

Module 3: Swing Highs and Swing Lows

模組三：波段高點與波段低點　[00:03:58 – 00:06:04]

【概念解釋】Concept Explanation

[00:04:07] Swings are turning points formed by three candles, and the pattern completes when the third candle closes.

波段是由三根 K 線形成的轉折點，第三根收盤時型態完成。

[00:04:52] Their importance comes from psychology: buyers place stops below the area, sellers above it, and breakout traders enter when violated.

其重要性來自心理面：買方把止損放在下方、賣方放在上方，突破交易者則在被突破時進場。

【關鍵步驟／操作流程】Key Steps / Workflow

1.      Identify swing highs and lows with the three-candle rule (optionally five candles).

用三根 K 線規則（可選五根）識別波段高低點。

2.      Mark BSL above the high and SSL below the low.

在高點上方標記 BSL、低點下方標記 SSL。

3.      Bullish: look for swing lows forming before price taps a demand area (00:05:31).

看漲：價格觸及需求區前，期待先形成波段低點（00:05:31）。

4.      Bearish: look for swing highs forming on the way to a supply zone (00:05:54).

看跌：前往供應區途中，期待先形成波段高點（00:05:54）。

【重要公式／專有名詞】Key Terms & Formulas

•      Swing High / Swing Low, Five-candle Swing.

波段高點／低點、五根 K 線波段。

【常見誤區／注意事項】Pitfalls & Notes

•      If not enough liquidity is gathered, price may drop lower to collect more sell-side liquidity before rising (00:05:41).

若流動性累積不足，價格可能再下探收集更多賣方流動性後才上漲（00:05:41）。

Module 4: Equal Highs and Lows

模組四：等高點與等低點　[00:06:43 – 00:08:25]

【概念解釋】Concept Explanation

[00:07:01] Double or triple bottoms look like rejection spots, so retail traders buy there with stops below, which is exactly what smart money targets.

雙底或三底看似反彈點，散戶因此在此買進並把止損放在下方，而這正是聰明錢的目標。

[00:07:35] A core SMC pattern: let price collect retail stops, then enter at the demand area (mirrored for supply).

SMC 核心模式之一：等價格收集散戶止損，再於需求區進場（供應區則反向）。

【關鍵步驟／操作流程】Key Steps / Workflow

1.      Mark equal lows (or highs) and the zone beyond them.

標記等低點（或等高點）及其外側區域。

2.      Wait for the break and sweep of the liquidity.

等待價格跌破並掃蕩流動性。

3.      Enter at the demand zone below the equal lows (supply zone above equal highs).

在等低點下方的需求區（或等高點上方的供應區）進場。

【重要公式／專有名詞】Key Terms & Formulas

•      Equal Highs/Lows, Demand/Supply Zone.

等高低點、需求／供應區。

【常見誤區／注意事項】Pitfalls & Notes

•      Entering before liquidity is collected leaves you as part of the stops being swept (00:07:21).

在流動性被收集前進場，容易成為被掃蕩的止損之一（00:07:21）。

黃金 30 分鐘圖：明確上升趨勢，支撐多次拉升價格，止損集中於其下方，左側 FVG 為聰明錢進場區。

歐元/美元 15 分鐘圖：強勁上升趨勢，最近推動波形成等低點，FVG 位於流動性正下方。

Module 6: Turning Points

模組六：轉折點　[00:10:26 – 00:12:13]

【概念解釋】Concept Explanation

[00:10:33] Turning points are where price reversed after a long trend; viewed as overbought/oversold and watched by retail and institutions.

轉折點是長期趨勢後價格反轉之處，被視為超買／超賣，散戶與機構皆關注。

[00:10:54] Importance: stop-loss accumulation, psychological significance, and market-maker tactics around these levels.

重要性：止損累積、心理意義，以及做市商在該區的操作手法。

【關鍵步驟／操作流程】Key Steps / Workflow

1.      Identify key turning points (sharp past reversals) (00:11:09).

識別關鍵轉折點（過去急劇反轉處）（00:11:09）。

2.      Wait for a liquidity grab, often a spike beyond the point followed by a sharp reversal (00:11:22).

等待流動性掠奪，常見為插針穿越後急速反轉（00:11:22）。

3.      Wait for reversal signs: a strong momentum candle or CHoCH (00:11:33).

等待反轉訊號：強勢動能 K 線或 CHoCH（00:11:33）。

4.      Enter in the reversal direction with the stop just beyond the sweep (00:11:43).

順反轉方向進場，止損設在掃蕩極值之外（00:11:43）。

5.      Take profit at the next support/resistance, or use Fibonacci retracement (00:11:52).

止盈設在下一個支撐／阻力，或參考斐波那契回撤（00:11:52）。

Module 7: Daily High and Low

模組七：每日高點與低點　[00:12:13 – 00:13:58]

【概念解釋】Concept Explanation

[00:12:28] Many traders place stops or pending orders around the daily high/low, so these levels are full of liquidity.

許多交易者把止損或預掛單放在每日高低點周圍，使其充滿流動性。

[00:12:38] Near the daily high price may spike above it, take out short stops and trigger buy orders, then fall back; the daily low mirrors this.

接近每日高點時價格可能先衝破，掃除空單止損並觸發買單後再回落；每日低點則相反。

【關鍵步驟／操作流程】Key Steps / Workflow

1.      Mark the daily high and low on the chart (00:13:03).

在圖表上標記每日高低點（00:13:03）。

2.      Watch how price behaves near them; a brief break followed by a reversal is the setup (00:13:12).

觀察價格接近時的反應；短暫突破後反轉即為機會（00:13:12）。

3.      Look for confirmation: CHoCH or reversal patterns, and check the overall trend (00:13:18).

尋找確認訊號：CHoCH 或反轉型態，並確認整體趨勢（00:13:18）。

4.      Enter after the reversal, stop beyond the breakout, target the next key level (00:13:33).

反轉後進場，止損設在突破位之外，目標為下一關鍵位階（00:13:33）。

【重要公式／專有名詞】Key Terms & Formulas

•      Daily High / Daily Low, Reversal Pattern.

每日高／低點、反轉型態。

【常見誤區／注意事項】Pitfalls & Notes

•      Trade in the same direction as the overall trend, and rely on more than the level alone.

應順著整體趨勢交易，不能只靠位階本身。

6. 實操行動清單 Action Items

1.      Pick one chart and mark every swing high and swing low using the three-candle rule, then label BSL above highs and SSL below lows.

選一張圖表，用三根 K 線規則標出所有波段高低點，並在高點上方標 BSL、低點下方標 SSL。

2.      Before each session, mark equal highs/lows, turning points, and the daily high/low as a fixed checklist.

每個交易時段前，固定將等高低點、轉折點與每日高低點標記為檢查清單。

3.      When you see equal lows above a demand zone or FVG, do not buy at the lows; plan a buy limit at the zone with the stop below it.

看到需求區或 FVG 上方有等低點時，不要在低點買進；改在該區域規劃限價買單並把止損放在下方。

4.      Review at least 20 past sweeps of daily highs/lows or turning points, recording the reversal signal, stop beyond the sweep, and take-profit level.

回顧至少 20 次每日高低點或轉折點的掃蕩案例，記錄反轉訊號、掃蕩外側止損與止盈位階。

5.      Write down your setup rules and risk-management plan, check the overall trend before each trade, and follow them through losses.

寫下交易策略規則與風險管理計劃，每次交易前確認整體趨勢，並在虧損時也照規則執行。

答案與解析 Answers (Select the text to reveal / 選取文字即可顯示)

Q1: B. Smart money needs enough counterparties (stop losses and pending orders) to fill large orders, so price goes to those areas first.

Q1：B。聰明錢需要足夠的對手單（止損單與預掛單）才能成交大單，因此價格會先前往這些區域。

Q2: A. The middle candle makes the highest high, and the candles before and after have lower highs and lower lows; the pattern completes at the third candle's close.

Q2：A。中間 K 線創高點，前後 K 線的高低點皆較低；第三根 K 線收盤時型態完成。

Q3: B. Retail buys at equal lows with stops below; smart money sweeps those stops first, then price rises from the demand zone/FVG.

Q3：B。散戶會在等低點買進並把止損放在下方；聰明錢先掃除這些止損，之後價格從需求區／FVG 上升。

Q4: B. A sweep of sell-side liquidity in a bullish market often signals continuation and a long opportunity in line with the dominant trend.

Q4：B。看漲市場中掃蕩賣方流動性，往往預示延續上漲，並提供順應主趨勢做多的機會。

Check: a demand zone below the equal lows, the prevailing uptrend, and reversal signs after the sweep. Liquidity can make market structure levels less significant.

Q5（參考）：跌破可能只是為了掠奪等低點下方的流動性，且其下方有需求區／FVG，價格很可能掃蕩後再上漲。需檢查：等低點下方是否有需求區、是否處於上升趨勢，以及掃蕩後是否出現反轉訊號。流動性會讓市場結構位階的重要性降低。
