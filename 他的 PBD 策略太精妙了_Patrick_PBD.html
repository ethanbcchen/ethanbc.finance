1. Executive Summary
一、核心總結

This video breaks down the high-stability trading system of German top trader Patrick, a two-time champion and leading contender in the Robbins World Cup Trading Championship (who tripled his account by October with an exceptional win rate). Unlike aggressive gamblers, Patrick prioritizes institutional liquidity and strict risk control. His methodology relies on two core tools: Weekly Volume Profile (成交量分布圖) to identify where institutional capital accumulates (the 70% Value Area), and 15-minute candlestick charts using his mentor Tom's PBD (Impulse-Consolidation Box) method. By waiting for price to travel from afar into fresh weekly Value Area boundaries or historical consolidation zones, drawing entity-based consolidation boxes ('P' for upward impulse, 'b' for downward impulse), and executing counter-impulse breakouts, false breakouts, or range bounces, he achieves asymmetric Risk-to-Reward Ratios (minimum 1.5R to 2R+) while capping single-trade risk at 1%. Ultimately, sustainable profitability stems from combining macro institutional auction zones with micro price-action confirmation and disciplined position scaling.

4. Mental Models & Underlying Logic
四、底層邏輯與思維模型

1. Institutional Auction Market Theory (機構拍賣市場理論 — Why & How)
Markets spend 70% of the time consolidating inside value ranges where big money executes large orders. Once liquidity in a range is exhausted, institutions push price aggressively to a new level to find fresh liquidity. Instead of chasing mid-range noise, Patrick waits at the edges of institutional liquidity pools.
市場有 70% 的時間在區間內震盪，這是大資金進行大額成交的場所；當區間內的單子不夠成交時，大資金會將價格推向下一站尋找流動性。Patrick 不在區間中央追價，而是守株待兔於大資金密集區的邊緣。

2. Multi-Timeframe Confluence: Macro Map + Micro Trigger (多週期共振：宏觀地圖 + 微觀扳機)
Weekly Volume Profile defines 'Where' to trade (high-probability structural zones), while the 15-minute PBD box defines 'When' to pull the trigger (precise order-flow shift and tight stop-loss placement).
週線成交量分布圖解決「在哪裡交易」的空間定位問題，而 15 分鐘 PBD 方框則解決「何時扣扳機」的進場時機與極小止損定位問題。

3. Asymmetric Payoff & Probabilistic Survival (不對稱盈虧與機率生存思維)
Even with a 50% baseline win rate, consecutive losing streaks are mathematically inevitable. By capping risk at 1% per trade and enforcing a minimum 1.5R–2R reward-to-risk ratio, a single winning trade covers multiple losses, ensuring survival and compounding.
即使勝率有五成，連續虧損在統計上也遲早會發生。透過單筆嚴控 1% 風險與強制執行 1.5 至 2 倍以上的盈虧比，一筆大賺即可覆蓋多次小賠，確保帳戶在長期機率中穩健複利。

5. Detailed Breakdown by Core Modules (with Timestamps)
五、詳細重點拆解（保留原始時間戳記）

Module 1: Macro Zone Identification via Weekly Volume Profile [00:00:55 - 00:01:56]
模組一：利用週線成交量分布圖標記宏觀關鍵區域 [00:00:55 - 00:01:56]

【Concept Explanation / 概念解釋】

Using Weekly Volume Profile to locate the 70% Value Area (VAH/VAL) and historical horizontal shelves where institutional orders cluster.
透過週線級別的成交量分布圖，找出佔一週 70% 成交量的價值區上下沿（VAH/VAL）及左側歷史橫盤老位置。

【Key Steps / 關鍵步驟與操作流程】

1. Open the Weekly chart on TradingView and add Visible Range Volume Profile (or Fixed Range Volume Profile for free users).
1. 打開 TradingView 週線圖，載入「可見範圍成交量分布圖」（免費版可用左側工具欄第五項的「固定範圍成交量分布圖」手動框選）。
2. Mark the Value Area High (VAH) and Value Area Low (VAL) as price bands, plus historical consolidation levels on the left.
2. 標出上週價值區上沿（VAH）與下沿（VAL）畫成價格帶，並標記左側歷史橫盤老位置。
3. Apply the 'Freshness Filter': Only trade zones when price travels from afar; ignore zones recently tested within the past few days (e.g., Crude Oil at $77 on Aug 9 was skipped in favor of $85.53).
3. 執行「新鮮度過濾」：區域必須等價格「從遠處打過來」才有效；前幾天剛到過、還沒走遠的位置直接放棄（例如 8 月 9 日美油在 77 美元剛測過上沿便放棄，改等上方 85.53 美元老位置）。

【Formulas & Terms / 重要公式與專有名詞】

Visible Range Volume Profile (VRVP 可見範圍成交量分布圖), Fixed Range Volume Profile (FRVP 固定範圍成交量分布圖), Value Area (價值區 70% 成交量區間).
可見範圍成交量分布圖（VRVP）、固定範圍成交量分布圖（FRVP）、價值區（Value Area，涵蓋 70% 成交量）。

【Common Pitfalls / 常見誤區與注意事項】

Trading stale zones that price has been hovering around for days, which leads to choppy false signals.
切忌在價格已經黏著震盪好幾天的「非新鮮區域」反覆掛單，這會導致頻繁被掃損。

Module 2: 15-Minute PBD Box Construction Rules [00:01:57 - 00:02:29]
模組二：15 分鐘圖 PBD 方框繪製標準 [00:01:57 - 00:02:29]

【Concept Explanation / 概念解釋】

Translating price movement into a two-phase visual structure: a sharp single-leg Impulse followed by a horizontal Consolidation Box.
在 15 分鐘週期上，將價格走勢拆解為「一口氣走出的推動段（Impulse）」與隨後的「橫盤整理方框（Box）」。

【Key Steps / 關鍵步驟與操作流程】

1. Switch to the 15-minute chart as price approaches a macro zone.
1. 當價格接近宏觀區域時，切換至 15 分鐘 K 線圖。
2. Identify an uninterrupted impulse move followed by sideways consolidation lasting roughly half a day to one full day.
2. 確認價格是「一口氣」推動過來的，並在推動結束後橫盤約半天至一天。
3. Draw the box strictly using Candle Bodies (Open to Close), ignoring occasional wicks.
3. 畫方框時嚴格以「K 線實體（開盤價到收盤價）」為準，偶爾衝出去的上下影線不計入框內。
4. Classify the shape: Upward impulse + box = 'P'; Downward impulse + box = 'b'. Confirm reliability when price touches box edges 3 times.
4. 判斷型態：往上推再橫住為大寫「P」，往下推再橫住為小寫「b」；若價格在框內來回碰邊達三次，方框可靠性大幅提升。

【Formulas & Terms / 重要公式與專有名詞】

PBD Method (推動盤整戰法), Candle Body (K線實體), Wick/Shadow (影線), 'P' & 'b' Formations (P型與b型結構).
PBD 戰法、K 線實體（開收盤區間）、影線（不計入方框）、P 型態（上推盤整）與 b 型態（下推盤整）。

【Common Pitfalls / 常見誤區與注意事項】

Including extreme wicks when drawing the box, which artificially widens the range and delays breakout confirmation.
畫框時把偶發的長影線包進去，會導致方框過寬，使後續突破訊號嚴重滯後。

Module 3: Three Execution Scenarios & Entry Triggers [00:02:30 - 00:03:34]
模組三：三種進場情境與觸發機制 [00:02:30 - 00:03:34]

【Concept Explanation / 概念解釋】

Adapting entry tactics based on whether price breaks out of the box, stays trapped inside a wide box, or spikes straight into a macro zone.
根據價格「突破方框」、「困在寬幅方框內」或「直接打到宏觀區域」，採取三種對應的精準進場策略。

【Key Steps / 關鍵步驟與操作流程】

1. Scenario 1 (Box Breakout): Wait for a 15m candle body to close outside the box. Counter-impulse breakouts are preferred ('P' breaks down = Short; 'b' breaks up = Long). Same-direction breakouts require a massive initial impulse. Confirm with a higher close (long) or retest/re-break (short), or place limit orders on box-edge retests. If price breaks out and quickly closes back inside (False Breakout, especially downward sweep & reclaim), trade the reversal.
1. 情境一（出了方框）：必須等 K 線「收盤」收在方框外面才算有效。最喜歡「反推動方向」突破（P 跌破下沿做空；b 突破上沿做多）；若順推動方向突破，則要求前置推動幅度必須遠大於方框。進場常等確認（向上需下一根收更高；向下等反彈再跌破前低，或提前在方框邊緣掛限價單等回測）。若突破後未延續反而收回框內（假突破），則反向操作（跌破下沿又收回做多是最佳機會；上衝收回則不做）。
2. Scenario 2 (Range Bounce): In wide boxes with 3+ edge touches, place limit buy orders at the bottom and limit sell orders at the top.
2. 情境二（一直不出方框）：針對極寬且能來回走好幾趟的方框，等價格來回碰邊三次後，在下沿掛多單、上沿掛空單來回操作。
3. Scenario 3 (Direct Macro Limit): When price approaches a fresh weekly Value Area edge from afar without forming a box, place a direct limit order (Short at VAH, Long at VAL).
3. 情境三（不等方框直接掛單）：價格從遠處直接打到週線價值區邊界時，直接在區域上掛限價單（漲上來掛空、跌下來掛多）。

【Formulas & Terms / 重要公式與專有名詞】

Counter-Impulse Breakout (反推動突破), Retest Limit Order (回測限價單), False Breakout Reclaim (假突破收回).
反推動突破（最優訊號）、回測方框邊緣掛單、假突破收回（僅做下破收回做多）。

【Common Pitfalls / 常見誤區與注意事項】

Chasing a candle that moves too fast without a confirmation pullback, or trading upward false breakouts (which Patrick explicitly avoids).
行情走太快沒等到回測確認就盲目追單，或是去做「向上衝出又收回」的假突破空單（Patrick 明確不做此類）。

Module 4: Stop-Loss Placement & Target Projection [00:03:35 - 00:04:43]
模組四：止損定位與止盈目標測算 [00:03:35 - 00:04:43]

【Concept Explanation / 概念解釋】

Defining objective invalidation points outside structural boundaries and projecting profit targets using origin points or measured moves.
在結構邊界外側設定客觀止損點，並依據推動起點、對側價值區或等距測幅設定止盈目標。

【Key Steps / 關鍵步驟與操作流程】

1. Breakout Trades: Place stop-loss outside the opposite side of the box. For counter-impulse trades, target the origin of the impulse (ideal if aligned with the opposite Value Area edge). For same-direction trades at All-Time Highs with no left reference, duplicate the impulse length upward (Measured Move).
1. 突破方框單：止損放方框另一側外面（做空放上沿上、做多放下沿下）。反推動突破的目標看「這段推動開始的地方」（若正好也是價值區另一邊最理想）；同方向突破且前方無歷史參考（如歷史新高）時，將前面推動段原樣複製一份量出等距目標。
2. False Breakout Trades: Place a tight stop-loss just beyond the false breakout wick/extreme; hold beyond the opposite box edge.
2. 假突破單：止損放在衝出去那一下的外面（極小止損），目標不必停在方框對邊，可繼續持有。
3. Range Bounce Trades: Stop-loss outside recent swing high/low; take 100% profit at the opposite box edge.
3. 框內來回單：止損放最近高低點外面，目標為方框對邊，到達即全平。
4. Direct Zone Trades: Stop-loss outside the zone; move stop to breakeven once price bounces out. If no chart reference exists, reverse-engineer stop-loss from target (e.g., 800-point target = max 200-point stop) to maintain >= 1.5R–2R.
4. 直接區域掛單：止損放區域外面，價格彈出區域後立即將止損推至進場價保本。若前方無圖表參照，則從目標倒推止損（如目標賺 800 點，止損最多放 200 點），確保所有單子盈虧比至少 1.5 倍、最好 2 倍以上。

【Formulas & Terms / 重要公式與專有名詞】

Measured Move (等距推動複製測幅), Breakeven Stop (保本止損), Reverse-Engineered Stop-Loss (目標倒推止損法).
等距推動測幅、保本止損（推至進場價）、目標倒推止損法。

【Common Pitfalls / 常見誤區與注意事項】

Taking a trade where structural stop-loss distance yields a Risk-to-Reward ratio below 1.5R.
在止損空間過大、導致潛在盈虧比低於 1.5 倍的情況下勉強進場。

Module 5: Position Sizing, 3-Part Scaling & Trailing Rules [00:04:44 - 00:05:06]
模組五：倉位控制、三等份分批止盈與移動止損 [00:04:44 - 00:05:06]

【Concept Explanation / 概念解釋】

Mathematical position sizing capped at 1% account risk, paired with a 3-tranche exit system to lock in gains while capturing trend extensions.
透過單筆 1% 風險上限反推下單手數，並將倉位拆為三等份分批止盈，兼顧落袋為安與波段延伸。

【Key Steps / 關鍵步驟與操作流程】

1. Calculate Lot Size: Determine stop-loss distance before entry -> Calculate max dollar loss (1% of account) -> Divide by loss per lot -> Round to a multiple of 3.
1. 計算手數：下單前先定好止損距離 -> 算出帳戶 1% 最多能虧多少錢 -> 除以一手打到止損虧多少錢得出手數 -> 湊成「3 的倍數」方便分批。
2. Scale Out in 3 Parts (except range trades): Part 1 at the nearest box/zone edge; Part 2 managed dynamically based on price action; Part 3 at the final target.
2. 分三份止盈（框內來回單除外）：第一份在離進場最近的方框或區域邊緣平倉；第二份視盤面走勢靈活處理；第三份留到最終目標。
3. Trailing Stop Discipline: Never widen a stop-loss. Only move it in the profitable direction, specifically trailing behind newly formed consolidation boxes along the trend.
3. 移動止損鐵律：止損只能往賺錢的方向挪動，絕對不能放寬；當價格走出去又橫出新方框時，將止損推至新方框外側保護利潤。

【Formulas & Terms / 重要公式與專有名詞】

1% Risk Rule (單筆 1% 風控法則), 3-Part Scaling (三等份分批止盈), Box-to-Box Trailing Stop (階梯方框移動止損).
單筆 1% 風險上限、三等份分批止盈、階梯方框移動止損。

【Common Pitfalls / 常見誤區與注意事項】

Widening stop-loss when price approaches invalidation, or trading full size without splitting exits.
價格逼近止損時心存僥倖放寬止損，或全倉進出導致心態失衡。

6. Practical Action Items
六、實操行動清單

1. Configure Weekly Volume Profile Workspace (建置週線成交量分布圖看盤環境)
Add Visible Range Volume Profile (or Fixed Range Volume Profile) to your Weekly charts on TradingView and mark the 70% Value Area High (VAH), Value Area Low (VAL), and historical consolidation shelves.
在 TradingView 週線圖載入「可見範圍成交量分布圖」（或固定範圍成交量分布圖），標記出 70% 價值區上下沿（VAH/VAL）及左側歷史橫盤老位置。

2. Apply the 'Freshness Filter' Before Every Session (盤前執行「區域新鮮度」過濾)
Eliminate any support/resistance level that price has already tested within the last few days; set price alerts only at untouched zones located far from current price.
剔除近幾天價格剛碰觸過且尚未走遠的區域，僅在距離現價較遠、尚未被測試的「新鮮區域」設定價格警報。

3. Practice Drawing Strict Body-Only PBD Boxes on 15m Charts (在 15 分鐘圖練習繪製「純實體」PBD 方框)
Backtest 20 historical setups by identifying single-leg impulses followed by half-day consolidations, drawing boxes strictly from candle open-to-close bodies while ignoring wicks.
回測 20 組歷史走勢，練習辨識一口氣推動後的半日橫盤區間，嚴格只用 K 線實體（開收盤價）畫框並忽略上下影線。

4. Build a Pre-Trade Position Size & 3-Tranche Calculator (建立盤前倉位與三等份分批計算表)
Calculate exact lot sizes before entry using (Account Equity × 1%) ÷ Stop-Loss Risk per Lot, rounding down to a multiple of 3 for systematic 3-part scaling.
每次下單前使用公式「（帳戶總資金 × 1%）÷ 單手止損金額」精算手數，並將手數湊成 3 的倍數以執行標準三等份分批止盈。

5. Enforce a Minimum 1.5R Filter & Box-Trailing Journal (執行 1.5 倍盈虧比過濾與階梯移動止損日誌)
Reject any setup with a Risk-to-Reward ratio below 1.5R, and log every trade's stop-loss adjustments to ensure stops are only trailed behind newly formed consolidation boxes.
堅決過濾盈虧比低於 1.5 倍的交易機會，並在交易日誌中檢核止損是否僅往獲利方向推移至新橫盤方框外側。
