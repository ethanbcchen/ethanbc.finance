1. Executive Summary
1. 核心總結

Daily Bias is not a fortune-telling prediction of absolute market certainty; rather, it is a structured probabilistic plan combined with a clear invalidation point that places a trader on the side of higher odds.
日線偏向（Daily Bias）並非像算命師般預測市場的絕對走勢，而是一套結合明確「失效點（Invalidation）」的機率性交易計畫，旨在將交易者置於勝率較高的一方。

To eliminate emotional guesswork and avoid getting chopped up in mid-range consolidation, speaker Red Go presents a repeatable five-step mechanical checklist executable within five minutes: (1) map the Higher-Time-Frame (HTF) 4-hour swing range and locate price within Premium or Discount zones using Fibonacci retracement; (2) identify obvious Liquidity Pools such as Prior Day High/Low (PDH/PDL) and Equal Highs/Lows (EQH/EQL) that act as price magnets; (3) build the market narrative by distinguishing between Pro-HTF continuation and Counter-HTF pullback phases while aligning with Internal Order Flow; (4) select a single logical Take Profit target at an opposing zone or imbalance; and (5) define a strict candle-body invalidation level.
為消除情緒化猜測並避免在區間中段反覆遭洗盤，講者 Red Go 提出一套可在五分鐘內執行的可重複五步驟機械化檢查清單：（1）標註高時間框架（HTF）4 小時波段區間，並利用斐波那契工具判斷價格處於溢價區（Premium）或折價區（Discount）；（2）標記前一日高低點（PDH/PDL）與等高/等低點（EQH/EQL）等流動性池作為價格磁鐵；（3）解讀市場敘事，區分順大時框（Pro-HTF）延續階段與逆大時框（Counter-HTF）回調階段，並順應內部訂單流（Internal Order Flow）；（4）在反向供需區或價格不平衡區設定單一止盈目標；（5）定義嚴格的實體 K 線突破失效條件。

Ultimately, sustainable profitability stems from disciplined execution, avoiding mid-range noise, and building conviction through 30 days of consistent data tracking and trade journaling.
歸根究柢，長期穩定獲利源於嚴格紀律、避開中段雜訊，並透過連續 30 天的一致性數據追蹤與交易日誌複盤來建立對策略的信念。

2. Key Concepts & Definitions (With Timestamps)
2. 知識地圖與核心概念（附時間戳記）

[00:01:32 - 00:03:56] Daily Bias (日線偏向)
A directional trade plan backed by probability and an explicit invalidation point ('Plan + Invalidation'), determining where price is most likely to gravitate next rather than a 100% prediction.
建立在機率與明確失效點之上的方向性交易計畫（計畫＋失效點），用於判斷價格下一步最可能趨向何方，而非追求 100% 絕對預測。

[00:03:31 - 00:03:56] Invalidation (失效點 / 失效條件)
A predefined price level (requiring a full candle body close beyond the key level, not merely a wick) that objectively proves a trade idea wrong and forces immediate re-evaluation.
預先設定的關鍵價位（需實體 K 線收盤突破並站穩，而非僅僅插針），一旦觸及即客觀證明交易構想錯誤，強制交易者立刻重新研判市場。

[00:05:35 - 00:07:09] Higher-Time-Frame (HTF) Swing Range (高時間框架波段區間)
The active trading boundary on the 4-hour chart defined by the most recent Swing Low (Strong Low) and Swing High (Weak High) following a Break of Structure (BOS).
在 4 小時圖表上，繼結構突破（BOS）之後由最近一個波段低點（強低點 Strong Low）與波段高點（弱高點 Weak High）所界定的當前有效交易邊界。

[00:07:09 - 00:10:22] Premium & Discount Zones / Equilibrium (溢價區、折價區與均衡位)
Dividing the HTF swing range at the 50% Fibonacci level (Equilibrium). Above 50% is Premium (expensive, look for shorts); below 50% is Discount (cheap, look for longs).
以斐波那契 50% 中軸（均衡位 Equilibrium）劃分大時框區間：50% 以上為溢價區（價格昂貴，尋找做空機會）；50% 以下為折價區（價格便宜，尋找做多機會）。

[00:10:22 - 00:12:53] Liquidity Pools & PDH / PDL (流動性池與前一日高低點)
Clusters of stop-loss and breakout orders resting above Prior Day High (PDH) and below Prior Day Low (PDL) that act as magnetic fuel for institutional price delivery.
聚集在前一日高點（PDH）上方與前一日低點（PDL）下方的止損單與突破掛單群，如同磁鐵般吸引價格前往掠奪以作為推動行情的燃料。

[00:12:53 - 00:14:16] Equal Highs (EQH) & Equal Lows (EQL) (等高點與等低點)
Two or more aligned swing highs or lows that retail traders mistake for support/resistance, but which actually represent concentrated stop-loss pools targeted for a Liquidity Sweep.
兩個或多個水平對齊的高點或低點；散戶常誤以為是支撐或阻力，實則為高度集中的止損流動性池，常成為主力進行流動性掃蕩（Liquidity Sweep）的目標。

[00:14:24 - 00:15:22] Liquidity Sweep (流動性掠奪 / 掃蕩)
Price piercing beyond a key high/low (e.g., PDH/PDL or EQH/EQL) to trigger resting stop orders; note that a sweep indicates a target magnet, not an automatic reversal without confirmation.
價格刺穿關鍵高低點（如前日高低點或等高低點）以觸發掛單流動性的動作；須注意流動性掠奪僅代表目標達成，若無結構確認未必立即反轉。

[00:16:09 - 00:19:50] Market Narrative: Pro-HTF vs. Counter-HTF (市場敘事：順大時框 vs. 逆大時框)
Identifying whether price is in a Continuation phase (Pro-HTF, lower and higher timeframes aligned) or a Pullback phase (Counter-HTF, short-term internal flow moving against HTF).
判斷市場處於趨勢延續階段（順大時框 Pro-HTF，大小時框方向一致）或回調階段（逆大時框 Counter-HTF，小時框短期逆著大時框運行以完成回調）。

[00:19:27 - 00:20:31] Internal Order Flow (內部訂單流)
The active directional momentum on the lower timeframe within the HTF swing range; traders should align with internal order flow rather than trying to catch a falling knife.
大時框波段區間內部的小時間框架當前動能方向；交易者應順應當前內部訂單流操作，避免在回調尚未結束時盲目逆勢接飛刀。

[00:21:06 - 00:22:41] Market Structure Shift (MSS) / Break of Structure (BOS) (市場結構轉變 / 結構突破)
Price taking out the last Lower High (for bullish shift) or last Higher Low (for bearish shift) after a liquidity sweep, confirming lower-timeframe alignment.
價格在完成流動性掠奪後，突破上一個降低的高點（轉多確認）或跌破上一個抬高的低點（轉空確認），標誌著小時框內部結構正式轉向。

[00:23:08 - 00:25:39] Opposing Zone & Imbalance / FVG (反向供需區與價格不平衡區)
Logical single Take Profit targets: either an opposing Supply/Demand zone where counter-participants enter, or an unfilled Imbalance (Fair Value Gap) caused by price inefficiency.
合理的單一止盈目標位置：包含預期會有對手盤介入的反向供給區/需求區，或是因價格快速單邊波動留下的未回補價格不平衡區（Imbalance / FVG）。

[00:38:52 - 00:39:52] Consistency-to-Conviction Loop (一致性到信念的循環鏈)
The psychological framework: Consistency breeds Competence, Competence breeds Confidence, and Confidence breeds Conviction in one's trading edge.
交易心理核心公式：執行的一致性（Consistency）孕育能力（Competence），能力孕育信心（Confidence），信心最終孕育對策略優勢的堅定信念（Conviction）。

4. Mental Models & Logic (Why & How)
4. 底層邏輯與思維模型

1. Probabilistic Expectation vs. Deterministic Prediction
1. Probabilistic Expectation vs. Deterministic Prediction (機率期望思維 vs. 決定論預測)

Why: Markets are inherently uncertain; seeking 100% certainty leads to paralysis or over-leveraging. How: Treat Daily Bias as a statistical filter ('Where is price most likely going next?') rather than a guarantee, pairing every hypothesis with a predefined exit if proven wrong.
底層邏輯（Why）：市場本質充滿不確定性，追求 100% 確定性只會導致猶豫或重倉賭博。運作方式（How）：將日線偏向視為統計濾網（「價格下一步最可能去哪？」），為每個假設配上明確的認錯機制。

2. Location-First Trading / Wholesale vs. Retail Pricing
2. Location-First Trading / Wholesale vs. Retail Pricing (位置優先思維 / 批發價與零售價模型)

Why: Even if your directional idea is right, entering at a poor location guarantees getting stopped out by normal retracements. How: Like shopping at a supermarket, buy only on sale (Discount zone <50%) and sell only when overpriced (Premium zone >50%), refusing to transact in the indecisive middle.
底層邏輯（Why）：即便看對大方向，在糟糕的位置進場仍會被正常回調掃出局。運作方式（How）：如同超市購物，只在打折時買入（折價區 <50%）、只在昂貴時賣出（溢價區 >50%），拒絕在猶豫不決的中間地帶交易。

3. Liquidity-Driven Market Delivery
3. Liquidity-Driven Market Delivery (流動性驅動傳遞模型)

Why: Institutional algorithms cannot execute massive orders without counterparty liquidity; price does not move randomly, it seeks resting stop orders. How: Map PDH/PDL and EQH/EQL as fuel tanks that price must sweep before initiating a sustained expansion.
底層邏輯（Why）：機構演算法需要足夠的對手盤流動性才能成交大額訂單，價格並非隨機波動，而是專門獵殺止損單。運作方式（How）：將前日高低點與等高低點視為「燃料庫」，預判價格先掃蕩流動性再展開主升/主跌段。

4. Second-Order Consequence Thinking
4. Second-Order Consequence Thinking (二階後果推演思維)

Why: Amateur traders react emotionally to the current candle; great traders anticipate multi-step scenarios before risking capital. How: Explicitly finish the sentence 'My bias is wrong if X happens' before entry, eliminating revenge trading and doubling down when invalidation hits.
底層邏輯（Why）：業餘者對眼前單根 K 線情緒化反應；頂尖交易者在冒險前已推演未來五步的連帶後果。運作方式（How）：進場前強制完成「若發生 X 則我的偏向錯誤」的句型，觸發失效點時冷靜重置，杜絕報復性交易。

5. The 4C Competency Flywheel
5. The 4C Competency Flywheel (4C 能力飛輪模型)

Why: Psychological doubt comes from a lack of empirical evidence, not a lack of positive affirmations. How: Execute the exact same checklist for 30 days (Consistency) -> master pattern recognition (Competence) -> trust execution (Confidence) -> achieve unshakeable self-belief (Conviction).
底層邏輯（Why）：交易中的懷疑源於缺乏實證數據，而非自我喊話不夠多。運作方式（How）：連續 30 天執行同一套清單（一致性）→ 熟練掌握盤面規律（能力）→ 信任自身執行（信心）→ 建立不可動搖的交易信念（信念）。

Module 1: Redefining Daily Bias & The Invalidation Principle [00:01:32 - 00:04:06]
模組一：重新定義日線偏向與「失效點」核心原則 [00:01:32 - 00:04:06]

[Concept] Daily Bias is an actionable directional hypothesis ('Plan + Invalidation') that filters high-probability setups, rather than a fortune-telling prediction. A bias without an invalidation point is merely 'hope,' which outsources trading responsibility to luck.
【概念解釋】日線偏向是具備高勝率過濾功能的方向性假設（「交易計畫＋失效點」），而非算命式預測。沒有失效點的偏向只是盲目的「希望」，等於把交易責任外包給運氣。

[Workflow] 1. Summarize today's directional bias in one clear sentence before looking for entries. 2. Explicitly state the exact price action required to prove the bias wrong. 3. Commit to staying out of the market when no clear bias or invalidation exists.
【關鍵步驟/操作流程】1. 尋找進場點前，先用一句話精準概括今日偏向。2. 明確寫下證明自己判斷錯誤所需的具體價格行為。3. 若無法建立清晰偏向或找不到合理失效點，嚴格執行空倉觀望。

[Terms & Formula] Daily Bias (日線偏向) | Invalidation (失效點) | Confluence (匯合點 / 共振信號)
【重要公式/專有名詞】核心公式：Daily Bias = Plan + Invalidation（日線偏向 ＝ 計畫 ＋ 失效點）；專有名詞：Confluence（多條件匯合確認）。

[Pitfalls] Mistaking personal perception for objective market reality; trading without a hard stop/invalidation and praying for price to turn around.
【常見誤區/注意事項】誤把個人主觀看法當成市場客觀事實；不設失效點，虧損時靠祈禱價格回頭來自我安慰。

Module 2: Step 1 — Mapping HTF Range & Premium/Discount Location [00:05:35 - 00:10:22]
模組二：步驟一 — 標註大時框區間與溢折價位置 [00:05:35 - 00:10:22]

[Concept] Before analyzing liquidity or entries, you must know where price sits inside the Higher-Time-Frame (4H) swing range. Trading location using the 50% Equilibrium level prevents buying expensive highs or selling cheap lows.
【概念解釋】在分析流動性或進場點前，必須先確認價格處於高時間框架（4 小時圖）波段區間的哪個位置。利用 50% 均衡位判斷位置，能避免追高買貴或殺低賣便宜。

[Workflow] 1. Open the 4-hour chart and identify the latest Break of Structure (BOS). 2. Mark the Swing Low (Strong Low) and Swing High (Weak High) bounding the current move. 3. Drag the Fibonacci Retracement tool from Swing Low to Swing High to locate the 50% Equilibrium line. 4. Look for shorts in Premium (>50%), longs in Discount (<50%), and stay out or reduce risk in the middle.
【關鍵步驟/操作流程】1. 打開 4 小時圖，識別最近一次結構突破（BOS）。2. 標記界定當前波段的波段低點（強低點）與波段高點（弱高點）。3. 從波段低點拉斐波那契回撤至波段高點，標出 50% 均衡線。4. 價格在溢價區（>50%）找做空機會，在折價區（<50%）找做多機會，處於中間地帶則觀望或大幅減倉。

[Terms & Formula] Higher-Time-Frame / HTF (高時間框架) | Break of Structure / BOS (結構突破) | Equilibrium (50% 均衡位) | Premium Zone (溢價區) | Discount Zone (折價區)
【重要公式/專有名詞】HTF（高時間框架，本片採用 4H）、BOS（結構突破）、Equilibrium（50% 中軸均衡位）、Premium（溢價區）、Discount（折價區）。

[Pitfalls] Trading in the middle of the range ('mid-range chop') where price is indecisive and retail traders repeatedly get stopped out by fakeouts.
【常見誤區/注意事項】在區間正中央（半空中）強行交易；該區域方向極不明確，散戶極易在此遭反覆假突破洗盤。

Module 3: Step 2 — Identifying Liquidity Pools as Price Magnets [00:10:22 - 00:16:09]
模組三：步驟二 — 標註流動性池（價格運行磁鐵） [00:10:22 - 00:16:09]

[Concept] Smart money requires resting stop orders (liquidity) to fuel directional moves. Prior Day High/Low and Equal Highs/Lows act as magnetic targets where price gravitates first.
【概念解釋】主力資金需要大量掛單與止損單（流動性）作為燃料來推動行情。前一日高低點與等高/等低點如同磁鐵，是價格第一優先被吸引前往的目標。

[Workflow] 1. Switch to the Daily timeframe and draw horizontal lines at the Prior Day High (PDH) and Prior Day Low (PDL). 2. Drop back to the 4H chart and mark any clean Equal Highs (EQH) or Equal Lows (EQL). 3. Evaluate which side holds the most obvious un-swept liquidity magnet to determine immediate directional pull.
